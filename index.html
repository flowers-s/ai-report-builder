Boris（Anthropic）分享了一个关于如何让 Claude 真正在企业级场景中产生回报的三部曲。核心洞见：真正的红利不在于辅助编码，而在于修复和维护工作能够*在后台自动发生*，从而解放团队去构建以前根本触及不到的东西。他建议不只是追踪使用量，更要衡量——如果没有 AI，工程投入*是否本会被花掉*。落地层面，这意味着为 Claude 提供端到端的验证路径、开启自动权限模式，以及使用能支持自主决策的界面，而非要求人工频繁审批。
Boris (Anthropic) shared a three-part thread on how to make Claude actually deliver ROI at enterprise scale. His key insight: the real payoff isn't in assisted coding — it's when fixing and maintaining code happens *in the background*, freeing teams to build things that were previously out of reach. He advocates measuring not just usage but whether engineering effort *would have been spent* without AI. Practically, that means giving Claude end-to-end verification pathways, enabling auto-mode permissions, and using interfaces that allow autonomous decision-making rather than constant human approval.
