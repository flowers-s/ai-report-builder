### Claude Blog — New in Claude Managed Agents: dreaming, outcomes, and multiagent orchestration
https://claude.com/blog/new-in-claude-managed-agents

Anthropic has launched three major upgrades to Claude Managed Agents: dreaming, outcomes, and multiagent orchestration.
Anthropic 为 Claude Managed Agents 推出了三项重大升级：dreaming（学习沉淀）、outcomes（目标导向）和多智能体编排。

**Dreaming** is a scheduled process that reviews past agent sessions to find patterns, extract learnings, and self-improve memory over time.
**Dreaming** 是一种定期回顾历史 agent 会话的机制，能够发现模式、提炼知识，让 agent 随时间不断自我进化。

Harvey used dreaming to boost agent task completion rates by ~6x in their tests.
Harvey 利用 dreaming 将 agent 任务完成率提升了约 6 倍。

**Outcomes** lets developers write a rubric describing what "success" looks like; a separate grader evaluates output and prompts the agent to self-correct until the bar is met.
**Outcomes** 允许开发者编写描述"成功"标准的评估规则；独立的评分器会检验输出结果，并驱动 agent 持续自我修正，直到达标。

In testing, outcomes improved task success by up to 10 percentage points, with the largest gains on the hardest problems.
测试中，outcomes 将任务成功率最多提升了 10 个百分点，且在最难的问题上提升最为显著。

File generation quality also improved: +8.4% on docx and +10.1% on pptx.
文件生成质量同步提升：docx 提升 8.4%，pptx 提升 10.1%。

**Multiagent orchestration** lets a lead agent break complex jobs into pieces and delegate each to specialist subagents running in parallel on a shared filesystem.
**多智能体编排**让主 agent 能够将复杂任务拆分，并行分派给具有独立 model、prompt 和工具的专属子 agent，共享文件系统协同作业。

Netflix's platform team built a log-analysis agent that processes hundreds of builds in parallel and surfaces only the patterns worth acting on.
Netflix 平台团队基于此构建了一个日志分析 agent，能并行处理数百次构建记录，只呈现值得关注的模式。

Spiral by Every uses multiagent orchestration with a Haiku lead agent delegating drafts to Opus subagents, enforcing writing quality via outcomes.
Every 旗下的 Spiral 让 Haiku 主 agent 协调 Opus 子 agent 并行起草内容，并通过 outcomes 强制保证写作质量。

### Training Data — Suno's Mikey Shulman: Everyone Can Make Music Now
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Jq3BIGz4vXQ

Published: 2026-05-13

Mikey Shulman, founder and CEO of Suno, argues that AI music's biggest unlock is turning everyone from a passive consumer into an active creator.
Suno 创始人兼 CEO Mikey Shulman 认为，AI 音乐最大的突破在于：它将每一个人从被动的听众变成了主动的创作者。

Before Suno, only a tiny fraction of the world's 8 billion people made music; on Suno, 90% of users create something on any given day.
在 Suno 之前，80 亿人中只有极少数人创作音乐；而在 Suno 上，任意一天都有 90% 的用户在创作。

The creation itself is the entertainment — not the downstream use of the content.
创作本身就是娱乐，而不是为了把内容拿去别处使用。

Suno models treat all sound as raw float-32 waveforms sampled at 48,000 Hz — no hardcoded musical knowledge like "12 tones" or "200 instruments" — which is what allows entirely new genre hybrids to emerge.
Suno 的模型将所有声音视为以 48,000 Hz 采样的原始 float-32 波形，不内置"12 个音调"或"200 种乐器"等音乐先验知识——这正是全新跨界流派得以涌现的根源。

Music is not a scale problem: models are relatively small, and preference data — not compute — is the key research lever.
音乐不是一个靠规模解决的问题：模型体量相对较小，偏好数据而非算力才是核心研究杠杆。

Suno chose to build full songs with human vocals (via autoregression) over short, crisp clips (diffusion), a contrarian bet that paid off: a song tells a story in a way background audio never can.
Suno 押注用自回归方式生成完整歌曲与人声，而非用扩散模型生成短促、音质精良的片段——这个逆势选择被证明是正确的：一首完整的歌在讲故事这件事上，背景音乐永远无法与之媲美。

Suno recently settled with Warner Music and sees the partnership as a chance to build entirely new fan-artist interaction experiences that couldn't have existed before AI.
Suno 近期与华纳音乐达成和解，并将其视为共同打造全新粉丝-艺人互动体验的机会——这类体验在 AI 之前根本不可能存在。

Mikey predicts that within twelve months we will see a truly interactive concert where the audience creates music live with the artist.
Mikey 预测，在未来十二个月内，我们将看到真正意义上的互动演唱会——观众能与艺人实时共同创作音乐。
