**AI & I by Every** — "How Hugging Face's Investor Spots Winners"
Published: 2026-10-14
https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLuMcoKK9mKgHtW_o9h5sGO2vXrffKHwJL

本期播客以 Frontier AI 竞争格局为开场：Google 发布 Gemini Argon 4，Meta 发布 Wonder（有 Standard 和 Full 两个版本——是的，你可以"Wonder full"，这是 Meta 的幽默）。主持人 Dan Shipper 通过实测揭示，两款模型在公开 benchmark 上亮眼，但实际知识工作表现远比数字复杂：Wonder 在 Dan 的"高级工程师基准测试"中仅得 50%，Gemini 4 在写作上出人意料地夺魁，让 Dan 的 8000 字文章效率翻倍，但编程能力仅得 20%。
The podcast opens with the Frontier duopoly crumbling: Google's Gemini Argon 4 and Meta's Wonder (yes, "Wonder full" — their sense of humor) both top public benchmarks, but Every's hands-on testing tells a more nuanced story. Wonder scores just 50% on Dan's senior engineer benchmark and "takes the bait" on literary analysis tricks. Gemini 4, surprisingly, becomes Dan's favorite writing model — 2x productivity boost on an 8,000-word piece — but scores only 20% on coding tasks.

本期核心采访聚焦 Hugging Face 的投资人如何在早期识别 AI 赢家：关注技术社区影响力、开源生态势能，以及创始人能否真正理解模型能力的边界。
The main interview digs into how Hugging Face's investor identifies winners early — community leverage, open-source momentum, and whether founders truly understand model capability boundaries.
