**No Priors** — "From Restoring Sight to Reimagining the Brain, with Max Hodak"

This episode features Max Hodak, founder and CEO of Science (formerly of Neuralink), discussing Prima — a retinal prosthesis implant that just received regulatory approval in Europe, enabling blind patients to regain a form of vision.
本集邀请到 Science 公司创始人兼 CEO Max Hodak（前 Neuralink 成员），深入探讨了 Prima——一款刚刚在欧洲获得监管批准的视网膜假体植入物，能帮助失明患者重获某种形式的视觉。
*"The brain very literally, very clearly, plainly is a computer."* — Max 开篇便以这句话奠定基调，将神经科学与计算机科学的边界彻底打通。
The conversation goes deep on the roadmap: adding depth, grayscale, and eventually color to restored vision, as well as the broader vision of "substrate independence" — preserving human consciousness independent of its biological substrate.
对话还深入探讨了技术路线图：逐步为恢复的视觉添加景深、灰度乃至色彩，以及更宏大的"基底独立性"愿景——让人类意识独立于生物基底而存在。
Max also touches on alignable representations between AI models and the future of neuroscience as a field.
Max 还谈到了 AI 模型之间的可对齐表征，以及神经科学作为一个学科的未来走向。
