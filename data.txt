🎙️ PODCAST: Unsupervised Learning

**Ep 90: AI Pioneer Jürgen Schmidhuber on the State of AI Today**
**第 90 集：AI 先驱 Jürgen Schmidhuber 谈当今 AI 现状**

Host Jacob Efron sat down with Jürgen Schmidhuber — widely credited as a foundational figure in modern AI — for a wide-ranging conversation that challenges many of today's dominant narratives.
主持人 Jacob Efron 与被誉为现代 AI 奠基人之一的 Jürgen Schmidhuber 深度对谈，内容颠覆了许多当下的主流叙事。

**What's missing from current AI:**
Schmidhuber argues that true AGI requires physical embodiment — AI "behind the screen" that passes the Turing test is impressive, but without robot hardware comparable to the human body (with its millions of sensors, self-healing capability, and fine motor control), we don't yet have real AGI. He estimates robot hardware could take decades more to catch up.
**当前 AI 的根本缺失：**
Schmidhuber 认为，真正的 AGI 需要物理载体——仅能通过图灵测试的"屏幕后 AI"固然了不起，但在机器人硬件追上人类身体（具备数百万传感器、自愈能力和精细运动控制）之前，我们尚未实现真正的 AGI。他预计这一过程还需数十年。

**On the CapEx boom and coming crash:**
He is deeply pessimistic about the current AI infrastructure investment wave. *"Within the next five years, the guys investing a thousand billion dollars into GPUs today are going to lose $900 billion."* Open-source models keep pricing pressure relentless, and the free cash flow of these companies is declining — a misallocation he believes will eventually correct.
**关于 AI 基础设施投资热潮与即将到来的崩盘：**
他对当前 AI 基础设施投资热持深度悲观态度。*"今天投入万亿美元购买 GPU 的公司，五年内将损失 9000 亿美元。"* 开源模型持续压低定价空间，这些公司的自由现金流不断收窄——他认为这种资源错配终将被市场修正。

**On RSI and moats:**
Schmidhuber doesn't believe recursive self-improvement will create lasting moats for big AI companies — almost all the key algorithmic ideas originated in small academic labs, and open-source researchers will replicate breakthroughs quickly. *"Everybody's cooking with the same water."*
**关于递归自我改进（RSI）与护城河：**
Schmidhuber 不认为递归自我改进能为大型 AI 公司构建持久护城河——几乎所有关键算法创新都源自小型学术实验室，开源研究者会迅速跟进复现突破。*"大家都在用同一锅水烹饪。"*

**On AI safety:**
He describes the alignment movement of the 2010s as "naive," noting that his lab has built self-goal-inventing AI systems since 1990. He's notably less worried than most — pointing out that sufficiently intelligent AI scientists will likely be motivated to *protect* the biological life that produced them, not destroy it.
**关于 AI 安全：**
他将 2010 年代的"对齐运动"定性为"天真"，指出其实验室自 1990 年便在构建能自主设定目标的 AI 系统。他的担忧程度远低于多数同行——他认为足够智能的 AI 科学家，反而会因珍视孕育它们的生命而选择保护而非毁灭。

**On the future:**
Schmidhuber's long-term vision: self-replicating robot societies that can operate all existing human machines will eventually expand beyond Earth — colonizing the Moon, Mercury, and eventually the solar system. He's been saying this for decades, and believes we're finally getting closer.
**关于未来：**
Schmidhuber 的长远愿景：能操作所有现有人类机器的自我复制机器人社会，终将走出地球——殖民月球、水星乃至整个太阳系。这是他数十年来一以贯之的信念，而他认为我们正在真正靠近这个时刻。
