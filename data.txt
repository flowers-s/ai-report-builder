**AI & I by Every — "Notion's Founder on the One Thing Your AI Is Missing"**

Notion founder Ivan Zhao joined Dan Shipper to discuss how AI has fundamentally changed the product that Notion was built on.
Notion 创始人 Ivan Zhao 与 Dan Shipper 对谈，探讨 AI 如何从根本上改变了 Notion 所构建的产品愿景。

The original vision of Notion — bringing computing power to everyone without requiring coding — was actually inspired by the 1960s and 70s computing pioneers who believed software should be as universal as reading and writing.
Notion 的初始愿景——无需编码便将计算能力带给所有人——其实源于六七十年代那批相信软件应像读写一样普及的计算机先驱们。

*"Everybody can start making their own software now. In some sense, the original vision of the 60s, 70s, hippie generation of computing come to life."*
*"现在每个人都可以开始制作自己的软件了。某种意义上，六七十年代那代嬉皮士对计算机的原始愿景终于变成了现实。"*

With the rise of vibe coding and LLMs, the separation between "software makers" and "software users" that solidified in the 1980s is finally dissolving.
随着 Vibe Coding 和大语言模型的兴起，1980 年代固化的"软件制作者"与"软件使用者"之间的分野正在消融。

Ivan sees Notion's block-based primitives as a precursor to the programmable, composable software world that AI is now making possible for everyone.
Ivan 认为 Notion 的积木式原语，是 AI 正在为所有人打开的可编程、可组合软件世界的先行者。
