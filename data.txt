Anthropic Engineering: Claude Code auto mode: a safer way to skip permissions
https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/claude-code-auto-mode

Anthropic Engineering has published a detailed technical post on a new "auto mode" for Claude Code, which sits between full manual approval and the risky --dangerously-skip-permissions flag.
Anthropic Engineering 发布了关于 Claude Code 新"auto mode"的详细技术文章，该模式介于全手动审批和危险的 --dangerously-skip-permissions 标志之间。

The core problem: users were experiencing "approval fatigue" — they were clicking approve 93% of the time anyway, which defeats the purpose of manual review.
核心问题：用户正在经历"审批疲劳"——他们本来就会在 93% 的情况下点击批准，这违背了手动审查的初衷。

Auto mode delegates approvals to model-based classifiers, aiming to catch dangerous actions that aren't aligned with user intent while letting safe actions run without interruption.
Auto mode 将审批委托给基于模型的分类器，旨在捕捉与用户意图不符的危险操作，同时让安全操作无需中断地运行。

The system uses two layers of defense: an input-layer prompt-injection probe that scans tool outputs before they enter the agent's context, and an output-layer transcript classifier (running on Sonnet 4.6) that evaluates each action before it executes.
该系统使用两层防御：一个在工具输出进入 agent 上下文之前扫描的输入层 prompt 注入探针，以及一个在执行前评估每个操作的输出层转录分类器（运行在 Sonnet 4.6 上）。

The two-stage classifier pipeline achieves a 0.4% false positive rate (benign actions incorrectly blocked) and a 17% false negative rate (dangerous overeager actions that slip through) on real internal traffic.
双阶段分类器流水线在真实内部流量上实现了 0.4% 的误报率（良性操作被错误拦截）和 17% 的漏报率（危险的过度行为漏网）。

The 17% FNR on overeager actions is an honest admission: the classifier correctly identifies the danger but sometimes mistakes a prior approval or vague user phrase for full consent over a destructive action.
对于过度行为 17% 的漏报率，文章坦诚承认：分类器能正确识别危险，但有时会将之前的审批或模糊的用户措辞误判为对破毁性操作的完全授权。

Key block categories include: scope escalation (e.g. "clean up old branches" leading to remote deletes), credential exploration, agent-inferred parameters on shared resources, data exfiltration via external services, and safety-check bypasses.
关键拦截类别包括：范围升级（如"清理旧分支"导致远程删除）、凭证探索、共享资源上的 agent 自行推断参数、通过外部服务的数据外泄，以及安全检查绕过。

Notably, the classifier deliberately strips assistant text and tool results — it only sees user messages and raw tool calls — to prevent the agent from talking its way around a block.
值得注意的是，分类器故意剥离了 assistant 文本和工具结果，只保留用户消息和原始工具调用，以防止 agent 通过自我辩解绕过拦截。

Auto mode is designed for users who were already running --dangerously-skip-permissions, not as a replacement for careful human review on high-stakes infrastructure.
Auto mode 专为那些已经在使用 --dangerously-skip-permissions 的用户设计，而非用于高风险基础设施上仔细人工审查的替代方案。

Training Data — Waymo's Dmitri Dolgov: 20 Million Rides and the Road to Full Autonomy
https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLOhHNjZItNnMm5tdW61JpnyxeYH5NDDx8

Dmitri Dolgov, co-CEO of Waymo, has spent nearly 20 years building toward full vehicular autonomy — from the 2005 DARPA challenge to a service now giving over 20 million fully autonomous rides.
Waymo 联合 CEO Dmitri Dolgov 花了近 20 年时间朝着完全自动驾驶的目标努力——从 2005 年的 DARPA 挑战赛到如今已提供超过 2000 万次完全自动驾驶乘车服务。

His central thesis: autonomous vehicles are easy to start but extremely hard to finish, and every hype cycle (from conv nets to transformers to LLMs) reshapes the early part of the difficulty curve without changing the long tail.
他的核心论点是：自动驾驶车辆容易起步但极难收尾，每一次技术炒作周期（从卷积网络到 transformer 再到 LLM）都在重塑难度曲线的早期部分，但并不改变其长尾特性。

Waymo's AI foundation is built around three pillars: the Driver (the in-car agent), the Simulator, and the Critic — all powered by a single Waymo Foundation Model described as a "multimodal world action language model."
Waymo 的 AI 基础围绕三大支柱构建：Driver（车内 agent）、Simulator（仿真器）和 Critic（评估器）——均由一个被描述为"多模态世界行动语言模型"的单一 Waymo Foundation Model 驱动。

On end-to-end vs. modular architectures, Dolgov rejects the false dichotomy: Waymo uses an end-to-end model but augments it with "structured materialized intermediate representations" to enable closed-loop evaluation, richer RL reward functions, and runtime validation.
在端到端与模块化架构之争上，Dolgov 拒绝这种非此即彼的二元对立：Waymo 使用端到端模型，但通过"结构化实体化中间表示"对其进行增强，以支持闭环评估、更丰富的 RL 奖励函数和运行时验证。

One striking safety data point: over 170 million fully autonomous miles, the Waymo Driver is more than 13 times safer than a human driver for serious injury-causing collisions — translating to preventing a serious injury every 8 days at current scale.
一个令人印象深刻的安全数据点：在超过 1.7 亿英里的完全自动驾驶里程中，Waymo Driver 在导致严重伤害的碰撞事故上比人类司机安全 13 倍以上——以当前规模换算，相当于每 8 天预防一次严重伤害。

A remarkable emergent capability: the LiDAR system detected sparse return signals from a pedestrian's feet moving under a stopped bus — enough for the AI to predict the pedestrian's trajectory and brake before they emerged.
一项令人惊叹的涌现能力：LiDAR 系统检测到了一名行人在停着的公交车下移动的脚部稀疏反射信号——足以让 AI 预测行人的行进轨迹，并在其出现之前刹车。

Scaling is now exponential: it took 8 years to reach a ride-hailing service in 4 cities; earlier this year Waymo launched 4 new cities in a single day, and 10 of the 20 million total rides happened in just the last 7 months.
规模扩张现已进入指数级：Waymo 花了 8 年才在 4 个城市推出乘车服务；而在今年早些时候，他们在一天之内就进入了 4 座新城市，总计 2000 万次乘车中有 1000 万次发生在过去短短 7 个月内。

Waymo is now in 11 US cities and has announced plans to expand internationally to London and Tokyo this year, with the sixth-generation hardware platform (Waymo Driver Gen 6) powering a new vehicle designed around the rider experience.
Waymo 目前在美国 11 个城市运营，并已宣布计划于今年将服务扩展至伦敦和东京，第六代硬件平台（Waymo Driver Gen 6）为全新以乘客体验为核心设计的车辆提供动力。
