### Unsupervised Learning — Ep 97: Jev Creator on Decision Models, Benchmark Fatigue & Why the Labs Missed It

**核心论点**
Large language models have succeeded at impressive benchmarks while systematically failing at the reliability that real software demands.
大型语言模型在基准测试上成绩亮眼，却在真实软件所需的可靠性上屡屡失手。

TypeSave CEO Jago argues that the AI field has been building around the wrong abstraction — string-output LLMs — when the future of software is machine-native, structured, and reliability-first.
TypeSave CEO Jago 认为，AI 领域一直在围错了抽象层构建——以字符串输出为核心的 LLM——而软件的未来应该是机器原生、结构化、可靠性优先的。

The JEV model (based on RLCD) is his attempt to build that cognitive primitive: a "System 1" intelligence designed to be composable, semantically reliable, and suited for machine-native consumption rather than human-pleasing output.
JEV 模型（基于 RLCD 方法）正是他为此构建认知原语的尝试：一种"系统一"式的智能，设计目标是可组合、语义可靠，服务于机器原生消费而非取悦人类。

**关键洞察**

- **基准疲劳已提前到来。** Jago 预期基准信任崩塌需要更长时间，但市场对无效 benchmark 刷分的免疫速度远超预期，尤其是在他们这类决策模型赛道。
  Benchmark fatigue arrived faster than expected. Jago thought it would take longer for the market to become immune to benchmark-gaming, but it happened quickly — especially in the decision-model category.

- **KV cache 是编程智能体的"暴政"。** 现有编程智能体的几乎所有设计决策——压缩、路由、子智能体上下文传递——都受限于 KV cache 的 append-only 特性，这让众多更优雅的架构模式无从实现。
  The KV cache is the "tyranny" holding back coding agents. Almost every UX decision in coding agents — compaction, routing, sub-agent context handoff — is constrained by the KV cache's append-only nature, blocking more elegant architectural patterns.

- **模型多样性被高估，可靠性被低估。** 越来越多的团队在追逐新颖的模型形态，却忽视了真正重要的东西：在实际工作负载中切实可用。
  Model shape diversity is overrated; reliability is underrated. Too many teams are chasing novel model shapes without caring enough about whether they're actually useful in real workloads.

- **GPT-3.5 时代就应该实现 AGI 意义上的自动化了。** Jago 认为，从 GPT-3.5 起，模型能力就已足以自动化大量低难度的经济性工作，只是整个行业没能将其可靠地落地。
  We could have automated much more with GPT-3.5-era models. The capability was there; the field just failed to reliably deploy it.

- **LLM 应用开发者陷入了"斯德哥尔摩综合症"。** 开发者已习惯于接受模型提供商的单方面路线图，而没有意识到自己有能力、也有责任推动模型能力与真实需求共同演进。
  Developers are in a "Stockholm syndrome" with model providers — accepting their roadmap rather than collaborating to push intelligence where it's actually needed.

**最值得关注的信号**
Jago's framing of "semantic dependent typing" — using JEV-like models to verify software properties expressed in natural language rather than strict types — points toward a future where AI is woven into the type system itself, not just the code generation layer.
Jago 提出"语义依赖类型"的构想——用 JEV 类模型验证用自然语言表达的软件属性，而非依赖严格类型系统——预示着 AI 未来将嵌入类型系统本身，而不仅仅是代码生成层。
