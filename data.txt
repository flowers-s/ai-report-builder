Zara Zhang (@zarazhangrui)

Zara surfaced a provocative paper coining the term **"Tragedy of the Cognitive Commons"**: AI output verification requires deep expertise; deep expertise comes from years of grunt work; but AI is eliminating the grunt work first. *"So we're building systems that need experts to verify them, while destroying the pipeline that creates those experts."* 她分享的这篇论文点出了一个深刻的悖论：AI 在加速消灭"低价值重复工作"的同时，也在瓦解培育专家的土壤——而我们恰恰需要这些专家来审查 AI 的输出。这是一种认知公地的悲剧。

🎙️ PODCAST: No Priors — "Chasing Trillion-Dollar Companies, Founder Ambition, Token Budgets, and Regulatory Capture"

Sarah Guo and Elad Gil delivered a wide-ranging conversation touching on some of the most pressing questions in AI investing and technology policy.

**On trillion-dollar companies:** Elad argues that while we've seen an unprecedented 5-year sprint to trillion-dollar valuations (Anthropic, OpenAI, SpaceX), this was a punctuated equilibrium, not a new normal. 他认为市场上存在一种危险的错觉——人们把过去五年的异常爆发当成了常态，但真正能在五年内再跑出万亿美元公司的赛道屈指可数。

**On founder ambition:** Both Sarah and Elad observe a worrying trend of top founders shying away from head-on competition with labs, instead retreating into niches. *"I'm concerned about the best founders — what are they doing?"* 他们担忧的不是普通创业者，而是那些本可正面挑战大模型厂商、却选择"安全路线"的顶级创始人。

**On token budgets & RSI:** Elad introduces the concept of "return on invested tokens" — as compute becomes scarce, labs will increasingly allocate it like capital: to the few researchers who generate outsized results. 他提出了"token 投资回报率"这一框架：当算力成为稀缺资源，谁能获得最多 token 配额，将取决于谁能产出最高价值的研究成果，这将重塑 AI 实验室的内部权力结构。

**On regulatory capture:** The nuclear energy parallel — France at 70% nuclear power, the US at 18% with no new reactor in 40 years — serves as a warning about AI regulation. 法国核能的成功与美国的停滞被用作一面镜子：过度安全优先的监管体制往往以牺牲全民福祉为代价，AI 监管者需要在风险和收益两侧同时看清楚。

**On California's billionaire tax & tech migration:** Both hosts see the tax bill as potentially triggering a mass exodus from California to Texas or other hubs, especially with a rumored exit tax in 2028. 他们认为加州正在用政策把最具创造力的群体逼走，而德克萨斯州的能源与硬件生态正在迅速崛起，成为最有竞争力的替代选项之一。
