## Guillermo Rauch (@rauchg)

Guillermo delivered one of the week's sharpest security takes: the internet's encryption infrastructure had a master key with the password *"4"* — just the number four. *"This key could be used to target and decrypt ~everything."*
Guillermo 发出本周最犀利的安全评论：互联网加密基础设施的主密钥密码竟然是 *"4"*——就是数字四。*"这把密钥可以针对性地解密几乎一切。"*

He also praised a real-world Generative UI example powered by AI Gateway, noting how adding a single line of code to the AI SDK gives you "Google" — not just search, but weather, sports, stocks, and elections.
他还称赞了一个由 AI Gateway 驱动的现实生成式 UI 案例，指出在 AI SDK 中仅需一行代码便可接入"谷歌"——不只是搜索，还有天气、体育、股市和选举信息。

## 🎙️ PODCAST: Training Data

**Episode:** David Silver and Lasse Espeholt of Ineffable Intelligence: A Superlearner for the Age of Experience
**Link:** https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLOhHNjZItNnMm5tdW61JpnyxeYH5NDDx8

### 核心论点
**Core Argument**

The central thesis of this episode is that superintelligence requires building systems that learn continuously from their own experience — not from human-generated data alone.
本期播客的核心论点是：通往超级智能的道路，在于构建能够从自身经验中持续学习的系统——而非单纯依赖人类生成的数据。

David Silver (co-creator of AlphaGo) and Lasse Espeholt, co-founders of Ineffable Intelligence, argue that the LLM paradigm has proven the power of scale — but the next leap requires agents that discover new knowledge the way humanity collectively has: from scratch, through experience.
David Silver（AlphaGo 联合创始人）与 Lasse Espeholt，Ineffable Intelligence 联合创始人，认为 LLM 范式已证明了规模的力量——但下一次跃迁需要智能体像人类集体认知那样从经验中自主发现新知识。

### 关键洞察
**Key Insights**

The "superlearner" concept: a system that doesn't just retrieve knowledge but keeps learning and discovering indefinitely — the missing piece for true AGI/superintelligence.
"超级学习者"概念：一个不只是检索知识、而是无限学习和发现的系统——这是通向真正 AGI/超级智能缺失的一环。

Silver's conviction from his AlphaGo days remains unchanged: reinforcement learning from experience — not supervised learning from human data — is the path to emergent intelligence.
Silver 自 AlphaGo 时代起的核心信念从未改变：从经验中强化学习——而非从人类数据中监督学习——才是智能涌现的路径。

The LLM era provided an unexpected lesson: massive pre-training followed by rapid fine-tuning is a powerful paradigm even for experience-based systems, and Ineffable is incorporating these insights.
LLM 时代带来了意外的启示：大规模预训练加上快速微调是一种强大范式，即使对于经验驱动的系统也适用，Ineffable 正在将这些洞察融入其方法论。

The contrast between human collective discovery and current AI is stark: humanity built language, science, and mathematics from nothing — AI systems haven't cracked this yet, and that gap defines the frontier.
人类集体认知与当前 AI 之间的差距依然巨大：人类从零开始构建了语言、科学和数学——AI 系统还未突破这一关，而这正是前沿的所在。

Espeholt brings a practical systems perspective: the engineering challenge is building systems that remain stable and continue improving without human intervention or data injection — a fundamentally different reliability requirement than today's models.
Espeholt 带来了务实的系统视角：工程挑战在于构建无需人类干预或数据注入也能持续稳定提升的系统——这与当今模型截然不同的可靠性要求。
