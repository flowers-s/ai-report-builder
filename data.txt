## Swyx (@swyx)

At a Midjourney meetup, Swyx had a "smol aha moment": the reason ontologies and knowledge graphs are suddenly trending now—not three years ago—is that "good enough" intelligence has become too cheap to meter.
在一场 Midjourney 聚会上，Swyx 灵光乍现：知识图谱（KG）现在才爆火而非三年前，恰恰是因为"足够好"的智能已经便宜到几乎免费。

When intelligence is commoditized, its complements—like structured knowledge—become more valuable. Talks on knowledge graphs and ontologies are on fire precisely because the "hardest part of KGs"—reasoning over them—is now cheap.
当智能本身被商品化，其互补资产（如结构化知识）价值反而上升。知识图谱相关演讲大受追捧，正因为推理这一曾经的难题如今已近乎免费。

## Peter Yang (@petergyang)

Peter is stress-testing the "vibe-coded SaaS" model: in today's environment, a fast-built SaaS can serve as a self-serve funnel into a more expensive services offering rather than generating direct SaaS revenue.

## Madhu Guru (@realmadhuguru)

Madhu is evangelizing a clear builder playbook: always prototype with the best frontier model first, validate the UX, then—6–8 weeks later—migrate to open-weight or smaller models once they catch up.
Madhu 力推一套清晰的创业者方法论：原型阶段永远用最强前沿模型，验证好用户体验之后，等 6-8 周开源或小模型追上来，再迁移降本。

The common failure mode he sees: teams get stuck in Step 1, never optimizing for production. Don't start with the cheapest model; validate first, optimize second.
他观察到的常见失误是：团队停留在第一步，从未针对生产环境优化。核心原则是：别从最便宜的模型开始，先验证，再优化。

## Zara Zhang (@zarazhangrui)

Zara dropped a sharp insight on technology adoption: people don't adopt new tools because of efficiency—they adopt them because of social proof and fear of being left behind. The diffusion of technology is fundamentally a SOCIAL process, not a rational one.
Zara 分享了一个关于技术采用的深刻洞见：人们接受新工具不是因为效率提升，而是因为社会认同和被抛下的焦虑。技术扩散的本质是社交过程，而非理性决策。

She also argued that meetings should have zero gaps between saying and doing: an efficient meeting leaves no to-do list behind because all actions are done during the meeting, ideally by agents listening in real time.
她还提出：高效会议不应留下待办清单，所有行动应在会议中即时完成——理想情况下由实时监听的 AI 智能体当场执行。

On AI training: the best enablement isn't a course—it's pulling an AI agent into a team group chat and letting people watch it work.
关于 AI 培训：最好的 AI 使能不是上课，而是把智能体拉进团队群聊，让大家直接观摩它工作。

## Aditya Agarwal (@adityaag)

Aditya announced an investment in TryRivo, which is building "self-driving finance": agents connect to your existing checking account, learn your cash flow, sweep dollars into Treasury-backed yield, and return them before bills hit. Every dollar earns until the moment you spend it.
Aditya 宣布投资 TryRivo——一家正在构建"自动驾驶金融"的公司：智能体接入现有支票账户，学习现金流规律，将闲置资金导入国债收益，并在账单到期前自动归还。每一分钱都在被花出去之前保持增值。

The hard problem is asymmetric cost: returning money a day early costs a little yield, but returning it a day late bounces a bill and destroys trust. The founder—with a PhD in AI, 7,000+ citations, and robotaxi experience at Cruise—has solved harder versions of this problem before.
核心难点在于非对称成本：提前一天归还损失一点收益，晚一天则导致账单跳票、信任崩塌。该创始人拥有 AI 博士学位、7000+ 引用，且曾主导 Cruise 的 L4 无人驾驶商业化——比这更难的问题他已经解决过了。

He also highlighted the importance of AI interpretability: "The right way to deploy AI at scale is to understand how it works. It does NOT need to be a black box."
他还强调 AI 可解释性的重要性："大规模部署 AI 的正确方式是理解它的工作原理——它不必是黑盒。"

## 🎙️ Podcast: Training Data

**"Chai Discovery's Bitter Lesson: Drug Design Is Another Scaling Problem"**
**《Chai Discovery 的苦涩教训：药物设计是另一个规模化问题》**

Josh and Matt, co-founders of Chai Discovery, joined Training Data to explain their thesis: drug discovery should look more like software engineering, with abstraction layers and rapid iteration—not the serendipitous trial-and-error of traditional biotech.
Chai Discovery 联合创始人 Josh 和 Matt 做客 Training Data 节目，阐述他们的核心理念：药物发现应该像软件工程一样，拥有抽象层和快速迭代能力——而非传统生物技术那种依赖偶然性的反复试错。

Their guiding principle is simplicity: Chai-1 (their predecessor model) had 23 distinct submodules, making iteration painful. Chai-2 was redesigned to be dramatically simpler, which unlocked cleaner scaling laws and faster research cycles.
他们的核心原则是简洁性：前代模型 Chai-1 有 23 个独立子模块，迭代极为痛苦。Chai-2 被彻底重新设计，大幅简化架构，解锁了更清晰的规模化规律和更快的研究周期。

The "bitter lesson" echoes Sutton's original: just as in chess and Go, the systems that scaled simply with more compute won. Chai Discovery is applying the same principle to molecular biology—and early results suggest it's working.
这一"苦涩教训"呼应了 Sutton 的原版论断：就像国际象棋和围棋一样，靠算力规模化的简单系统最终获胜。Chai Discovery 将同样的原则应用于分子生物学——早期结果表明这一路线正在奏效。

Drug discovery has historically sat at the boundary between what can be engineered and what must be tested in the real world. That boundary is shifting: AI is pushing more of the process into the "engineerable" side, reducing the need for expensive wet-lab experiments.
药物发现历来处于"可工程化"与"必须在现实中验证"的边界。这条边界正在移动：AI 正在将越来越多的流程推向可工程化的一侧，减少对昂贵湿实验室实验的依赖。
