Madhu Guru (@realmadhuguru) — AI & Training Data

Madhu Guru 指出一个普遍误解：训练数据是低技能的重复劳动。恰恰相反，推动模型能力前沿所需的训练数据，要求极深的领域专业知识，尤其是软件工程之外的复杂知识工作。
Madhu Guru challenged the misconception that training data is low-skill grunt work. The data needed to advance the model frontier requires deep domain expertise for high-economic-value tasks — especially complex knowledge work outside software engineering.

*"That's why we have SWE agents and not knowledge work agents yet."*

这也是为何目前 AI 在软件工程 Agent 上突飞猛进，而在更广泛知识工作 Agent 上仍进展缓慢——高质量训练数据的稀缺是核心瓶颈。他特别提及 Mercor 等公司正在做的数据工作是"高杠杆、高技能"且被严重低估的贡献。
This explains why we have SWE agents but not general knowledge-work agents yet — scarce high-quality training data is the core bottleneck. Companies like Mercor doing this work are doing critically important, deeply underappreciated work.

Aaron Levie (@levie) — Box CEO

Aaron Levie 今日发表了三条关于 AI 与企业软件未来的深度洞察。第一，他预判未来 AI 使用场景将在模型家族间显著分化——高端任务依赖前沿模型，高频低复杂度任务将迁移至更廉价的模型，而能够高效路由工作负载的 Agent 编排层将成为新的核心竞争力。
Aaron Levie shared three sharp observations today. First, he predicts AI use cases will stratify between frontier models for high-end tasks and cheaper models for high-volume workloads, making intelligent routing and cost-optimizing agent orchestration a major new hard problem.

*"The layer that can efficiently route the workload to the right model will then become increasingly valuable."*

第二，他指出市场低估了 AI 蚕食企业软件的真正障碍——软件开发成本只是其中一环，企业软件真正的大头成本在于 GTM（市场进入）：咨询式销售、实施支持、生态集成。AI 让软件变便宜，但反而让差异化和可发现性变得更难。
Second, he argued the market misunderstands AI eating enterprise software: development costs are only one piece. GTM — consultative selling, implementation support, integration — is the plurality of cost, and AI actually makes discoverability and differentiation *harder*, not easier.

第三，Box 宣布推出 Web 端 Markdown 编辑器、完整 CLI 支持、评论与版本历史，以及 Box Drive 挂载能力，让用户可在 Claude Cowork、Codex、Cursor、Obsidian 等工具中直接访问所有文件。
Third, Box announced a Markdown editor, full CLI, commenting, version history, and Box Drive mount support — letting users access all their files directly from Claude Cowork, Codex, Cursor, Obsidian, and more.
