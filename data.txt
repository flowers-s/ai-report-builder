## 🎙️ Podcast: AI & I by Every

**How Sam Altman Uses Dots to Take Back His Time**

This episode features Dan Shipper in conversation with Sam Altman, recorded live at OpenAI Dev Day 2026.
本期节目收录了 Dan Shipper 与 Sam Altman 在 OpenAI Dev Day 2026 现场的对话。

**核心论点 / Core Argument**

Sam Altman argues that AI — particularly OpenAI's new "Dots" personal agent — is ushering in a renaissance, not an industrial revolution. The key distinction: the Renaissance was centered on people and human potential; the Industrial Revolution turned people into cogs. Sam's vision is AI that gives people back control of their time and creative energy, not one that replaces them.
Sam Altman 认为，AI——尤其是 OpenAI 新推出的个人助理"Dots"——正在开启一场文艺复兴，而非工业革命。核心区别在于：文艺复兴以人和人类潜能为中心；工业革命将人变成了机器的齿轮。Sam 的愿景是用 AI 让人重新掌控自己的时间和创造力，而非取代人类。

**关键洞察 / Key Insights**

- *"People just want so much more stuff."* Latent demand has massively exceeded expectations. Rather than AI eliminating jobs, it's surfaced enormous unmet needs — developers have never been able to do more, but the bar has never been higher either.
  "人们只是想要更多的东西。"潜在需求已大幅超出预期。AI 并非在消灭工作，而是释放了巨量未被满足的需求——开发者从未有过如此强大的能力，但门槛也从未如此之高。

- Dots gave Sam back his mornings. He describes how checking his phone used to pollute his best creative hours with urgent but manageable issues. Now his Dot handles triage, and he gets deep work time back.
  Dots 让 Sam 重新找回了清晨的时光。他描述了过去查看手机如何用紧急但可处理的事务污染了他最宝贵的创造力时段。现在他的 Dot 负责分诊，他重新获得了深度工作的时间。

- *"The final boss of building is connecting."* With code now essentially free, distribution and discovery become the critical bottleneck. This is why OpenAI launched its platform ecosystem and app marketplace at Dev Day.
  "构建的最终 Boss 是连接。"随着代码成本趋近于零，分发和发现成为关键瓶颈。这也是 OpenAI 在 Dev Day 推出平台生态系统和应用市场的原因。

- On Decisions API / ultra-fast inference: Sam confirmed the massive difference speed makes to the iterative thinking loop. He references Brett Victor's talk on creators needing *"immediate connection to what they're creating"* as the intellectual framing for why Ultrafast matters.
  关于 Decisions API / 超快推理：Sam 确认了速度对迭代思维循环的巨大影响。他引用 Brett Victor 关于创作者需要"与所创造物建立即时连接"的演讲，作为 Ultrafast 重要性的思想框架。

- There will always be a frontier tier (faster, smarter, pricier), but OpenAI is committed to continuing its Moore's-law-beating compression of value per dollar for the mainstream tier.
  始终会有前沿层级（更快、更智能、更昂贵），但 OpenAI 承诺将继续以超越摩尔定律的速度压缩主流层级的每美元价值。

- Sam wants to build and ship something himself — not just a side project, but a real ChatGPT plugin extension — because he genuinely believes friction reduction unlocks disproportionate creation.
  Sam 想亲自构建和发布一些东西——不仅仅是个人小项目，而是一个真正的 ChatGPT 插件扩展——因为他真心相信降低摩擦能带来不成比例的创造力释放。
