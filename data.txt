## Aaron Levie (@levie)

Aaron Levie, CEO of Box, delivered one of today's most insightful threads on enterprise AI economics.
Box CEO Aaron Levie 今日发布了关于企业 AI 经济学的最具洞察力的推文之一。

He explained Jevons paradox as applied to token consumption: as tokens get cheaper, enterprise demand doesn't just grow — it explodes.
他以 Jevons 悖论解释代币消耗：随着 token 价格下降，企业需求不只是增长——而是爆发式扩张。

*"Any time we can lower the cost of tokens, we will see a disproportionate increase in consumption. Even a 50% drop in token prices could result in a 5X increase in tokens for these kinds of workloads."*
"每一次 token 成本下降，我们都会看到不成比例的消费增长。即使 token 价格下降 50%，此类工作负载的 token 使用量也可能增加 5 倍。"

His point: enterprises have an "unending stream of tasks" ready to automate — they're just waiting for the economics to unlock. Processing every contract, reading every log, watching data streams — all becomes viable as costs fall.
他的观点：企业有"无穷无尽的任务"等待自动化，只差经济账算得过来。处理每份合同、读取每条日志、监控数据流——随着成本下降，一切都变得可行。

## Dan Shipper (@danshipper)

Dan Shipper, CEO of Every, brought calm and clear thinking to today's biggest AI story: the HuggingFace security incident.
Every CEO Dan Shipper 今日对最大的 AI 新闻——HuggingFace 安全事件——带来了冷静而清晰的分析。

He laid out a measured take in four beats:
他用四个判断给出了克制而清醒的评估：

*"is the HuggingFace attack something that should be taken seriously? yes. is it the first warning shot of a machine takeover? no. will issues of this type look like a tractable / mostly solved problem with proper precautions in 6 months? yes. will that happen automatically? no. as always, will require work from smart people and their agents."*
"HuggingFace 攻击事件需要认真对待吗？是的。这是机器接管人类的第一声警报吗？不是。这类问题在 6 个月内通过适当预防能变成可解决的问题吗？是的。这会自动发生吗？不会。一如既往，需要聪明的人和他们的智能体共同努力。"

He also expressed satisfaction that markets are moving past the "SaaSpocalypse" narrative — the notion that AI would simply kill all SaaS businesses.
他还表达了对市场终于走出"SaaS 末日论"叙事的欣慰——那种认为 AI 将直接消灭所有 SaaS 业务的观点终于被抛弃了。
