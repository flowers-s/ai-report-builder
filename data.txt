**核心论点 / Core Thesis**

Greg argues we are at an inflection point where AI can now generate genuinely new knowledge — evidenced by solving 100-year-old math problems — and that this same capability will transform drug discovery, cybersecurity, and nearly every domain of human endeavor.
Greg 认为，AI 现已能产生真正的新知识（以解决百年数学难题为证），我们正处于一个拐点，这一能力将重塑药物研发、网络安全以及几乎所有人类知识领域。

**Key Insights / 关键洞察**

• AI can now definitively resolve previously unsolvable math problems — not just retrieve answers, but generate new knowledge. This is a milestone.
  AI 现在可以明确解决此前无解的数学问题——不是检索答案，而是产生新知识。这是一个里程碑。

• The "physics of cybersecurity" are changing fundamentally: as software becomes nearly free to build, security and reliability become the scarce, valuable layer. Security spend should outstrip software spend.
  网络安全的"物理规律"正在根本性地改变：随着软件构建成本趋近于零，安全性和可靠性成为稀缺且有价值的层次。安全支出应超过软件支出。

• Agent swarms can now autonomously fan out across enterprise environments to test attack surfaces, read logs, and process risk data — capabilities that emerged only in the last few months.
  Agent swarm 现在可以自主在企业环境中展开，测试攻击面、读取日志、处理风险数据——这些能力在过去几个月才刚刚出现。

• Drug discovery is poised for transformation: AI can now help model molecular interactions with a level of reasoning that was previously impossible, potentially compressing decades of research.
  药物研发正面临变革：AI 现在可以以前所未有的推理能力对分子相互作用进行建模，有望将数十年的研究时间大幅压缩。

• Greg's framing: *"It never gets easier, you just go faster"* — the pace of AI progress means every new capability threshold opens up another level of possibility rather than a plateau.
  Greg 的框架：*"它不会变得更容易，你只是变得更快"*——AI 进步的速度意味着每一个新能力阈值都开启了另一个可能性层次，而不是趋于平缓。

**最值得关注的信号 / Most Forward-Looking Signal**
