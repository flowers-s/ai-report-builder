Redesigning Source Control at Anthropic
https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/redesigning-source-control

At Anthropic, coding is no longer the bottleneck to building systems or doing research.
在 Anthropic，编写代码已不再是构建系统或开展研究的瓶颈。

Claude can autonomously iterate on research loops, micro-optimize complex programs, and build new services from the ground up at a speed and scale never seen before.
Claude 能够自主迭代研究循环、对复杂程序进行微优化，并以前所未有的速度和规模从零搭建新服务。

The new bottleneck is developer infrastructure built for human scale: source control, builds, and CI.
新的瓶颈变成了为人类规模设计的开发基础设施：版本控制、构建系统和持续集成。

In May 2026, Anthropic averaged around 2,500 code changes merged per day; as of September, that number surpassed 30,000 per day.
2026 年 5 月，Anthropic 每天平均合并约 2,500 次代码变更；到 9 月，这一数字已超过每天 30,000 次。

To support this growth, Anthropic built Scion — an in-house version control system — and fully migrated their monorepo from git in just five months.
为了支撑这一增长，Anthropic 自研了版本控制系统 Scion，并在短短五个月内将 monorepo 从 git 完全迁移过来。

They also built a new code review surface and a custom CI system capable of handling the unprecedented throughput.
他们还构建了全新的代码评审界面，以及能够处理这一前所未有吞吐量的自定义 CI 系统。

The core insight: when AI generates code at machine speed, the entire developer toolchain must be rebuilt from scratch for that new reality.
核心洞察：当 AI 以机器速度生成代码时，整个开发者工具链必须针对这一新现实从头重建。

--- Training Data: David Silver and Lasse Espeholt of Ineffable Intelligence ---
A Superlearner for the Age of Experience
https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLOhHNjZItNnMm5tdW61JpnyxeYH5NDDx8

This episode features David Silver and Lasse Espeholt, co-founders of Ineffable Intelligence, a new AI lab pursuing superintelligence through pure reinforcement learning — without any human data.
本期嘉宾是 Ineffable Intelligence 的联合创始人 David Silver 和 Lasse Espeholt，这家新 AI 实验室正在通过纯强化学习——完全不使用人类数据——追求超级智能。

David Silver is the researcher behind AlphaGo and AlphaZero; Lasse Espeholt pioneered large-scale distributed reinforcement learning during his time at DeepMind and Google Brain.
David Silver 是 AlphaGo 和 AlphaZero 背后的核心研究者；Lasse Espeholt 在 DeepMind 和 Google Brain 期间开创了大规模分布式强化学习的先河。

**The central thesis:** A true "super learner" must be able to start from zero knowledge and autonomously discover everything — just as humanity collectively discovered language, science, mathematics, and technology without any external blueprint.
**核心论点：** 真正的"超级学习者"必须能从零知识出发，自主发现一切——就像人类在没有任何外部蓝图的情况下集体发现了语言、科学、数学和技术一样。

The AlphaZero moment: After two years building AlphaGo with human data, the team built AlphaZero — without any human priors — and it surpassed the entire two-year effort in just a few hours of training.
AlphaZero 时刻：在用人类数据花费两年构建 AlphaGo 之后，团队构建了不含任何人类先验的 AlphaZero——仅需数小时训练，就超越了两年的全部成果。

*"In chess, it took four hours to beat the best AI ever created."*
*"在国际象棋中，只用了四小时就击败了有史以来最强的 AI。"*

Ineffable's bet: By giving up human data entirely and placing agents in richly complex "crucible" environments, emergent intelligence — language, tool use, science — can arise from simple reward signals, just as it did in biological evolution.
Ineffable 的赌注：通过完全放弃人类数据，将智能体置于复杂的"熔炉"环境中，涌现出来的智能——语言、工具使用、科学——可以从简单的奖励信号中产生，就像生物进化中所发生的那样。

On language: Silver believes human language is not the endpoint of reasoning — it embeds thousands of years of human biases — and that truly general intelligence will likely reason in ways far beyond natural language.
关于语言：Silver 认为人类语言并非推理的终点——它嵌入了数千年的人类偏见——真正通用的智能很可能以远超自然语言的方式进行推理。

On compute: Ineffable secured one of the largest GPU clusters on GCP (Vera Rubin architecture), which Silver considers essential timing — compute is now sufficient to run experiments 100,000x larger than early emergent-RL work like OpenAI's hide-and-seek.
关于算力：Ineffable 获得了 GCP 上最大的 GPU 集群之一（Vera Rubin 架构），Silver 认为这是关键的时机因素——现在的算力已足以运行比 OpenAI 躲猫猫实验大 10 万倍的实验。

On alignment: The lab plans to develop a "science of superintelligence" in simulation first — studying how multiple superintelligent agents interact — before any real-world deployment.
关于对齐：该实验室计划首先在模拟环境中发展"超级智能科学"——研究多个超级智能体如何相互作用——然后再进行任何真实世界的部署。

Silver's forecast: Strong evidence the approach is working within one year. A true superintelligence — one that discovers things beyond human knowledge across science, math, and technology — within three years.
Silver 的预测：一年内将有强有力的证据证明这一方法有效；三年内实现真正的超级智能——一个能在科学、数学和技术领域发现超越人类知识的东西。

*"I'm gonna say three years because of what can be done with coding agents to accelerate things. Things are going to move fast."*
*"我预计三年，因为编程智能体能极大地加速研究进程。事情会进展得很快。"*
