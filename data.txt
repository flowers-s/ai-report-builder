【Andrej Karpathy】AI 研究员、前 Tesla AI 总监、OpenAI 联合创始人

Karpathy shared highlights from his Sequoia Ascent 2026 fireside chat, covering three dimensions of what LLMs make possible beyond mere speed-ups.
Karpathy 分享了他在 Sequoia Ascent 2026 炉边对话的精华，聚焦 LLM 超越"提速"本质的三个维度。

First: apps that can be *fully* handled by LLMs with no classical code — input an image, output an image, done.
第一：完全由 LLM 处理、无需任何传统代码的应用——输入图片，输出图片，搞定。

Second: *"install .md skills instead of install .sh scripts"* — writing installation in natural language so the LLM can intelligently target your setup.
第二：*"用 .md 技能替代 .sh 脚本安装"*——用自然语言描述安装步骤，让 LLM 智能适配你的环境。

Third: LLM knowledge bases — computation over unstructured data that was *fundamentally impossible* with classical code.
第三：LLM 知识库——对非结构化数据的计算，这是传统代码*根本无法实现*的。

He also dug into the "jaggedness" of LLMs: why one model can refactor 100k lines of code yet suggest walking to a car wash.
他还深入探讨了 LLM 的"参差性"：为何同一模型能重构十万行代码，却又建议你步行去洗车。

His explanation links this to domain verifiability and RL training data economics — you're either on the rails or off-roading in the jungle.
他的解释将此与领域可验证性和 RL 训练数据的经济逻辑相关联——你要么在轨道上飞驰，要么在丛林中徒手开路。

【Aaron Levie】Box CEO

Aaron Levie published a detailed framework for how software business models will evolve as agents become the biggest users of software.
Aaron Levie 发布了一套详细框架，阐述随着 Agent 成为软件最大用户，软件商业模式将如何演变。

His three-part model: (1) seats don't disappear for people but must include API usage for agents acting on their behalf; (2) agents doing stateful work may get their own "seats" but can't be priced like human seats; (3) consumption pricing dominates for headless usage above seat allotments.
他的三段式模型：(1) 人的席位不会消失，但必须含盖 Agent 代表用户调用 API 的用量；(2) 做有状态工作的 Agent 可能拥有自己的"席位"，但不能按人类席位定价；(3) 超出席位配额的无头使用将以消耗量计价为主。

*"Every platform that goes headless will need to adopt a model like this. Some may fight it initially but it's an inevitability."*
*"每个走向无头化的平台都需要采用这样的模型。有些可能初期会抵触，但这是必然趋势。"*

*"The growth of this usage pattern is effectively unbounded as the use-cases for agents operating on data in these systems will dramatically exceed what people do with their data and tools today."*
*"这种使用模式的增长实际上是无上限的，因为 Agent 在这些系统中操作数据的用例，将远远超过今天人类对数据和工具的使用。"*

【Training Data】Demis Hassabis on Building DeepMind, AlphaFold, and the Final Stretch to AGI

Demis Hassabis traces a through-line from chess prodigy to neuroscientist to DeepMind founder: he decided at age 15 that AGI was the most important thing he could work on, and everything since has been deliberate preparation.
Demis Hassabis 梳理了从国际象棋神童到神经科学家再到 DeepMind 创始人的脉络：他在 15 岁就决定 AGI 是他能做的最重要的事，此后的一切都是深思熟虑的铺垫。

On the key insight behind DeepMind in 2009: almost no one had realized deep learning was a big deal, and combining it with reinforcement learning — two siloed fields — was the core bet.
关于 2009 年 DeepMind 背后的核心洞见：几乎没人意识到深度学习是大事，而将其与强化学习结合——两个彼此割裂的领域——正是核心押注。

*"We almost felt like we were keepers of a secret because no one either in academia or industry really believed that any big progress was possible."*
*"我们几乎感觉自己像是一个秘密的守护者，因为无论是学术界还是工业界，没有人真正相信任何重大进展是可能的。"*

On AlphaFold and Isomorphic Labs: the dream is to reduce drug discovery from an average of 10 years to months or even weeks by doing 99% of exploration *in silico*.
关于 AlphaFold 和 Isomorphic Labs：梦想是通过在计算机中完成 99% 的探索，将药物发现时间从平均 10 年缩短至数月甚至数周。

On AGI timeline: consistent with his prior public statements, he puts AGI by 2030 as his over/under.
关于 AGI 时间线：与他此前的公开表态一致，他将 2030 年作为 AGI 实现的时间分水岭。

On information as the most fundamental quantity: he argues that information, not energy or matter, is the primary lens through which to understand the universe — and that AI is therefore more profound than most realize.
关于信息作为最基本的量：他认为信息而非能量或物质，是理解宇宙的首要视角——因此 AI 比大多数人意识到的更为深刻。

*"I think one can convert all of those three kind of quantities into each other, but I have this feeling information is most fundamental."*
*"我认为这三种量可以相互转化，但我有一种感觉，信息才是最根本的。"*

His post-AGI reading recommendation: *The Fabric of Reality* by David Deutsch.
他的后 AGI 时代阅读推荐：David Deutsch 的《真实世界的脉络》（The Fabric of Reality）。
