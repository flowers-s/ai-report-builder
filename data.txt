Guillermo raised a sharp security concern: agents running in cloud VMs are potentially vulnerable to timing attacks — a class of side-channel exploits on shared-host VMs known since at least 2015, with no complete solution.
Guillermo 提出了一个尖锐的安全问题：运行在云端虚拟机中的智能体可能面临时序攻击——这是一类自 2015 年以来就已知的共享宿主虚拟机侧信道漏洞，目前尚无完整解决方案。

*"Agents can play 'Ouija' in the cloud."*
*"智能体可以在云端'玩碟仙'。"*

He noted that the world's cyber infrastructure runs on the assumption that such attacks are "theoretically possible, but who would have the ingenuity & gall to go that far!" — and that with AI agents, this assumption is now being stress-tested at scale.
他指出，全球网络基础设施一直运行在这样的假设之上：此类攻击"理论上可行，但谁会有这种奇思妙想和胆量真的去实施呢！"——而随着 AI 智能体的出现，这一假设正在被大规模地挑战。

## 🎙️ PODCAST: No Priors — "Democratizing Security for the Agentic Web with Cloudflare CTO Dane Knecht"

Published: October 23, 2026
Source: https://www.youtube.com/@NoPriorsPodcast

### 深度总结

**核心论点**

AI is simultaneously the cause of the new security crisis on the internet and the most powerful tool available to defend against it.
AI 同时是互联网新安全危机的根源，也是应对这一危机最强大的防御工具。

As agentic AI systems proliferate, the attack surface is exploding — and legacy security assumptions built for human-speed threats are breaking down at machine speed.
随着自主 AI 系统的大规模扩散，攻击面正在爆炸性扩大——专为人类速度威胁构建的传统安全假设正在以机器速度崩溃。

Cloudflare's CTO Dane Knecht believes the only path forward is building security infrastructure that is native to the agentic web from the ground up.
Cloudflare 首席技术官 Dane Knecht 认为，唯一的出路是从头构建原生于智能体互联网的安全基础设施。

**关键洞察**

- **The AI security paradox**: The same AI capabilities that enable sophisticated attacks also enable more powerful defenses — but defenders must move faster to close the gap. AI 安全悖论：赋能复杂攻击的 AI 能力，同样也能赋能更强的防御——但防御者必须更快地行动以弥合差距。

- **Air-gapping is not a viable strategy**: Serious technologists have suggested air-gapping critical infrastructure, but Knecht argues this is neither practical nor sufficient — the internet is too deeply embedded in how civilization functions. 气隙隔离不是可行策略：虽有严肃的技术专家建议对关键基础设施实施气隙隔离，但 Knecht 认为这既不实际也不够用——互联网已深度嵌入文明运转的方式之中。

- **Timing attacks are back**: AI agents running on shared cloud infrastructure reintroduce classic side-channel vulnerabilities (like timing attacks) at unprecedented scale, because agents can probe systems at machine speed and volume. 时序攻击卷土重来：在共享云基础设施上运行的 AI 智能体，以前所未有的规模重新引入了经典侧信道漏洞（如时序攻击），因为智能体能以机器的速度和规模探测系统。

- **The "who would dare?" assumption is dead**: Legacy security postures assumed that certain sophisticated attacks were possible in theory but too labor-intensive to actually execute. AI agents invalidate this assumption entirely — they can execute complex, multi-step attacks continuously and autonomously. "谁会真的这么做？"的假设已经死亡：传统安全态势假定某些复杂攻击理论上可行，但实际执行代价太高。AI 智能体彻底推翻了这一假设——它们可以持续自主地执行复杂的多步骤攻击。

- **Security must move from reactive to proactive**: The future of cyber defense is AI agents that continuously test, probe, and harden systems — running offense to improve defense — rather than waiting for breaches to patch. 安全必须从被动转向主动：网络防御的未来是 AI 智能体持续测试、探测和强化系统——以攻促守——而非等到被攻破后再打补丁。

### Deep Summary (English)

Dane Knecht, CTO of Cloudflare, joined the No Priors podcast to discuss what the rise of agentic AI means for internet security.
Cloudflare 首席技术官 Dane Knecht 参加了 No Priors 播客，探讨自主 AI 的崛起对互联网安全意味着什么。

He opened with a striking framing: AI went from being "how do we get everyone to use it?" to "how do we secure it?" almost overnight — and security, as usual, was an afterthought until the stakes became undeniable.
他以一个引人注目的框架开场：AI 几乎在一夜之间，从"如何让所有人使用它？"变成了"如何保护它的安全？"——而安全，一如既往，是事后才想到的，直到风险变得无可忽视。

The conversation centered on three interconnected themes: the expanding attack surface introduced by AI agents, the inadequacy of legacy security models, and the emerging category of AI-native security tools.
对话围绕三个相互关联的主题展开：AI 智能体带来的攻击面扩张、传统安全模型的不足，以及正在兴起的 AI 原生安全工具类别。

On attack surface: AI agents introduce new vectors. They can be prompt-injected, they operate with broad permissions, and they interact with systems at speeds and volumes no human could match. Traditional perimeter security assumes a human on the other end — agents break that model.
关于攻击面：AI 智能体引入了新的攻击向量。它们可以被提示注入，以广泛的权限运行，并以人类无法匹敌的速度和规模与系统交互。传统边界安全假设另一端是人类——智能体打破了这一模型。

On legacy models: The classic "trust but verify" or even "zero trust" frameworks were designed for human-speed interactions. Knecht argued that with agents making thousands of API calls per second, the economics of traditional security tooling break down — you need systems that can reason about intent and context at machine speed.
关于传统模型：经典的"信任但验证"乃至"零信任"框架，都是为人类速度的交互而设计的。Knecht 认为，当智能体每秒发起数千次 API 调用时，传统安全工具的经济逻辑就崩溃了——你需要能在机器速度下推理意图和上下文的系统。

Cloudflare's positioning is as the connective tissue of the internet — sitting between clients and servers for a huge portion of global web traffic — which gives them unique visibility into attack patterns across the entire ecosystem.
Cloudflare 将自身定位为互联网的结缔组织——在客户端和服务器之间处理全球大量网络流量——这赋予了他们对整个生态系统攻击模式的独特洞察力。

The optimistic note: the same properties that make AI dangerous (speed, scale, persistence) also make AI-powered defense unprecedented in its potential effectiveness.
乐观的一面：正是让 AI 变得危险的特性（速度、规模、持久性），也使 AI 驱动的防御在潜在有效性上前所未有。
