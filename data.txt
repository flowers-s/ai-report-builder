### AI & I by Every — "How a Professional Writer Writes With AI"

In this episode, Every staff writer Katie Parrott sits down with head of AI consulting Natalia Quintero to discuss her two-year AI writing journey and her new Compound Writing plugin.
本期节目中，Every 驻站作者 Katie Parrott 与 AI 咨询主管 Natalia Quintero 深度对谈，分享她两年来的 AI 写作历程以及她新开发的"Compound Writing"插件。

Katie explains that great AI writing starts not with prose but with context: audience personas, brand messaging, product differentiators, and fresh data that the model cannot access on its own.
Katie 解释道，出色的 AI 辅助写作并不从遣词造句开始，而是从提供上下文入手：受众画像、品牌定位、产品差异化优势，以及模型无法自行获取的最新原创数据。

*"Inputs are really what makes the writing unique."*
她总结道："输入的内容才是让写作变得独特的关键所在。"

She describes her Compound Writing plugin as a "gym for writers" — it codifies expert frameworks from authors like Vonnegut, Hitchcock, and Sorkin as reviewable skills you can invoke to evaluate and improve any piece.
她将 Compound Writing 插件比作"作家的健身房"——将冯内古特、希区柯克、索金等作家的专业框架编码为可调用的技能，帮助你从多个维度审视和改进文章。

On AI's broader impact, her thesis is that education and access will matter more than ever: the compounding benefits of AI risk concentrating in a small early-adopter elite, and the real challenge is expanding that opportunity to everyone.
谈及 AI 的更广泛影响，她的核心论点是：教育和获取渠道将比以往任何时候都更加重要——AI 的复利效应存在集中于少数早期采用者的风险，真正的挑战在于如何将这一机遇扩展至更多人。
