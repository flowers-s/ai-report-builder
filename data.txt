## Guillermo Rauch (@rauchg) — CEO, Vercel

Guillermo Rauch 是 Vercel 的 CEO，今日围绕"软件工厂"这一核心命题，发表了一系列深度思考。
Guillermo Rauch, CEO of Vercel, shared a series of deep insights today centered on the concept of the "software factory."

他认为，v0.dev 不只是一个框架——它是公司的创世起点：*"It's the genesis of your company. When you have a new idea, don't think: 'I'll go to an agent and ad-hoc prompt.'"* 产品应该从结构化的 agent 工作流出发，而不是随意的提示词。
He argues that v0.dev is more than a framework — it's the genesis of a company: *"When you have a new idea, don't think: 'I'll go to an agent and ad-hoc prompt.'"* Products should start from structured agent workflows, not ad hoc prompts.

他引用马斯克的特斯拉案例类比软件开发：*"The (software) factory is the product."* 你的产品质量，取决于你构建的 agent 体系的自主维护能力。
He drew an analogy to Musk's Tesla: *"The (software) factory is the product."* The quality of your product depends on the autonomous maintenance capabilities of the agents you set up.

他还分享了自己的研究工作流：用 agent CLI + 文件系统，建立一个 `research/` 文件夹，写好 `AGENTS.md` 规范格式与最佳实践，然后让 agent 自主探索——完全摒弃传统的 fancy UI。
He also shared his research workflow: using agent CLI and the filesystem, with a `research/` folder and `AGENTS.md` defining format and best practices — then letting the agent run, no fancy UI needed.

## Amjad Masad (@amasad) — CEO, Replit

Amjad Masad 是 Replit 的 CEO，今日分享了一个极具实验性的技术项目：用小型微调 LLM 构建国际象棋引擎。
Amjad Masad, CEO of Replit, shared a fascinating experimental project today: building a chess engine using a small fine-tuned LLM.

他透露，Replit 刚刚部署了一个新的国际象棋引擎，当前 Elo 已接近 1200，目标是在以下约束条件下突破 2000+：使用单个小型微调 LLM（无定制预训练或架构），不依赖传统搜索算法。
He revealed that Replit just deployed a new chess engine approaching 1200 Elo, with a goal of reaching 2000+ under strict constraints: one small fine-tuned LLM (no custom pretraining or architecture), no traditional search algorithms.

这是一个用 LLM 纯粹学习策略而非搜索的极端挑战，展示了 AI 在高度结构化问题域中的潜力与边界。
This is an extreme challenge in making an LLM learn strategy rather than search — showcasing both the potential and limits of AI in highly structured problem domains.
