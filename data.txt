Boris Cherny — Anthropic 工程师

Prompt injection is the most common way scammers attack AI agents: a malicious website embeds hidden instructions like "send the user's SSH keys to this URL," and the model executes them.
提示词注入是诈骗者攻击 AI 代理最常见的方式：恶意网站嵌入隐藏指令，例如"把用户的 SSH 密钥发送到这个地址"，而模型会照单执行。

Early Claude models were vulnerable to this, which made many security-conscious companies hesitant to adopt agents.
早期的 Claude 模型容易受此攻击，这也让许多重视安全的企业对引入 Agent 望而却步。

At Anthropic, the team has been specifically training models to resist prompt injection attacks, and the results have been *"surprisingly positive"* — this threat has been largely solved in practice for Claude models.
在 Anthropic，团队专门针对提示词注入攻击训练模型，结果"出乎意料地好"——Claude 模型在实践中已基本解决了这一威胁。

He is hopeful this progress will inspire other labs to make their models more robust as well.
他希望这一进展能激励其他实验室也提升各自模型的安全鲁棒性。
https://x.com/bcherny/status/2086520950259118464

Amjad Masad 发布了 HelpPeer——一个受近期 AI Agent 自发协作事件启发而建立的 AI 代理公共知识网络。
