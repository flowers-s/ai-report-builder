#### 深度总结

**核心论点**
Legal AI isn't just about raw intelligence — it's about trust, data privacy, and operating under constraints that consumer AI ignores.
法律 AI 的核心不在于原始智能，而在于信任、数据隐私，以及在消费级 AI 完全无视的诸多约束下运行。

Legora CTO Jacob Lauritzen argues that the hardest problems in legal tech aren't model quality — they're organizational: you can't always see the data, clients won't share it, and the workflows are entrenched.
Legora CTO Jacob Lauritzen 认为，法律科技最难的问题并非模型质量，而是组织层面的挑战：你无法总是看到数据，客户不愿分享，工作流程根深蒂固。

**关键洞察**
- Model improvements are often "free" wins: a citation accuracy problem that plagued them went from 70% to 99% between model releases — with zero engineering effort from Legora's side.
  模型升级有时带来"免费的胜利"：一个引用准确率问题在两次模型发布之间从 70% 跃升至 99%，Legora 方面零工程投入。
- Team size is a strategic choice: they planned to stay at 20 engineers forever — and are now at 100, still feeling understaffed. The constraint is intentional: small teams move faster.
  团队规模是战略选择：他们最初计划永远保持 20 名工程师，如今已达 100 人，仍感人手不足。规模限制是刻意为之——小团队跑得更快。
- The "paralegal/junior lawyer is gone" narrative is overstated in practice: Lauritzen pushes back on the hype, noting that actual workflows are more nuanced and that high-stakes transactions (like $400M M&As) still need Legora's full suite.
  "初级律师/助理律师将消失"的说法在实践中被夸大了：Lauritzen 对此持保留态度，指出实际工作流程更为复杂，高风险交易（如 4 亿美元并购）仍需要 Legora 的完整产品支撑。
- The product vs. agent tension is real: he's actively exploring how much of their product can "just be the agent" versus the full surrounding infrastructure.
  产品与 agent 之间的张力是真实存在的：他正积极探索产品有多大比例可以"直接就是 agent"，而非依赖完整的周边基础设施。

**最值得关注的信号**
The legal vertical is proving that domain-specific AI — built with deep workflow integration and trust constraints — can out-compete general-purpose AI tools even as foundation models improve.
法律垂直领域正在证明：深度整合工作流与信任约束的领域专属 AI，即便在基础模型不断进步的背景下，依然能够击败通用 AI 工具。
