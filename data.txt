### No Priors — "How Nuclear Will Unlock Energy Abundance with Valar Atomics Founder Isaiah Taylor"

No Priors, the leading AI and technology venture podcast, featured Isaiah Taylor, founder and CEO of Valar Atomics, recorded live at their Utah nuclear facility.
No Priors，顶尖AI与科技创投播客，邀请了Valar Atomics创始人兼CEO Isaiah Taylor，现场录制于其犹他州核设施。

This episode marks a historic moment: Valar Atomics became the first startup ever to generate nuclear power, and the first advanced reactor to make power outside a national laboratory.
本集记录了一个历史性时刻：Valar Atomics成为有史以来第一家产生核电的初创公司，也是第一座在国家实验室以外发电的先进反应堆。

Taylor explained Valar's philosophy: nuclear is fundamentally a hardware execution problem, not a design problem — *"we are willing to verticalize anything that is necessary on our path to scale."*
Taylor阐述了Valar的理念：核能从根本上是一个硬件执行问题，而非设计问题——"我们愿意将规模化道路上所需的任何事情都垂直整合进来。"

They demonstrated that their TRISO-fueled, helium-cooled reactor can passively cool itself after shutdown with no moving parts — eliminating the decay heat risk that caused Fukushima and Three Mile Island.
他们展示了其TRISO燃料、氦冷却反应堆在停堆后可以无需运动部件被动冷却——消除了导致福岛和三里岛事故的衰变热风险。

Valar also made history by powering the first-ever AI chip (NVIDIA Blackwell) using a nuclear reactor, hosting a live website at nuclearwebsite.com directly from the reactor.
Valar还创造了历史，用核反应堆为首个AI芯片（NVIDIA Blackwell）供电，并直接从反应堆托管了实时网站nuclearwebsite.com。

Taylor outlined a "tick rate" metric — the time between reactor milestones — going from 2 years 4 months for first atom split to 7 months for the second, with the goal of eventually deploying a new reactor every few minutes.
Taylor提出了"tick rate"指标——反应堆里程碑之间的时间——从第一次分裂原子用了2年4个月，到第二次只需7个月，目标是最终每隔几分钟就部署一台新反应堆。

He shared a compelling vision: by making energy 10x cheaper through AI-driven autonomous manufacturing, *"the cost of buying a thing will become the cost of energy used to make it"* — pointing toward a future where most physical goods approach near-zero cost.
他分享了一个引人深思的愿景：通过AI驱动的自主制造将能源成本降低10倍，"购买一件物品的成本将等同于制造它所消耗的能源成本"——指向一个大多数实物商品成本趋近于零的未来。
