## Madhu Guru (@realmadhuguru)

Madhu shared part 4 of her series on building great AI evals, focusing on "laddered eval strategies" that enterprises need for real production AI systems.
Madhu 分享了她关于构建优质 AI eval 系列文章的第四篇，聚焦于企业在真实生产 AI 系统中所需的"阶梯式 eval 策略"。

She outlined four key eval types: hill-climbing evals (pushing product quality frontiers), regression evals (ensuring you haven't broken existing functionality), smoke test evals (catching critical failures), and launch evals (close to real traffic, most realistic).
她梳理了四种核心 eval 类型：爬坡 eval（推动产品质量边界）、回归 eval（确保未破坏现有功能）、冒烟测试 eval（捕捉关键故障）以及上线 eval（接近真实流量，最贴近实际）。

## Aaron Levie (@levie)

Aaron highlighted a compelling post on "post-training for applied AI" — the practice of fine-tuning models specifically for vertical enterprise workflows to reduce cost and improve accuracy.
Aaron 重点介绍了一篇关于"应用 AI 的后训练"的精彩文章——即针对垂直企业工作流对模型进行专项微调，以降低成本、提升准确率。

他的核心洞见是：一旦你积累了足够深厚的领域专业知识和足够大的任务量，为特定任务专门构建模型就非常合理——尤其是在同时优化成本与质量的场景下。

## 🎙 Podcast: No Priors

**From Restoring Sight to Reimagining the Brain, with Max Hodak**

This episode of No Priors features Max Hodak — co-founder of Neuralink and founder of Science Corp — discussing his journey from brain-computer interfaces to his latest work restoring vision in blind patients using light-sensitive proteins (optogenetics), and his broader vision for reimagining what the brain can do.
这期《No Priors》邀请了 Max Hodak——Neuralink 联合创始人、Science Corp 创始人——讲述他从脑机接口到最新研究的历程：通过光敏蛋白（光遗传学技术）让盲人患者恢复视力，以及他对重塑人脑潜能的宏大愿景。

The conversation spans the ethics of brain enhancement, the hardware and biology challenges of neural interfaces, and what it truly means to push the frontier of human cognition in the age of AI.
对话涵盖脑增强的伦理问题、神经接口的硬件与生物学挑战，以及在 AI 时代真正推动人类认知边界意味着什么。
