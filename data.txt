Ep 96: Nebius CTO on Racing the Hyperscalers Before AWS Wakes Up
Ep 96：Nebius CTO 谈如何在 AWS 觉醒前与超大规模云厂商赛跑

Published: 2026-11-02 | Source: https://www.youtube.com/@RedpointAI

---

In this episode, Jacob Efron (Redpoint) sits down with Danila, the CTO of Nebius, to dig into the AI cloud infrastructure market, Nebius's origin story from Yandex, and the real bottlenecks in the AI buildout.
本期节目中，Jacob Efron（Redpoint）与 Nebius CTO Danila 深入对话，探讨 AI 云基础设施市场、Nebius 脱胎自 Yandex 的创业历程，以及 AI 算力扩张中真正的瓶颈所在。

**核心论点 | Core Thesis**

Nebius is betting it can become a genuine, software-first AI cloud — not just a GPU rental shop — and reach competitive parity with AWS and GCP before the chip shortage ends and the hyperscalers fully wake up to the market.
Nebius 的核心赌注是：在芯片短缺结束、超大规模云厂商全面发力之前，将自身打造成一家真正的软件优先 AI 云，而不仅仅是 GPU 出租商，从而与 AWS 和 GCP 形成平等竞争。

**关键洞察 | Key Insights**

Software is the moat, not the hardware.
软件才是护城河，而非硬件。
Nebius's founding principle: the ability to serve a customer running 10,000 GPUs in one interconnected cluster on the same platform as a self-service user running one GPU for 30 minutes is their core differentiator.
Nebius 的立身之本：在同一平台上，既能服务万卡互联集群客户，又能服务按需使用 30 分钟的个人用户——这才是真正的差异化竞争力。

The real bottleneck to the AI buildout is execution ability, not any single resource.
AI 基础设施扩张的真正瓶颈是执行力，而非某种单一资源短缺。
GPU shortage, CPU shortage, NVMe shortage, transformer lead times of years — all are problems simultaneously. The winner is whoever can navigate all of them at once. The most in-demand person in AI infrastructure right now? A great project manager.
GPU、CPU、NVMe 短缺，变压器交货期长达数年——这些问题同时存在。胜出者是能同时应对所有难题的那一方。当前 AI 基础设施领域最稀缺的人才？优秀的项目经理。

Hyperscalers lost early market share partly due to chip allocation constraints, not lack of interest.
超大规模云厂商早期市场份额受限，部分原因在于芯片分配约束，而非缺乏意愿。
AWS and Google face a constant juggle: use scarce GPU allocations for internal AI workloads or rent to the market. For them, the AI cloud business is still relatively small vs. their core revenue, making it hard to justify full infrastructure redesigns. This window won't last forever.
AWS 和 Google 面临持续的资源博弈：将稀缺 GPU 用于内部 AI 工作负载，还是对外出租？对它们而言，AI 云业务相对于核心营收仍属小盘，难以支撑完整的基础设施改造。但这一窗口期不会永远持续。

Inference is far from a solved problem — and the optimization opportunity is huge.
推理远未是一个「已解决」的问题，优化空间依然巨大。
Running open-weight models out of the box is "not that fast, not that cheap." Nebius invests in kernel optimization, model compression, selective pruning, and speculative decoding — and acquired a company specifically for that talent. 95% of their customers run modified, fine-tuned models, not vanilla open source.
开箱即用地运行开源权重模型「既不快，也不便宜」。Nebius 在内核优化、模型压缩、选择性剪枝和推测解码上重金投入，还专门收购了一家公司来获取这方面的人才。他们 95% 的客户运行的是经过微调的定制模型，而非原始开源模型。

The shift from "requests" to "outcomes" is the next frontier Danila is watching most closely.
从「请求」到「结果」的范式转变，是 Danila 目前最关注的下一个前沿。
As agents become long-running and multi-turn, companies will bid per outcome rather than per token. Nebius has a small research group focused on how to increase the probability of reaching the right outcome — not by scaling models, but by optimizing the harnesses, tools, and scaffolding around them.
随着 Agent 进入长周期、多轮对话模式，企业将按「结果」而非「token」定价。Nebius 组建了小型研究团队，专注于研究如何提高 Agent 达成目标的概率——不是靠扩大模型规模，而是优化模型周围的工具链和脚手架。

AI coding went from "total bullshit" to an essential daily tool in just one year — but Danila applies it conservatively.
AI 编程在短短一年内从「一无是处」变为每日必备工具——但 Danila 的团队采用极为审慎。
His policy: engineers are fully responsible for any AI-generated code — it's their PR, their on-call duty. Fully autonomous agents are banned from touching core services. He sees "slop" proliferating in merge requests and is vigilant about it.
他的政策：工程师对所有 AI 生成代码负完全责任——这是他们的 PR，他们的 on-call 职责。完全自主的 Agent 被禁止触碰核心服务。他观察到「低质量内容」正在 MR 中蔓延，对此保持高度警惕。
