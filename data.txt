## Aaron Levie (@levie)

Agent swarms are going to be transformative because they make compute a critical variable for tasks that were previously bottlenecked by human time — research, data processing, QA testing, cyber security, life sciences.
Agent 集群将带来颠覆性变革，因为它们让算力成为过去受限于人力时间的任务的核心变量——研究、数据处理、质量测试、网络安全、生命科学皆如此。

In a world where agents can use tools, write code, process data, and coordinate with each other, the only parameter that matters for solving arbitrarily complex problems is how many tokens you throw at it.
在一个 Agent 能够使用工具、编写代码、处理数据并相互协调的世界里，解决任意复杂问题的唯一参数就是你投入多少 token。

This changes not just individual fields but the competitive dynamics within them — compute-constrained problems in the limit become a new form of leverage.
这不仅改变了各个领域，还改变了这些领域内部的竞争格局——算力约束型问题在极限情况下成为一种新的杠杆形式。

## 🎙 Podcast: No Priors — Beam: The Great American Open Model

**Episode:** Beam: The Great American Open Model with ReflectionAI Co-Founder and CEO Misha Laskin
**Source:** https://www.youtube.com/@NoPriorsPodcast

### Deep Dive Summary / 深度总结

This episode features Misha Laskin, co-founder and CEO of Reflection AI, who built his company around a bold thesis: frontier AI should be open-weight and widely accessible. Over the past year, Reflection scaled from 30 to 300 people, assembled pre-training, mid-training, and reinforcement learning teams, and released Beam — their first open model.
本期播客聚焦 Reflection AI 联合创始人兼 CEO Misha Laskin，他的公司建立在一个大胆命题之上：前沿 AI 应该开放权重、广泛可用。过去一年，Reflection 从 30 人扩张到 300 人，组建了预训练、中间训练和强化学习团队，并发布了他们的第一个开放模型 Beam。

**On open vs. closed models / 关于开放与封闭模型之争：**

Misha argues that removing "cyber offensive" capabilities from AI simultaneously removes cyber defensive capabilities. He invokes Linus's Law: "with enough eyeballs, all bugs become shallow" — and applies the same principle to AI safety vulnerabilities.
Misha 认为，从 AI 中移除"网络攻击"能力的同时，也移除了网络防御能力。他援引 Linus 定律："足够多的眼睛盯着，所有 Bug 都会变浅"——并将同样的原则应用于 AI 安全漏洞。

He cited a concrete example: a powerful closed model was used to hack another company, and that company could only remediate itself by using open models to defend. The empirical world, he argues, is already making the case for openness.
他举了一个具体案例：一个强大的封闭模型被用于攻击另一家公司，而那家公司只能依靠开放模型来自我防御。他认为，现实世界的案例已经在为开放性代言。

**On scaling challenges / 关于规模化的挑战：**

The three major bottlenecks — compute, talent, and data — are real, but the talent dimension has been particularly intense given the ongoing "researcher wars." Reflection tackled this by being mission-driven: building truly frontier open intelligence, not just fine-tuning existing models.
三大瓶颈——算力、人才和数据——都是真实存在的，但鉴于持续的"研究员争夺战"，人才维度尤为激烈。Reflection 通过使命驱动来应对这一挑战：构建真正的前沿开放智能，而非仅仅微调现有模型。

**Key takeaway / 核心观点：**

Open-weight models aren't just a philosophical preference — they're increasingly a practical necessity for security, resilience, and innovation. Beam is Reflection AI's opening move in making that case at the frontier level.
开放权重模型不只是哲学偏好——它们正日益成为安全性、韧性和创新的实际需要。Beam 是 Reflection AI 在前沿层面为这一命题发声的第一步棋。

## 📝 Blog: Anthropic Engineering — How We Contain Claude Across Products

**Source:** https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/how-we-contain-claude

Twelve months ago, granting Claude enough access to take down an internal Anthropic service would have been unthinkable. Today it's routine — and Anthropic developers are more productive for it.
十二个月前，授予 Claude 足以关闭 Anthropic 内部服务的访问权限，是难以想象的。如今这已成为常规——Anthropic 的开发者因此工作效率更高。

The core engineering challenge: as agents become capable of doing work that once required a team, the cost of not deploying grows large — so the question becomes how to cap the "blast radius" of a failure rather than avoiding deployment entirely.
核心工程挑战：随着 Agent 能够完成原本需要一个团队才能完成的工作，不部署的代价变得越来越大——因此问题变成了如何限制故障的"爆炸半径"，而非完全回避部署。

Key insight: human-in-the-loop supervision has an approval fatigue problem — Anthropic telemetry showed users approved 93% of permission prompts, with attention declining over time. Claude Code auto-mode was built to reduce this fatigue by automating safer approvals.
关键洞察：人在回路监督存在"审批疲劳"问题——Anthropic 遥测数据显示，用户批准了 93% 的权限提示，且随时间推移注意力不断下降。Claude Code 自动模式正是为了通过自动化更安全的批准来减少这种疲劳而构建的。

Source: https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/how-we-contain-claude

## 📝 Blog: Anthropic Engineering — An Update on Recent Claude Code Quality Reports

**Source:** https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/april-23-postmortem

Anthropic traced widely-reported quality degradation in Claude Code to three separate changes: (1) a reasoning effort downgrade from high to medium on March 4, later reverted; (2) a bug that caused session context to be cleared every turn after an hour of inactivity; (3) a verbosity-reduction instruction added April 16 that, combined with other prompt changes, hurt coding quality.
Anthropic 将 Claude Code 广泛报告的质量下降追溯到三个独立变更：(1) 3 月 4 日将推理努力程度从高调低至中，后来已撤回；(2) 一个 Bug 导致会话上下文在空闲一小时后每轮都被清除；(3) 4 月 16 日添加的减少冗余指令，与其他提示词变更叠加，损害了编码质量。

Because each change affected different traffic slices on different schedules, the aggregate looked like broad, inconsistent degradation — making it hard to diagnose quickly.
由于每次变更影响的流量切片不同、时间表不同，叠加效果看起来像是广泛的、不一致的质量下降——这使得快速诊断变得困难。

All three issues were resolved as of April 20 (v2.1.116). Anthropic committed to better canary testing, staged rollouts, and faster rollback procedures going forward.
三个问题均已在 4 月 20 日（v2.1.116）解决。Anthropic 承诺未来将进行更好的金丝雀测试、分阶段发布和更快的回滚流程。

Source: https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/april-23-postmortem
