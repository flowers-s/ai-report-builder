Aaron Levie (@levie)

Aaron Levie, CEO of Box, delivered one of the week's sharpest macro takes on the AI market shift.
Box CEO Aaron Levie 就 AI 市场转型发表了本周最犀利的宏观见解之一。

*"What's happened is that we went from AI chat tools that were relatively cheap and had small context windows, to AI agents that have giant context windows, the ability to keep track of longer running work, and models that cost an order of magnitude more on inference because they're that much better."*
*"我们经历了从相对廉价、上下文窗口有限的 AI 聊天工具，到拥有超大上下文窗口、能够追踪长期任务、推理成本高出一个数量级的 AI 智能体的转变——因为它们确实好那么多。"*

He argues AI cost stratification is widening — not converging — with frontier use cases like coding, finance, and science demanding premium inference, while simpler tasks route to cheaper capable models.
他认为 AI 成本分层正在扩大而非收窄——编程、金融、科学等前沿场景需要高溢价推理，而更简单的任务则流向更经济实用的模型。

Enterprises will need new finance teams and technology solutions to price-optimize across this landscape, and the platforms that enable this will win.
企业将需要新的财务团队和技术方案来在这一格局中优化成本，能够赋能这一点的平台将胜出。

🎙️ PODCAST: The MAD Podcast with Matt Turck

**OpenAI's Yann Dubois: Why AI Progress Suddenly Feels Real**

This episode features Yann Dubois, who co-leads the Post-Training Frontiers team at OpenAI, in conversation with Matt Turck.
本期节目邀请了 OpenAI 后训练前沿团队联合负责人 Yann Dubois 与 Matt Turck 展开对话。

The central thesis: AI progress has crossed an inflection point — not just in benchmarks, but in the emotional and practical experience of builders shipping real products.
核心论点：AI 进步已越过拐点——不仅体现在基准测试上，也体现在构建者发布真实产品的情感和实践体验上。

Key topics include how OpenAI structures its vertical and horizontal teams, the mechanics of test-time compute scaling, why reinforcement learning is proving so powerful (and its limits), and why startups should focus relentlessly on the last mile of AI rather than competing with foundation models.
关键话题包括 OpenAI 如何组织垂直与水平团队、测试时计算扩展的机制、强化学习为何如此强大（及其局限），以及为何初创公司应坚定专注于 AI 的最后一英里，而非与基础模型竞争。

Dubois also addresses the evaluation bottleneck, continuous learning, and whether foundation models will eventually absorb the agent harness layer.
Dubois 还探讨了评估瓶颈、持续学习，以及基础模型是否最终会吞并智能体框架层。
