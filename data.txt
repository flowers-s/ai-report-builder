**The MAD Podcast with Matt Turck**
When AI Improves Itself | Richard Socher (Recursive)

## 一、深度总结

**核心论点**
Richard Socher 以 $6.5 亿美元融资创立 Recursive，核心命题是：AI 将驱动科学研究进入递归式自我改进的循环——AI 不仅辅助科学，更将生成新的科学假设、设计实验、验证结果，并以此训练出更强的下一代 AI。
Richard Socher founded Recursive with $650M raised, built on the central thesis that AI will drive science into a recursive self-improvement loop — AI that not only assists science but generates hypotheses, designs experiments, validates results, and uses that to train even better AI.

**关键洞察**

- 科学进步正在放缓，不是因为人变懒了，而是知识已从"一个知识体系"碎裂为"3.4 万个期刊构成的迷宫"，跨领域整合几乎不可能。
  Scientific progress is slowing not because researchers are less capable, but because the body of knowledge has shattered from a unified whole into a labyrinth of 34,000 journals, making cross-domain synthesis nearly impossible.

- Socher 认为"任何可以被模拟的，AI 都将解决"——这是 RSI（递归自我改进）的底层逻辑：模拟能力一旦到位，AI 改进 AI 的飞轮就会启动。
  Socher argues "anything you can simulate, AI will solve" — this is the foundational logic of RSI: once simulation capability is in place, the flywheel of AI improving AI begins to spin.

- 当前 AI 距离智能上限还有巨大距离。学术系统的风险规避文化（论文被拒）抑制了基础创新，而 AI 研究恰恰需要大量高风险实验——Socher 本人早年 NLP 论文屡遭拒稿的经历印证了这一点。
  Current AI is still far from the upper bounds of intelligence. The risk-averse culture of academia suppresses fundamental innovation — Socher's own early NLP papers were repeatedly rejected, a pattern that validates why AI research needs to escape institutional constraints.

- 他的新书《The Eureka Machine》主张：AI + RSI 将是解锁下一代科学突破（癌症、新材料、能源）的关键，而不只是效率工具。
  His new book *The Eureka Machine* argues that AI + RSI will be the key to unlocking the next generation of scientific breakthroughs — cancer, new materials, energy — not merely an efficiency tool.

- 生物学是 RSI 最有潜力的战场：领域够深、实验可模拟、数据量巨大，但当前跨领域沟通成本极高（行话壁垒），AI 正好可以成为"翻译层"。
  Biology is the most promising frontier for RSI: deep enough, simulatable, data-rich — yet currently plagued by jargon barriers that make cross-domain communication nearly impossible, exactly where AI can serve as a translation layer.

**最值得关注的信号**
RSI 不是遥远的科幻概念，而是 Recursive 正在用真实资本落地的工程命题。$6.5 亿的融资规模意味着这一赛道已进入主流资本视野；Socher 的科研背景（Stanford NLP、Salesforce Einstein）使这一押注比纯商业叙事更具可信度。
RSI is not a distant science-fiction concept — it's an engineering thesis being capitalized at $650M right now. Socher's research pedigree (Stanford NLP, Salesforce Einstein) lends this bet considerably more credibility than a purely commercial narrative.
