## 一、深度总结

**核心论点**

OpenAI 联合创始人兼总裁 Greg Brockman 在这期节目中系统阐述了 AI 在数学突破、药物研发和网络安全三大领域的变革性影响，并对"人类始终应该掌握主导权"这一 OpenAI 核心价值观做了深入诠释。

**主要内容**

*数学突破与科学加速*

Greg 认为，本周 OpenAI 在 Navier-Stokes 等数学难题上的突破，标志着 AI 能够产生"真正新知识"的历史节点已经到来。他强调，重要的不只是机器给出了答案，而是这些证明过程中蕴含的洞察——数学社区正在从约 400 道已被 AI 解决的重要开放问题中挖掘出令人惊喜的新思路。*"It is definitively, yes, possible"* — 他对 AI 能产生新知识这件事给出了明确肯定。

*药物研发革命*

Greg 将药物发现列为 AI 最重要的应用场景。他指出，人类在生物学领域本来就"非常糟糕"，而神经网络没有理由在自然语言上比生物语言表现更好，因此 AI 在生物领域的突破同样值得期待。他特别提到，一些瓶颈并非纯粹的生物理论问题——例如监管申报文件的撰写，AI 可以大幅加速。药物研发的 15 年周期和 15 亿美元成本，有望通过 AI 在各个环节被系统性压缩。

*网络安全的物理学变革*

Greg 提出了"防御者窗口"的概念：当前是顶尖 AI 能力尚未广泛扩散、但攻击者能力正在快速提升的关键期，防御者必须抓住这个窗口。他做出了一个大胆断言：*"cyber spend, security spend should actually vastly outstrip software spend"* — 因为软件的门槛成本正在趋向于零，真正的价值在于软件的可靠性与安全性。OpenAI 内部已经将 25 名生产工程师的项目暂停，专门用于用最新模型扫描自身基础设施的安全漏洞。

*DOTS 与无界面 AI 的未来*

Greg 介绍了 OpenAI 的新产品 DOTS——一个始终在线的主动式 AI agent，可跨越邮件、Slack、日历等系统整合信息，并主动提醒用户未完成的事项。他认为，最好的界面就是没有界面：AI 应该根据用户需求动态生成所需的 UI，而不是让用户学习固定的菜单。

*消费者与企业的融合*

Greg 预测消费者助手与企业助手的边界将逐渐模糊，主要差异将集中在计算资源的投入量上。OpenAI 在 ChatGPT 上为 12 亿用户提供免费服务，同时也拥有领先的企业编程业务，这两者的统一是其核心战略。

**值得思考的问题**

Greg 关于"安全支出应该超过软件支出"的判断，对企业 CIO/CISO 是一个强烈信号。他对 AI 自我改进和人类监督共存的思考——将人类的角色比喻为董事会成员或 CEO——也值得深入品味。

## 二、English Summary

In this episode of No Priors, hosts Regina and Elad sit down with Greg Brockman, co-founder and President of OpenAI, fresh off a week of major announcements including GPT-6 and a historic math breakthrough.

**Key takeaways:**

- **Math & new knowledge**: OpenAI solved ~400 important open problems including Navier-Stokes. Greg argues AI is now definitively producing novel knowledge, not just retrieving it. The mathematical community is mining these proofs for unexpected insights.

- **Drug discovery**: Greg calls this "the #1 application of AI." He argues neural nets have no intrinsic reason to be better at language than at biology — so the same capabilities should translate. The 15-year/$1.5B drug development cycle can be compressed at multiple stages, including regulatory filings, which AI can dramatically accelerate.

- **Cybersecurity physics**: Greg introduces the "defender's window" — a critical period before frontier AI capabilities diffuse to attackers. His bold claim: *security spend should vastly outstrip software spend*, because software is becoming nearly free while trust and reliability remain scarce. OpenAI internally froze 25 engineers' projects to security-harden their infrastructure using their own frontier models.

- **DOTS**: OpenAI's always-on proactive agent that monitors Greg's inbox, calendar, and Slack, flags missed commitments, and surfaces conflicts. He has "one little owl" covering his entire work life.

- **No-UI future**: Greg believes the best UI is no UI — AI should dynamically generate exactly what you need, when you need it. The ChatGPT text box was the starting point; the destination is an AI you just talk to.

- **Human agency**: A core OpenAI value. Even as models surpass humans in capability, humans should remain "in the driver's seat." Greg uses the Steve Jobs iMac handle metaphor — users should always feel they can control the machine.
