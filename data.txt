【Aaron Levie @levie】

Aaron Levie identified "token budgeting" as the emerging enterprise challenge nobody is talking about yet.
Aaron Levie 指出"Token 预算管理"正在成为企业面临的新挑战，但目前几乎无人讨论。

As agents run longer tasks and consume vastly more compute, enterprises will need to allocate tokens across teams the same way they allocate headcount, marketing spend, or travel budgets — with full visibility and controls.
随着智能体承担更长时程的任务并消耗更多算力，企业需要像管理人力成本、营销预算或差旅费用一样，跨团队分配 Token——并配备完整的可见性与管控机制。

*"We'll need all new software just to solve this problem, and it's probably an opportunity for startups in its own right."*
*"我们需要全新的软件来解决这个问题，这本身可能就是一个创业机会。"*

He predicts agentic spend will eventually move out of the IT budget entirely and land in departmental P&Ls — a new era of enterprise resource planning.
他预测，智能体支出最终将完全脱离 IT 预算，进入各部门的损益核算——一个企业资源规划的全新时代由此开启。

Source: https://x.com/levie/status/2052903105256382679

【Matt Turck @mattturck】

Matt Turck is rethinking AI agent pricing models and finds the answer less obvious than most assume.
Matt Turck 正在重新审视 AI 智能体的定价模式，并发现答案远比大多数人预想的更复杂。

*"That sounds oddly seat-like? just not human-seat-like."*
*"听起来奇怪地像按席位收费？只是席位不再是人类的席位。"*
