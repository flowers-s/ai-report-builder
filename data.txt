Nan Yu (@thenanyu)

Nan Yu argues that "bragging about running 10 Claude Code tabs is just theater" — real productivity isn't about tab count. He also makes a sharp point: the "real-time strategy game" model of managing AI agents is a dead end, since AI already beats 99%+ of human players and outmicros them by a huge margin. Managing agents like RTS units fundamentally misunderstands the scale advantage AI brings.
Nan Yu 直言，炫耀同时开10个 Claude Code 标签页不过是表演，真正的生产力不在于标签数量。他还犀利指出：把管理AI智能体类比为即时战略游戏操作的思路已经走进死胡同——AI本就以压倒性优势超越人类玩家，用"微操"思维管理智能体根本误解了AI的规模优势。

Garry Tan (@garrytan)

Garry Tan shares two powerful connected observations. First, from Osaka: Japan ran the experiment on hitting a ceiling — 30 years of zero growth produced the world's best trains, service, and craft. When you can't compete on "more," you compete on "better." Now we can have better AND more simultaneously. Second, a sweeping insight: the real constraint on human wealth was never resources — it was good ideas and the leverage to act on them. AI just deleted the leverage constraint for everyone. Now only the ideas remain. Go have them, then build.
Garry Tan 在大阪分享了两个相互呼应的洞察。一是从日本的经验看：30年零增长反而造就了世界上最好的铁路、服务和工艺——当无法在"量"上竞争时，就在"质"上竞争。而现在我们可以同时实现"更多"与"更好"。二是更宏观的判断：人类财富真正的约束从来不是资源，而是好想法和将其付诸实践的杠杆。AI刚刚为所有人消除了杠杆约束，剩下的只有想法——去想，然后去创造。
