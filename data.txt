### Aaron Levie (levie on X)

Aaron Levie 是 Box 的 CEO，近期发表了对 AI agent 采用现状的深度分析。

He observes that AI agent adoption is currently very bimodal: coding and coding-adjacent tasks have taken off, while everything else is still far behind.
他观察到，AI agent 的采用目前呈现明显的两极分化：编程及相关任务已经起飞，而其他几乎所有领域依然远远落后。

Even within coding, there's a wide continuum — a small percentage of developers are deploying background agents working on multiple projects in parallel, while most are still working with agents one-on-one.
即便在编程领域内，情况也大相径庭——少数开发者已经在并行部署后台 agent 处理多个项目，而大多数人仍在和 agent 一对一地配合工作。

For the rest of knowledge work, real agentic adoption is still very early, because most workflows still need to be reengineered: data must be wired up in new ways, accountability and liability practices must be rethought, and governance, compliance, and security paradigms all need to change.
对于其余的知识工作而言，真正的 agent 化采用仍处于非常早期的阶段，因为大多数工作流还需要被重新设计：数据需要以新方式连接，问责与责任机制需要重新思考，治理、合规和安全范式也都需要改变。

*"We're still so unbelievably early in what this is going to look like outside of a few categories of work right now. This is why you can basically expect 100X more agent adoption from what we've seen so far."*
"在少数几个已经起步的领域之外，我们对未来的样貌还处于难以置信的早期阶段。这就是为什么你基本上可以预期未来 agent 的采用规模将是我们目前所见的 100 倍。"

### Ryo Lu (ryolu_ on X)

Ryo Lu 是一位独立创作者与产品思考者，近期发表了一篇关于"均值收敛"的深度长文。

He argues that when everyone looks at everyone else to decide what to make, we eventually stop making anything of our own.
他认为，当所有人都在互相观望、模仿来决定做什么时，我们最终会停止创作真正属于自己的东西。

We copy what works, borrow the language, the aesthetic, the features — calling it "learning from the market" or "following best practices" — until everything feels familiar, polished, interchangeable, but nothing alive.
我们复制有效的东西，借用别人的语言、美学和功能——美其名曰"学习市场"或"遵循最佳实践"——直到一切都变得熟悉、精致、可互换，却毫无生气。

He points out that AI accelerates this loop: it can draw from more references than we could see in a lifetime, but access to every perspective isn't the same as having one.
他指出，AI 加速了这个循环：它能调用的参考资料比我们一生所能看到的还多，但获取所有视角并不等于拥有自己的视角。

His remedy: approach making things more like an artist — develop a perspective through the work itself, through making and noticing and failing and returning.
他的解药：像艺术家一样去创作——在实际的制作过程中，通过不断创作、观察、失败和回归，来发展出属于自己的视角。

*"What is the point of everyone being able to make something, if we all end up making the same thing?"*
"如果我们最终做出的都是同一件东西，那让每个人都能创作的意义究竟在哪里？"

### The MAD Podcast with Matt Turck
**Who Feeds the GPUs? Inside AI's Hidden $30B Layer | Renen Hallak, VAST Data**

This episode explores the crucial but often overlooked data infrastructure layer that sits between AI models and the GPUs that power them.
本期节目深入探讨了一个至关重要却常被忽视的数据基础设施层——它位于 AI 模型与驱动它们的 GPU 之间。

Host Matt Turck sits down with Renen Hallak, founder and CEO of VAST Data, a company valued at $30 billion that powers top AI players including xAI and many of the world's biggest AI cloud providers.
主持人 Matt Turck 与 VAST Data 的创始人兼 CEO Renen Hallak 对谈，这家公司估值 300 亿美元，为包括 xAI 在内的顶级 AI 玩家以及全球最大的 AI 云服务商提供支持。

**The scale of demand is staggering.**
需求规模令人震惊。

One AI cloud customer initially told VAST they would need about 500 petabytes over the next three years — then came back last week saying they now need an extra two exabytes on top of that.
一位 AI 云客户最初告诉 VAST，他们未来三年大约需要 500 PB 的存储——然后上周回来说，还需要额外再加 2 EB。

Hallak argues that the pace of change in the next ten years will exceed what happened in the last thousand years, driven by the insatiable appetite of AI training and inference workloads for data.
Hallak 认为，由 AI 训练和推理对数据的无尽需求所驱动，未来十年的变化速度将超过过去千年。

**VAST Data's architectural bet is that the traditional separation between storage tiers (hot, warm, cold) is becoming obsolete.**
VAST Data 的架构赌注是：存储层级（热、温、冷）之间的传统分离正在变得过时。

Instead, they built a unified storage platform that treats all data as equally accessible, which is critical for AI workloads where models need to randomly access massive datasets at high speed.
他们构建了一个统一的存储平台，将所有数据视为同等可访问的，这对 AI 工作负载至关重要——模型需要高速随机访问海量数据集。

**The company's decision to stay private longer, rather than rushing to IPO, reflects a deliberate strategy to compound value before going public.**
公司决定继续保持私有状态而非急于 IPO，这体现了一种深思熟虑的策略：在上市之前继续复利增长价值。

Hallak explains that when you are growing fast and have strong economics, the math often favors waiting.
Hallak 解释说，当你快速增长且具备强劲的财务表现时，数学上往往更倾向于等待。

**A key theme is the convergence of storage and compute.**
一个关键主题是存储与计算的融合。

Hallak sees a future where data doesn't move to the compute — instead, some computation happens directly where the data lives, dramatically reducing the latency and bandwidth costs that are currently bottlenecks in large-scale AI pipelines.
Hallak 展望了一个数据不再移动到计算侧的未来——部分计算将直接在数据所在位置发生，从而大幅降低目前大规模 AI pipeline 中瓶颈性的延迟和带宽成本。

**For anyone building or investing in AI infrastructure, this is a must-listen on what happens beneath the model layer.**
