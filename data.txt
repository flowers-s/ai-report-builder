### AI & I by Every — "Why Every Traded Personal Agents for One Company Agent"

Every's Dan Shipper and team made a deliberate bet: instead of each person having their own personal AI agent, build one shared company agent ("Dot") that knows the whole organization.
Every 的 Dan Shipper 和团队做出了一个刻意的决定：不是每个人拥有自己的个人 AI agent，而是构建一个了解整个组织的共享公司 agent（"Dot"）。

OpenAI's Dots are persistent, proactive agents that were launched at Dev Day.
OpenAI 的 Dots 是在 Dev Day 发布的持久化主动式 agent。

After two weeks of live usage across a 30-person team, several clear use cases emerged: scanning school emails for parents, catching Slack access requests, working hands-free on mobile during launches.
在一支 30 人团队两周的实际使用后，几个清晰的使用场景浮现：为家长扫描学校邮件、捕获 Slack 权限请求、在发布期间免手动在手机上处理工作。

The key insight: a company-level agent develops institutional memory that a personal agent can't replicate.
核心洞察：公司级 agent 能形成个人 agent 无法复制的组织记忆。

When Dot knows about ongoing projects, team members, Slack dynamics, and company context, it becomes dramatically more useful than any single person's agent.
当 Dot 了解正在进行的项目、团队成员、Slack 动态和公司背景后，它的价值远超任何单个人的 agent。

The tradeoff: privacy and trust — employees need to feel comfortable with a shared agent that can see everything.
权衡之处：隐私与信任——员工需要对一个能看到一切的共享 agent 感到放心。

Every's approach: radical transparency about what Dot knows and does.
Every 的做法：对 Dot 的所知和所做保持彻底的透明度。

The forward-looking signal: the "company agent" paradigm may spread well beyond Every — any org that wants to move fast at AI scale will need shared institutional memory, not just individual copilots.
前瞻性信号："公司 agent"范式可能远不止于 Every——任何希望以 AI 规模快速前进的组织，都将需要共享的组织记忆，而不仅仅是个人副驾驶。
