【Claude Blog】Building intelligent apps for Apple platforms with Claude in the Foundation Models framework
Anthropic released a Swift package enabling Apple developers to use Claude via Apple's Foundation Models framework. Claude handles complex multi-step reasoning, code generation, and web search as an extension of on-device Apple Intelligence workflows. Works with iOS 27, iPadOS 27, macOS 27, visionOS 27, and watchOS 27 — just add the package and sign in with an Anthropic API key.
Anthropic 发布了一个 Swift 包，让 Apple 开发者能够通过 Apple 的 Foundation Models 框架调用 Claude。Claude 作为设备端 Apple Intelligence 工作流的扩展，负责处理复杂的多步骤推理、代码生成和网页搜索任务。支持 iOS 27、iPadOS 27、macOS 27、visionOS 27 和 watchOS 27——只需添加该包并使用 Anthropic API 密钥登录即可。
🔗 https://claude.com/blog/claude-for-foundation-models

He makes the case that AI's current explosion is the culmination of 50 years of slow, steady groundwork.
Kevin Kelly 回顾了数十年来的技术预测历程——从1987年的 VR 到今天的 LLM——认为变革性技术往往比预期花费更长时间，但一旦"时机成熟"便会以迅雷之势到来。他的论点是：当前 AI 的爆发，是50年来缓慢而稳定的基础积累的结果。
