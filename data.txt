No Priors — "Powering AI-Driven Drug Discovery with President of Isomorphic Labs Max Jaderberg"

Max Jaderberg, President of Isomorphic Labs (backed by Alphabet), joins No Priors to discuss how AI is transforming drug discovery.
Isomorphic Labs（Alphabet 旗下）总裁 Max Jaderberg 做客 No Priors，探讨 AI 如何变革药物研发。

The core thesis: drug discovery has always been an empirical, trial-and-error process — and AI's biggest opportunity is replacing real-world iteration with in-silico simulation at experimental accuracy.
核心论点：药物研发始终是一个依赖经验的反复试错过程——而 AI 最大的机会，是用达到实验精度的计算机模拟取代真实世界的迭代。

The key insight driving Isomorphic: AlphaFold was the first proof that deep learning can generalize meaningfully in biological space.
推动 Isomorphic 创立的核心洞察：AlphaFold 是深度学习能够在生物学空间进行有意义泛化的第一个概念验证。

*"I'd be really disappointed if we fast forward even five years' time and the predictive understanding of disease biology has not step changed in a massive way."*
「如果五年后，我们对疾病生物学的预测性理解没有发生质的飞跃，我会非常失望。」

Drugs fail mainly in clinical trials — not because of bad science, but because we can't see toxicity and off-target effects until drugs are tested in real patients.
药物失败主要发生在临床试验阶段——不是因为科学不好，而是在药物真正用于患者之前，我们无法预见毒性和脱靶效应。

The goal is to do far more design and validation on a computer, so when you do go into the real world, the probability of success is dramatically higher.
目标是在计算机上完成更多的设计和验证，这样在真正进入现实世界时，成功概率会大幅提升。

Isomorphic builds AI drug design models entirely in-house and uses them to run both fully-owned internal drug programs and collaborative programs with pharma partners.
Isomorphic 完全自研 AI 药物设计模型，并将其用于自主内部药物项目和与制药公司的合作项目。

Internal focus areas: oncology, immunology, and inflammation — but the models are disease-agnostic and modality-agnostic.
内部重点领域：肿瘤学、免疫学和炎症——但模型本身不针对特定疾病或特定药物形式。

On team composition: roughly one domain expert per five AI scientists/engineers — similar to the ratios seen in early ML-driven biotech.
团队构成：大约每五名 AI 科学家/工程师配一名领域专家——与早期 ML 驱动的生物技术公司的比例相近。
