### Latent Space: "What Frontier Labs Buy: A Data Deep Dive with Handshake AI — Garrett Lord & Team"

**核心论点 / Core Argument**

The AI training data market is undergoing rapid expansion — from $1 billion three years ago to $10 billion today, and potentially $100 billion within three years.
AI 训练数据市场正在经历快速扩张——三年前规模10亿美元，如今已达100亿，三年后极有可能突破1000亿。

Frontier labs are not buying data for traditional fine-tuning; they are buying specialized, human-verified data for RLHF, evaluation, and edge-case coverage that synthetic data cannot reliably produce.
前沿实验室购买数据并非用于传统微调，而是为了 RLHF、评估和边缘案例覆盖——这些场景是合成数据无法可靠生成的。

**关键洞察 / Key Insights**

- The "AI won't cure cancer" critique often misunderstands the actual failure mode: a 95% accurate output is 100% wrong in medical contexts because fixing a flawed output takes as long as building from scratch.
  "AI 不会治愈癌症"的批评往往误解了真正的失败模式：在医疗场景中，95% 准确的输出等于100%错误，因为修复一个有缺陷的输出和从头构建同样耗时。

- Human-generated data retains a premium specifically for tasks requiring nuanced judgment, edge-case coverage, and verifiable correctness — areas where synthetic data is structurally weak.
  人工生成数据在需要细微判断、边缘案例覆盖和可验证正确性的任务中仍然具有溢价——这些正是合成数据结构性薄弱的领域。

- The bottleneck is not data volume but data quality and labeler expertise; frontier labs are increasingly paying for domain experts, not generic crowdworkers.
  瓶颈不在于数据量，而在于数据质量和标注者的专业水平；前沿实验室越来越多地付费购买领域专家的标注，而非通用众包工人。

- Handshake AI is positioning itself as a marketplace that matches high-skill domain experts with the specific annotation and evaluation tasks frontier labs need most.
  Handshake AI 正将自身定位为一个市场，将高技能领域专家与前沿实验室最需要的特定标注和评估任务进行匹配。

**最值得关注的信号 / Key Signal**

The transition from "$1B to $100B in 6 years" is not a prediction — it is the thesis underpinning how frontier labs are staffing and budgeting today.
"6年内从10亿到1000亿"不是预测——这是前沿实验室当前人员配置和预算决策背后的核心逻辑。

Watch for increasing differentiation between commodity annotation providers and high-expertise data partners; the latter will capture disproportionate value.
关注商品化标注服务商与高专业水平数据合作伙伴之间日益扩大的分化；后者将获取不成比例的市场价值。
