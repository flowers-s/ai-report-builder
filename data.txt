The MAD Podcast with Matt Turck — How to Build Long-Horizon AI Agents — Mitch Troyanovsky, Basis

This is a must-listen reference episode for anyone building autonomous AI agents outside of coding.
这是一期面向所有在编程领域之外构建自主 AI Agent 的人的必听参考播客。

Mitch Troyanovsky, co-founder of Basis, shares how his team builds agents that run for hours or days to complete complex tasks like end-to-end tax returns.
Basis 联合创始人 Mitch Troyanovsky 分享了他的团队如何构建能够运行数小时乃至数天、完成端到端报税等复杂任务的 Agent。

**核心洞察 / Key Insights:**

LLMs have very large working memories but by default no short-term or long-term memory — long-horizon agents must be engineered to compensate.
LLM 拥有极大的工作记忆，但默认没有短期或长期记忆——长时域 Agent 必须通过工程手段来弥补这一缺陷。

The Memento analogy: like the amnesiac protagonist who must write notes for his future self, agents must be engineered to manage their own state across inference steps.
《记忆碎片》类比：就像那位健忘症主人公必须为未来的自己写下便条，Agent 必须在推理步骤之间管理自身状态。

Behavior specs (markdown files defining how an agent should behave) are as much a product decision as a technical one — subjective, requiring collaboration between domain experts and ML researchers.
行为规范（定义 Agent 应如何行为的 Markdown 文件）既是产品决策也是技术决策——具有主观性，需要领域专家与 ML 研究员协作完成。

Outcome-based evals alone are insufficient: passing 100 evals doesn't mean the process was correct, just as passing unit tests doesn't mean the architecture is sound.
仅凭结果导向的评估是不够的：通过 100 项评估并不意味着过程是正确的，正如通过单元测试并不意味着架构是合理的。

*"Even if you got it right 100 out of 100 times, if a person is just getting it right because they're going to Wikipedia, the accounting firm wouldn't hire them, and so they shouldn't hire us either."*
*"即使你 100 次都答对了，如果一个人只是靠查维基百科得到正确答案，会计事务所不会雇用他，所以也不应该雇用我们。"*

The real moat is not technology but business position — getting deeply embedded before AGI arrives.
真正的护城河不是技术，而是商业地位——在 AGI 到来之前深度嵌入市场。

*"You need that maybe at the Olympiad, like math competitions, but not like doing work in the real economy."* (on Move 37-style breakthroughs)
*"你也许在奥林匹克竞赛那样的场合需要 Move 37，但在现实经济中做事不需要。"*（关于 Move 37 式的突破）
