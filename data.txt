### The MAD Podcast with Matt Turck — "Stripe's AI Chief: How AI Agents Will Buy, Sell, and Pay"

This episode features Emily Sands, Head of Data and AI at Stripe, in conversation with host Matt Turck.
本期节目由 Stripe 数据与 AI 负责人 Emily Sands 与主持人 Matt Turck 对谈。

The central theme: the economic infrastructure for AI agents — how agents will buy, sell, and transact on behalf of users and businesses.
核心主题：AI Agent 的经济基础设施——Agent 将如何代表用户和企业进行购买、销售与交易。

**Token theft — the most under-discussed topic in AI:**
**Token 盗窃——AI 领域最被忽视的话题：**

Emily revealed a shocking statistic: more than 1 in 6 signups at AI companies are fraudulent, designed purely to steal compute tokens.
Emily 披露了一个令人震惊的数据：AI 公司超过六分之一的新用户注册是欺诈性的，目的仅仅是盗取算力 token。

*"Fraudsters have figured out that in AI, you actually don't really need to steal money or credentials. You can just steal tokens."*
*"欺诈者已经发现，在 AI 领域，根本不需要偷钱或凭证，只需要偷 token 就够了。"*

She calls this the "dine and dash, but for tokens."
她将此称为"餐厅逃单，但针对的是 token"。

**Agentic commerce — agents as buyers and sellers:**
**Agent 经济——Agent 作为买方和卖方：**

The conversation moved from "Emily permissioning an agent to buy on her behalf" to a more ambitious vision: Emily has an agent tasked with running a business — including buying, selling, and making profits.
话题从"Emily 授权 Agent 代为购买"延伸到更宏大的愿景：Emily 有一个 Agent，其任务是运营一家业务——包括采购、销售和创造利润。

When asked if people are comfortable letting agents buy things for them, the common concerns are: overspending, buying the wrong thing, and whether they can stop it.
当被问及人们是否愿意让 Agent 代为购物时，最常见的顾虑是：超支、买错东西，以及是否能及时叫停。

*"Those are actually all legitimate concerns."*
*"这些顾虑实际上都是合理的。"*

**Per-seat pricing is breaking:**
**按席位定价模式正在崩溃：**

The rise of AI agents is straining traditional per-seat SaaS pricing models — as a single agent can perform the work of many seats, the unit economics no longer hold.
AI Agent 的兴起正在冲击传统的按席位 SaaS 定价模式——一个 Agent 可以完成多个"席位"的工作，单位经济学逻辑已不再成立。

New pricing models will need to emerge that better reflect the value created by agents rather than the number of human users.
新的定价模式需要出现，更好地反映 Agent 创造的价值，而非人类用户的数量。

**The 12-month vision:**
**未来 12 个月的展望：**

Emily's goal: in twelve months, be talking about a world where agents are not just buying on your behalf, but running entire businesses — with buying, selling, and profit generation all automated.
