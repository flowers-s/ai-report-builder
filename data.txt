## Madhu Guru (@realmadhuguru)

Madhu delivers a sharp framework for PMs: if you're building AI products, you need to know the model frontier better than anyone else for your specific use cases. That means understanding what models at each size can and can't do, knowing the failure modes, having workarounds, and predicting the trajectory.
马杜为产品经理提出了一套犀利的方法论：如果你在做 AI 产品，你必须比任何人都更了解适用于你特定场景的模型前沿。这意味着你需要清楚不同规模模型的能力边界、失败模式、变通方法以及演进轨迹。

- "If you're a PM, there is immense alpha in understanding the model frontier for your specific product and use cases."
  "作为产品经理，深度理解适合你产品的模型前沿，是巨大的竞争优势所在。"
- Key questions every AI PM should answer: What can each model size do today? Where does it fail? What workarounds exist? Where is the trajectory heading?
  每位 AI PM 都应能回答：各规模模型今天能做什么？在哪里失败？有哪些变通方案？未来走向如何？

## Aaron Levie (@levie)

Aaron sees a new wave of opportunity: as open-weights models improve and post-training infrastructure matures, companies with large proprietary datasets no longer have to just license their data — they can **train their own models**. Meanwhile, AI security is heating up; frontier models still lead in cyber, but open models are closing the gap fast.
亚伦·利维看到了新一波机遇：随着开源模型持续进步、后训练基础设施日趋成熟，拥有大量专有数据的公司不再只能被动出售数据授权——他们完全可以**训练自己的模型**。与此同时，AI 安全正在升温；前沿模型在网络安全领域仍处领先，但开源模型追赶速度惊人。

- "Now that base open weights models are getting far better… companies that have large amounts of data [can] train their own models." — The data moat just got a new use.
  "随着基础开源模型持续进步……拥有大量数据的公司可以训练自己的模型。" —— 数据护城河有了全新用武之地。

## Dan Shipper (@danshipper)

Dan offers a nuanced take on AI anthropomorphism: it's a tool, not a taboo. When used to help us understand, predict, and use AI better — it's good. When weaponized to sow panic or manipulate moral status — it's bad. He also drops a punchy one-liner: "Pragmatists stay winning in the age of AI."
丹·希珀对 AI 拟人化给出了一个细腻的判断：它是工具，而非禁忌。当它帮助我们更好地理解、预测和使用 AI 时——是好的；当它被用来制造恐慌或操纵道德身份地位时——是有害的。他还抛出一句精辟短语："务实主义者在 AI 时代永远是赢家。"

- "Anthropomorphization of AI is good when it serves to help us use AI better. It's bad when it serves to sow panic, fear, and unrealistic comparisons to human beings."
  "AI 拟人化在帮助我们更好使用 AI 时是好的；在制造恐慌、恐惧以及不切实际的人类比较时是有害的。"
- "Pragmatists stay winning in the age of AI." — No philosophy paralysis here.
  "务实主义者在 AI 时代永远是赢家。" —— 没有哲学式的瘫痪，只有行动。
