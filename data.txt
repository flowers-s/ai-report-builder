### The MAD Podcast with Matt Turck × Matthew Prince (Cloudflare CEO)
**Cloudflare CEO: Bot Takeover, Edge AI & The Hard Decision Every CEO Will Face**

**[EN]** In a landmark episode, Cloudflare CEO Matthew Prince revealed that bot traffic has already surpassed human traffic on the internet in the first half of 2026 — a milestone that arrived 18 months ahead of Cloudflare's own forecast. Prince explained this explosive growth is driven almost entirely by AI agents and LLMs scraping and traversing the web at unprecedented scale.
**[中]** 在这期重磅播客中，Cloudflare CEO Matthew Prince 披露：2026 年上半年，互联网机器人流量已正式超过人类流量——比 Cloudflare 自己的预测提前了整整 18 个月。这一爆炸性增长几乎完全由 AI Agent 和 LLM 抓取、遍历网络所驱动。

*"We actually had bot traffic pass human traffic online in the first half of twenty twenty six."*

**[EN]** Prince painted a stark picture of the internet's future business model crisis: the web has run on advertising for 28 years, but bots don't click on ads. With bot traffic potentially growing 1000x in five years, the fundamental economics of the internet must change — and Cloudflare is positioning itself at the center of that transformation through its AI Gateway, Workers platform, and agent security infrastructure.
**[中]** Prince 描绘了互联网商业模式危机的严峻图景：网络广告模式运行了 28 年，但机器人不点广告。随着机器人流量可能在五年内增长 1000 倍，互联网的根本经济逻辑必须重构——Cloudflare 正通过 AI Gateway、Workers 平台和 Agent 安全基础设施，将自己定位于这场变革的核心。

*"The underlying business model of the internet for the last twenty eight years has remained basically the same... The problem is bots don't click on ads."*
*"Over the next five years, the business model of the Internet is gonna change radically."*

**[EN]** On the workforce side, Prince discussed the "hard decision every CEO will face" — Cloudflare's decision to rightsize its team in the AI era. He argued this is an inevitable exercise nearly every company will undertake in the next 6-12 months as AI dramatically reshapes staffing needs.
**[中]** 在人力资源方面，Prince 讨论了"每位 CEO 都将面临的艰难抉择"——Cloudflare 在 AI 时代对团队规模进行调整。他认为，未来 6-12 个月内几乎每家公司都将经历类似的重组，AI 正从根本上重塑企业的人员配置需求。

*"In the next six to twelve months, almost every company is going to go through some exercise like this where they're gonna cut a bunch of their team."*
