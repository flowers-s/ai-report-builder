**Scaling Managed Agents: Decoupling the brain from the hands**
A key insight from Anthropic Engineering: traditional agent harnesses encode assumptions about what Claude can't do on its own — but that's changing.
Anthropic Engineering 的核心洞察：传统的 agent 框架往往预设了 Claude 自身能力的边界——但这一切正在改变。

The new Managed Agents architecture decouples the reasoning layer ("the brain") from the tool-execution layer ("the hands"), enabling more scalable and reliable long-running agents.
新的 Managed Agents 架构将推理层（"大脑"）与工具执行层（"双手"）解耦，从而实现更可扩展、更可靠的长期运行 agent。

This design allows the orchestration logic to scale independently of execution infrastructure, a fundamental step toward production-grade agentic systems.
这一设计使编排逻辑能够独立于执行基础设施进行扩展，是迈向生产级 agentic 系统的关键一步。
