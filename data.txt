Training Data — Waymo's Dmitri Dolgov: 20 Million Rides and the Road to Full Autonomy
(Published: 2026-05-04)

Waymo co-CEO Dmitri Dolgov joined the Training Data podcast to reflect on 20+ years of building autonomous vehicles, from the 2005 DARPA Challenge to 20 million fully autonomous rides.
Waymo 联合 CEO Dmitri Dolgov 接受 Training Data 播客采访，回顾了他从 2005 年 DARPA 挑战赛到完成 2000 万次完全自动驾驶行程的 20 余年历程。

He described Waymo's Foundation Model as a multimodal world-action-language model powering three pillars: the driver, the simulator, and the critic — built for superhuman precision, not just demos.
他介绍了 Waymo 基础模型——一个多模态世界行动语言模型，支撑三大核心支柱：驾驶员、模拟器和评判器，目标是达到超人水平的精确度，而非仅仅是演示。

On safety: *"Today we're driving more than 4,000,000 miles in full autonomy per week... the Waymo driver is more than 13 times safer than a human driver when it comes to serious injury causing collisions."*
关于安全性：*"我们现在每周完全自动驾驶超过 400 万英里……Waymo 驾驶员在严重伤害事故方面比人类司机安全超过 13 倍。"*

One remarkable emergent capability: Waymo's LiDAR detected a pedestrian's feet moving under a bus, allowing the car to predict and safely avoid the person before they emerged.
一个令人惊叹的涌现能力：Waymo 的激光雷达检测到行人双脚在公交车下方的移动，使车辆在行人出现前就能预判并安全避让。

On scaling: it took 8 years to reach service in 4 cities; earlier this year, Waymo launched 4 cities in a single day. London and Tokyo expansions are planned for this year.
