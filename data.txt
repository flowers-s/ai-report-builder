## 🤖 Thibault Sottiaux @thsottiaux (Codex & ChatGPT @ OpenAI)

OpenAI shipped a major improvement to Codex: reusable, inheritable permission profiles that bind OS-enforced file read/write/deny rules — including `**/*.env` — to per-domain network and Unix socket controls, plus fail-closed admin allowlists.
OpenAI 为 Codex 推出重大改进：可复用、可继承的权限配置文件，将操作系统强制执行的文件读/写/拒绝规则（包括 `**/*.env`）与按域名划分的网络和 Unix 套接字控制绑定，并支持默认拒绝的管理员白名单。

This replaces the previous coarse sandbox modes with a least-privilege-per-task approach.
这取代了以前粗粒度的沙箱模式，实现了按任务最小权限的精细控制。

Codex usage limits were also fully reset after an investigation into faster-than-expected usage consumption, with an extra reset credited to affected users.
在调查使用量消耗速度超预期问题后，Codex 使用限额已完全重置，并向受影响用户额外补偿了一次重置额度。

Source: https://x.com/thsottiaux/status/2071636285807059315
Source: https://x.com/thsottiaux/status/2071740419030053227

## 🎙️ PODCAST: No Priors — Re-engineering the Semiconductor Supply Chain with Intel CEO Lip-Bu Tan

**深度总结 / Deep Summary**

No Priors 本期邀请了传奇投资人、Cadence 前 CEO，现任 Intel CEO Lip-Bu Tan，探讨他重塑英特尔的计划、美国政府作为大股东的影响，以及能否在美国本土制造芯片等核心议题。
This episode of No Priors features Lip-Bu Tan — legendary investor, former CEO of Cadence, and current CEO of Intel — discussing his plan to transform Intel, the US government's role as a major shareholder, and whether chip manufacturing can be revived in the United States.

**核心论点 / Central Argument**

Lip-Bu Tan, at 66, took what many called "the hardest job in the industry" out of patriotism and belief in Intel's importance to the US semiconductor ecosystem.
Lip-Bu Tan 在66岁时接下了许多人所说的"业界最艰难的工作"，出于对英特尔在美国半导体生态系统重要性的信念和爱国情怀。

His strategy: start by strengthening the balance sheet, simplify the product line, listen to customers, and then drive next-generation leadership products.
他的策略：首先巩固资产负债表，简化产品线，倾听客户需求，然后推动下一代领先产品。

The agentic AI wave has created surging demand for inference CPUs — a tailwind Intel is now positioned to capture.
Agentic AI 浪潮创造了对推理 CPU 的爆炸性需求——英特尔现在正处于捕捉这一红利的有利位置。

Jensen Huang's $5B investment in Intel, now grown to $25B, is a landmark vote of confidence.
Jensen Huang 对英特尔的50亿美元投资，现已增值至250亿美元，是对英特尔的里程碑式信任背书。

**关键洞察 / Key Insights**

Even after being asked to resign by President Trump early one morning over conflict-of-interest concerns, Lip-Bu persisted — he got a meeting, explained himself (born in Malaysia, raised in Singapore, MIT graduate, lifelong US resident), and earned back the chance.
即使有一天清晨被特朗普总统因利益冲突问题要求辞职，Lip-Bu 也坚持了下来——他争取到了会面机会，解释了自己的背景（马来西亚出生，新加坡成长，MIT 毕业，长期居美），并赢回了机会。

His investing philosophy: 9 out of 10 companies change their business plan halfway through — so he always backs entrepreneur teams, not solo founders.
他的投资哲学：十家公司中有九家会在中途改变商业计划——因此他始终支持创业者团队，而非单打独斗的创始人。
