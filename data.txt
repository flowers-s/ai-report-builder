## Dan Shipper (danshipper on X) — CEO at Every

Every CEO Dan Shipper 在 Thesis 大会上分享了他对"自动化时代人类工作价值"的深度思考。
Every CEO Dan Shipper shared a deeply considered take on the value of human work in the age of automation, presented at the Thesis conference.
他描述了自己上周用 AI 一人完成了五个人的工作：分析客户调研数据、为团队制作游戏、搭建 CRM、主动拓客——这在 2020 年需要数据科学家、工程师、市场人员等五名专业人才。
He described completing the work of five people in one week using AI: analyzing a customer survey, building a team game, creating a CRM, and doing outreach — work that in 2020 would have required a data scientist, engineer, marketer, and more.
然而他的核心观点并非"AI 将取代人类"，而是相反——当每个人都能用同一套 AI 时，真正稀缺的是个体视角和责任感。
His core argument is not that AI replaces humans, but the opposite — when everyone has access to the same models, what becomes scarce is individual perspective and a sense of responsibility.
_"Great work is the perspective you bring to the model."_ 每位团队成员从同一份数据中看到了完全不同的关键洞察，而这正是默认 prompt 无法复制的。
"Great work is the perspective you bring to the model." Each team member found completely different key insights from the same dataset — something a default prompt cannot replicate.
他认为我们需要重新定义 AI 评估标准：不仅是"模型能解决哪些共识问题"，更是"模型能多好地从我的视角完成我的工作"。
He argues we need to rethink how we measure AI: not just "how well does it solve consensus problems" but "how well does it do my work from my perspective."

## AI & I by Every — "A New Vision for Human Work After Automation"

Every 旗下播客 AI & I 本周发布了一期重磅内容：Every 联合创始人兼 CEO Dan Shipper 在公司首届大会 V.C.S.S.（Thesis: 2027）的闭幕演讲全文。
Every's podcast AI & I this week released a landmark episode featuring the full closing talk by Every co-founder and CEO Dan Shipper from the company's first-ever conference, V.C.S.S. (Thesis: 2027), held in Brooklyn.

Dan 通过亲身经历提出了一套理解 AI 时代人类价值的新框架，核心区分是"宏观视角"（aggregate）与"微观视角"（intimate）。
Dan proposed a new framework for understanding human value in the AI era, centered on the distinction between the "aggregate" view and the "intimate" view.

从宏观视角来看，OpenAI 的 GDPval 基准显示 AI 在专家工作中击败人类的概率已从 2024 年的 12.4% 升至 2026 年的 83%，这条曲线令人触目惊心。
From the aggregate view, OpenAI's GDPval benchmark shows AI beating human experts at expert tasks 83% of the time in 2026, up from 12.4% in 2024 — a trajectory that looks terrifying for human workers.

但微观视角揭示了被宏观遮蔽的东西。
But the intimate view reveals what the aggregate obscures.

Dan 将同样的客户调研数据交给全团队，每个人都找到了完全不同的关键洞察——这些洞察无法通过默认 prompt 复制，因为每个人对业务某一领域的责任感帮助他们锁定搜索方向，而个人视角帮助他们判断什么值得行动。
Dan gave the same customer survey data to his whole team, and each person found entirely different key insights — none of which could be replicated by a default prompt, because each person's responsibility for a specific area of the business narrowed where they looked, and their perspective helped them decide what was worth acting on.

研究还发现，Claude 在 400 次尝试中有 93% 的概率能预测 Dan 会在 Slack 说什么，但在自己的答案中找到正确那一个的概率却只有 60%。
Research found that Claude can predict what Dan would say in Slack 93% of the time — but only if given 400 attempts; when asked to rank its own answers, the right one lands in the top 10% only about 60% of the time.

这说明：生成可能性很廉价，但排序（即判断什么重要）依然困难。
This illustrates the core insight: generating possibilities is cheap, but ranking them — knowing which ones matter — is still hard.

Dan 的结论是："allocation economy"时代，默认答案是人人都能获得的基准，真正稀缺的是你用自己的视角和责任感推动模型超越基准所产生的价值。
Dan's conclusion: in the allocation economy, the default answer is a baseline everyone can reach; what's truly scarce and valuable is how you use your own perspective and responsibility to push the model past that baseline.

随着 AI 能力提升并普及，它抬高了所有人的下限；而真正的"alpha"来自于你独特的视角，这是 AI 无法自行复制的。
As AI capabilities improve and spread, they raise the floor for everyone; the true "alpha" comes from your unique perspective, which AI cannot replicate on its own.

他以此鼓励在场所有人：我们不是被 AI 取代的对象，而是骑在能力曲线之上、决定去哪里的人。
He closed by encouraging everyone in the room: we are not being replaced by AI — we are the ones riding the capability curve, deciding where it goes.
