The MAD Podcast with Matt Turck
Episode: Cloudflare CEO: The Internet's Business Model Is Dead
URL: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UN47z_opfmo

**核心论点**
The central argument of this episode is that AI agents and bots have crossed a historic threshold: for the first time, bot traffic has exceeded human traffic on the internet.
本期播客的核心论点是：AI 智能体和爬虫越过了一个历史性门槛——互联网上的 bot 流量首次超越了人类流量。

Matthew Prince, CEO of Cloudflare, argues this fundamentally breaks the internet's 28-year-old advertising-based business model, because bots don't click on ads.
Cloudflare CEO Matthew Prince 认为，这从根本上颠覆了互联网延续了 28 年的广告商业模式，因为 bot 不会点广告。

**关键洞察**

- Bot traffic crossed human traffic in early 2026 — much earlier than Cloudflare's internal projections from late 2025, which had estimated it wouldn't happen until end of 2027.
  Bot 流量在 2026 年上半年就超过了人类流量——远早于 Cloudflare 2025 年底预测的 2027 年底。

- In 5 years, bot traffic may be 1,000x human traffic. Prince says he'd take the "over" on that bet, given the exponential growth trajectory.
  5 年后，bot 流量可能是人类流量的 1000 倍。Prince 表示，鉴于当前的指数增长轨迹，他愿意押"超出"这个数字。

- When a human shops for a digital camera, they visit ~5 websites. When an AI agent does the same task, it visits ~5,000. That's the multiplier driving the traffic explosion.
  人类购物时大约访问 5 个网站，AI 智能体执行同样任务时会访问约 5000 个。这个倍数正是流量爆炸的驱动因素。

- "Agent," "bot," and "crawler" are synonyms — they all mean a machine accessing resources instead of a human with a browser. The framing just depends on whether you want to sound positive (agent) or icky (crawler).
  "智能体"、"bot"和"爬虫"是同义词——都指代机器（而非浏览器里的人类）访问资源。只是措辞上，想听起来积极就说"agent"，想听起来糟糕就说"crawler"。

- The concept of "brand" will have to be reinvented. Brands exist as a shortcut for human recognition; in an agentic world where bots have infinite patience to discover everything, the entire logic of branding changes.
  "品牌"的概念需要重新发明。品牌是人类认知的捷径；而在智能体的世界里，bot 有无限耐心去探索一切，品牌逻辑将彻底改变。

**最值得关注的信号**
The most actionable takeaway: the internet's entire monetization stack — advertising, brand, SEO, click-through — was built for human eyeballs.
最值得关注的信号：互联网整个变现体系——广告、品牌、SEO、点击率——都是为人类眼球设计的。

With bot traffic dominating, every company building on top of the internet needs to start thinking now about what a "bot-native" business model looks like.
随着 bot 流量占据主导，每一家依托互联网构建业务的公司，现在就需要开始思考什么是"bot 原生"的商业模式。
