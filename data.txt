🎙️ PODCAST: Unsupervised Learning — Ep 98: China Expert on Jensen's Return

This emergency episode features Jacob Efron and China expert Jordan Schneider (ChinaTalk) breaking down the geopolitical earthquake around Jensen Huang's firing and reinstatement at NVIDIA.

Key takeaways:
- Jensen's removal was driven by Treasury Secretary Bessent and Secretary Rubio, who were furious about NVIDIA chips reaching the PLA via smuggling networks (involving entities like Mega Speed), combined with revelations that Jensen was romantically involved with a Chinese national who had contact with Chinese intelligence (MSS).
- NVIDIA is expected to pay a ~$25M fine as part of a settlement, with Trump effectively providing political cover for Jensen's return.
- A new 132-page rule (the "diffusion rule") was just promulgated, restricting remote access to NVIDIA chips by Chinese hyperscalers (Alibaba, Tencent, ByteDance) via neo-cloud data centers in Malaysia and other third countries — but its enforcement is now uncertain given Jensen's return.
- Jordan argues Jensen's major sin is "turning a blind eye to chip smuggling" going into Chinese military AI research, drawing a direct parallel to how US banks were eventually forced to take sanctions compliance seriously.
- On robotics: Jordan was deeply disappointed visiting the CORAL conference — humanoid robots "can barely pick up a muffin," China has hardware advantages but the US leads in model quality, and rare earth dependencies remain a critical vulnerability.
- Longer-term concern: AI is deeply unpopular (only 2% of Americans want it to progress faster), setting up a potential political backlash in future presidential cycles.

这期紧急播客由 Unsupervised Learning 主播 Jacob Efron 与中国问题专家 Jordan Schneider（ChinaTalk）联袂录制，深度复盘了 Jensen 黄被解雇又复职的地缘政治风波。
