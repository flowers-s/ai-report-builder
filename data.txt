Garry Tan (@garrytan)

Garry Tan shared results from his open-source harness eval repo, recommending developers keep Claude Opus 5.5 as their default coding model in Claude Code. His benchmark across 52 coding tasks showed that while the newer Fable 5.2 solved a comparable number of tasks (82.7% vs. 84.6% for Opus 5.5), it cost 2.80x more per solved task—making Opus 5.5 the better value. He also signed the #StandWithJensen letter, calling for due process rather than trial-by-media.
Garry Tan 分享了其开源测试框架的评测结果，建议开发者在 Claude Code 中继续以 Claude Opus 5.5 作为默认编程模型。他在 52 个编程任务上的基准测试显示，新款 Fable 5.2 完成率（82.7%）与 Opus 5.5（84.6%）相近，但每个已完成任务的费用高出 2.80 倍——综合性价比 Opus 5.5 更优。他也签署了 #StandWithJensen 公开信，呼吁给予当事人正当程序，而非媒体审判。

Nikunj Kothari (@nikunj)

Nikunj Kothari put the FAA nationwide ground stop in historical context: it's only the third time in modern history, after 9/11 and the 2023 NOTAM system outage. He also pushed back against benchmark-maxxing in AI, arguing that since Llama 4, there seemed to be a truce on inflated benchmarks—but it appears the "benchmark cartel" is back. His take: real-world usage of models in your actual workflows is the only honest evaluation.
Nikunj Kothari 将 FAA 全国停飞事件置于历史背景下解读：这是现代史上仅有的第三次，此前两次分别是 9/11 和 2023 年 NOTAM 系统故障。他还对 AI 行业的"刷榜"现象提出批评：Llama 4 发布后本以为业界已暂停这种风气，但"基准测试卡特尔"似乎卷土重来。他认为，唯有在真实工作流中亲身使用模型，才是最诚实的评测方式。

🎙️ PODCAST: Training Data — "A Model For Everyone: How Ronak From Trajectory Is Building Products For Continual Learning"

This episode of Training Data features Ronak from Trajectory, a startup focused on making continual learning accessible to every company. Ronak uses a compelling Apple analogy: just as Apple combined the internet and the phone into something no one had imagined, Trajectory is combining training, evaluation, and deployment into a single, seamlessly looping platform. He describes the current state of AI as "pseudo continual learning"—like the old AAA road maps era—where model updates happen in large offline batches. Trajectory's goal is to move toward something like Google Maps: a system where every interaction continuously updates the model in production, creating a true learning loop accessible to all companies, not just AI giants.
本期 Training Data 播客专访了 Trajectory 创始人 Ronak。Trajectory 致力于让持续学习能力惠及每一家企业。Ronak 以苹果类比阐述愿景：正如苹果当年将互联网与手机融合，创造出无人预见的革命性产品，Trajectory 正将模型训练、评估与部署整合为一个无缝闭环平台。他将当前 AI 现状描述为"伪持续学习"——类似早年依赖 AAA 纸质地图的时代——模型更新仍以大批量离线方式进行。Trajectory 的目标是迈向"Google Maps 时代"：每一次交互都持续更新生产环境中的模型，真正实现所有企业（而非仅限于 AI 巨头）都能使用的持续学习闭环。
