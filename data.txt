### Unsupervised Learning — Ep 86: Yann LeCun on Leaving Meta, Breaking The LLM Paradigm, & Why Hinton is Wrong

This episode features Yann LeCun, one of the godfathers of deep learning and co-winner of the Turing Award, in a wide-ranging conversation about why LLMs are not a path to human-level intelligence.
本期节目邀请了深度学习教父之一、图灵奖得主 Yann LeCun，就 LLM 为何不是通往人类级智能的路径展开了深度对话。

LeCun announced he has left Meta to start AMI Labs (Advanced Machine Intelligence), focused on building world models using the JEPA (Joint Embedding Predictive Architecture) he pioneered.
LeCun 宣布已离开 Meta，创立 AMI Labs（先进机器智能），专注于使用他开创的 JEPA（联合嵌入预测架构）构建世界模型。

His core thesis: *"LLMs are not a path towards human level or human like intelligence or even animal like intelligence."* They are useful tools, but lack two essential capabilities — the ability to predict the consequences of actions, and the ability to plan through optimization and search.
他的核心论点：*"LLM 不是通往人类级别或类人智能甚至动物级智能的路径。"* 它们是有用的工具，但缺少两种关键能力——预测行为后果的能力，以及通过优化和搜索进行规划的能力。

On safety: LeCun argued that LLMs are *"intrinsically unsafe"* — not just because they hallucinate, but because they cannot predict the consequences of their actions. His proposed alternative is "objective-driven AI" with explicit world models and hardwired safety constraints.
关于安全性：LeCun 认为 LLM"本质上是不安全的"——不仅因为它们会产生幻觉，还因为它们无法预测自身行为的后果。他提出的替代方案是具有显式世界模型和硬编码安全约束的"目标驱动型 AI"。

On leaving Meta: He clarified a major misconception — he had *"zero technical contribution to LLAMA"* and stepped down from directing FAIR back in 2018. His departure was driven by the mismatch between his world model research ambitions and Meta's increasing focus on catching up in the LLM race.
关于离开 Meta：他澄清了一个重大误解——他对 LLAMA *"没有任何技术贡献"*，早在 2018 年就卸任了 FAIR 的主任职务。他的离开源于他的世界模型研究抱负与 Meta 日益聚焦于 LLM 竞赛之间的错位。

On Geoffrey Hinton changing his views: *"I didn't change my mind. They changed their mind."* LeCun believes Hinton's pivot toward AI safety concerns after GPT-4 was intellectually understandable but not technically justified.
关于 Geoffrey Hinton 改变观点：*"我没有改变想法，是他们改变了想法。"* LeCun 认为 Hinton 在 GPT-4 发布后转向 AI 安全担忧，在智识上可以理解，但在技术上并不成立。

His five-year vision for AMI: *"Five years, complete world domination"* — a tongue-in-cheek reference to Linus Torvalds' famous quote, suggesting JEPA world models will eventually underpin intelligent systems the same way Linux underpins the internet.
他对 AMI 的五年愿景：*"五年内，完全统治世界"*——这是对 Linus Torvalds 名言的戏谑引用，暗示 JEPA 世界模型最终将成为智能系统的基础，就像 Linux 支撑互联网一样。

He also described Tapestry, a separate initiative: a federated open-source LLM platform where contributors worldwide share model parameter vectors (not data), enabling AI sovereignty for countries that don't want to depend on US or Chinese models.
他还介绍了另一个独立项目 Tapestry：一个联邦式开源 LLM 平台，全球贡献者共享模型参数向量（而非数据），为不希望依赖美国或中国模型的国家实现 AI 主权。
