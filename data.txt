## 🎙️ PODCAST: Unsupervised Learning — Ep 89

**AI Research Legend's Honest Assessment of Where We Are**
**AI 研究传奇人物对当前进展的坦诚评估**

Guest: Lucas Kaiser — co-author of the original Transformer paper, formerly at Google Brain and OpenAI.
嘉宾：Lucas Kaiser——Transformer 原始论文联合作者，曾就职于 Google Brain 和 OpenAI。

**Core Thesis 核心论点**

Reasoning-based scaling has taken us remarkably far, but it likely cannot reach true generalization alone — something else is needed.
基于推理的规模扩展已让我们走了相当远，但它单独可能无法实现真正的泛化——还需要其他东西。

The open-vs-closed model gap question is legitimate, but the answer depends heavily on which application layer you're building for.
开源与闭源模型的差距问题确实存在，但答案很大程度上取决于你在为哪个应用层构建。

**Key Insights 关键洞察**

• Anthropic succeeded in coding first because they made a deliberate strategic decision to focus there — while OpenAI was pursuing ChatGPT's broader mandate, Anthropic picked a wedge where it could actually compete.
  Anthropic 率先在代码领域取得成功，是因为他们做出了刻意的战略决策——当 OpenAI 追求 ChatGPT 更宏观的目标时，Anthropic 选择了一个真正能够竞争的切入点。

  *"Anthropic made this very good decision to focus on coding. OpenAI was like, we're doing ChatGPT. Partway Anthropic made this decision was that they just could not compete."*
  *"Anthropic 做出了聚焦代码的明智决策。OpenAI 在做 ChatGPT。Anthropic 之所以做出这个决策，部分原因是他们在其他方向根本无法竞争。"*

• Current transformers with reasoning + agents + shell access can do amazing things — but there's a ceiling on pure reasoning-based generalization.
  当前具备推理能力的 Transformer 加上智能体和 Shell 访问权限，能做到令人惊叹的事情——但纯推理泛化存在天花板。

• There is something beyond current reasoning that "possibly could generalize much better" — Kaiser believes new architectural or training paradigms are needed.
  在当前推理能力之外，存在某种"可能泛化得更好"的东西——Kaiser 认为需要新的架构或训练范式。

• The transformer architecture will persist, but its shortcomings (especially in out-of-distribution generalization) are real and will drive the next wave of research.
  Transformer 架构将持续存在，但其缺陷（尤其是分布外泛化）是真实存在的，将推动下一波研究浪潮。

• Future research directions Kaiser is most excited about: architectures that can learn structured world models, not just pattern-match over tokens.
  Kaiser 最期待的未来研究方向：能够学习结构化世界模型的架构，而非仅仅在 token 上进行模式匹配。
