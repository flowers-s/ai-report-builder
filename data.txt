**Aaron Levie (@levie)** — CEO @ Box

Aaron dropped a series of substantive AI takes. **PODCAST: The MAD Podcast with Matt Turck**

*Jev: The AI That Makes Decisions, Not Text — and Why It Changes Everything (Diogo Almeida)*

In this episode, Matt Turck sits down with Diogo Almeida, CEO of TypeSafe, to unpack Jev — the model that went viral a month after launch and spawned a wave of copycats. Diogo explains that Jev is fundamentally different from text-generation models: it makes *decisions*, not tokens. He critiques pre-training as "a giant dumpster fire of wasting resources" and argues that the industry could have had AGI sooner if it had focused on the right problems. The fast-followers copying Jev are missing the hard part: the architectural insight, not just the output format.
在本期节目中，Matt Turck 与 TypeSafe CEO Diogo Almeida 深度对话，拆解 Jev 的本质——这个模型在发布一个月后走红，引发了大量模仿。Diogo 解释说，Jev 与文本生成模型有根本区别：它做的是"决策"，而非生成 token。他批评预训练是"浪费资源的垃圾堆"，并认为如果行业更早聚焦正确问题，AGI 本可更早到来。那些迅速复制 Jev 的追随者，恰恰错过了最难的部分：核心架构洞见，而非输出格式。

*"95% Einstein, 5% Mr. Bean. And if it doesn't tell you when it's the Mr. Bean, then how are you gonna automate anything?"*
"95% 爱因斯坦，5% 憨豆先生。如果它不告诉你什么时候是憨豆时刻，你怎么可能自动化任何事情？"

*"Pre-training is a giant dumpster fire of wasting resources."*
"预训练是一场浪费资源的大型垃圾场火灾。"
