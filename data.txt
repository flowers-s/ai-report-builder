### The MAD Podcast with Matt Turck
**OpenAI's Dan Roberts: Why AI Can Now Make Discoveries**

In this episode, Matt Turck sits down with Dan Roberts, lead of the Foundations of Reinforcement Learning team at OpenAI, to explore a landmark moment: AI systems from OpenAI, DeepMind, and Anthropic have cracked some of the most famous long-unsolved problems in mathematics — the Erdős problems.
在这一期节目中，Matt Turck 与 OpenAI 强化学习基础团队负责人 Dan Roberts 深入探讨了一个里程碑式的时刻：OpenAI、DeepMind 和 Anthropic 的 AI 系统已攻克了数学领域最著名的一批长期悬而未决的难题——Erdős 问题。

Dan explains what the Foundations of RL team does — studying the science of reinforcement learning, understanding how it works, and exploring scaling laws for RL versus pretraining.
Dan 解释了强化学习基础团队的工作：研究强化学习的科学原理，理解其运作方式，并探索 RL 与预训练的缩放规律。

*"I feel really excited that we will get to really answer a lot of fundamental questions in the field of science that we care about, with the models being the driving force."*
*"我非常兴奋，我们即将借助模型作为驱动力，真正解答科学领域我们最关心的一批根本性问题。"*

A key insight from Dan: to solve hard math problems, AI had to learn to go against the grain — to assume a statement is false and persevere down a long, contrarian calculation path, requiring strong conviction in the approach.
Dan 的核心洞见：为了解决困难的数学问题，AI 必须学会逆向思考——假设某个命题为假，并沿着漫长的反向计算路径坚持下去，这需要对方法本身有极强的信念。

The conversation also covers what's ahead for AI and science, and why reinforcement learning is the most important paradigm in AI right now.
对话还涵盖了 AI 与科学的未来走向，以及为何强化学习是当前 AI 领域最重要的范式。
