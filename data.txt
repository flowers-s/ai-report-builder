### No Priors — "Building an Autonomous Enterprise for Real-World Services with Netic Founder Melisa Tokmak"

Melisa Tokmak, founder and CEO of Netic, joined No Priors to talk about building autonomous AI for the real-world services industry.
Netic 创始人兼 CEO Melisa Tokmak 来到 No Priors，分享如何为现实世界的服务行业构建自主 AI。

Netic builds AI agents to run millions of real-world businesses in essential services — HVAC, plumbing, electric, pet care, wellness, automotive, and more.
Netic 构建 AI agent，为数百万家从事基本服务的实体企业提供支持——涵盖暖通空调、管道、电气、宠物护理、健康wellness、汽车服务等领域。

Before founding Netic, Melisa spent four years at Scale AI, building real-world business units in government, logistics, manufacturing, financial services, and healthcare.
在创立 Netic 之前，Melisa 在 Scale AI 工作了四年，负责搭建政府、物流、制造业、金融服务和医疗健康等实体业务板块。

The core problem Netic solves: when a billion-dollar HVAC company has five employees quit on Monday morning during a summer heat wave, Netic agents handle every inbound customer call, text, and web inquiry — understanding need, checking availability, scheduling the right technician, and optimizing for customer lifetime value.
Netic 解决的核心问题是：当一家十亿美元体量的暖通公司在夏季热浪期间周一早上突然有五名员工离职时，Netic agent 接管所有入站客户电话、短信和网页咨询——理解需求、确认档期、安排合适技术员，并优化客户终身价值。

Over 70% of Netic's customers are now "Netic first" — meaning Netic agents handle every first customer interaction, not just overflow.
Netic 超过 70% 的客户现在已是"Netic 优先"——意味着 Netic agent 处理每一个客户的首次交互，而不仅仅是溢出流量。

On why she built a software product instead of doing an AI roll-up: *"I'm a builder, I am an engineer, I'm a product person. In a lot of those roll-ups, the most important thing is the M&A itself. That just doesn't fit my skill set."*
谈到为何选择做软件产品而非 AI 并购整合：*"我是一个建造者、工程师、产品人。在很多并购整合中，最关键的是 M&A 本身，这不符合我的能力特长。"*

On robotics: she believes physical robotics in field services is still many decades away — buildings aren't standardized, dexterity challenges remain immense, and the human element in these industries is irreplaceable in the near term.
谈机器人：她认为实体机器人在现场服务领域还需数十年才能落地——建筑物没有标准化，灵巧性挑战依然巨大，这些行业的人情味在短期内无可替代。

On the talent mindset she looks for: *"Agency is not something you have now — if you care about agency, you've shown agency continuously in your life."*
谈她看重的人才特质：*"主动性不是你现在有没有的问题——如果你真的看重主动性，你一生中必然一直在展现它。"*

She cited Martin Luther: *"The Christian shoemaker doesn't honor God by putting little crosses on the shoes, it does so by building the best shoe."*
她引用马丁·路德的话：*"基督徒鞋匠荣耀上帝的方式，不是在鞋上印小十字架，而是做出最好的鞋。"*

Netic has generated over $600 million in revenue for its customers from AI-handled interactions so far.
Netic 迄今已通过 AI 处理的交互为客户创造了超过 6 亿美元的收入。

On what excites her most about AI's future: education — *"no one will be limited anymore... everything you want to learn and everything you want to do is kind of in your pocket."*
谈到她对 AI 未来最期待的事：教育——*"没有人会再受到限制……你想学的一切、想做的一切，都装在你的口袋里。"*
