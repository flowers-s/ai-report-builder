### Box CEO Aaron Levie (levie on X)

Aaron Levie shared a counterintuitive observation: software job postings are actually outpacing other fields, even as AI was supposed to replace software engineers.
Aaron Levie 分享了一个反直觉的观察：尽管 AI 被认为会取代软件工程师，但软件岗位的招聘量实际上正在超越其他领域。

His explanation draws on Jevons Paradox: when the cost of producing software drops, demand for software explodes — more projects get started, more systems need maintenance, and more people are needed to decide what to build.
他的解释援引了杰文斯悖论：当软件生产成本降低时，对软件的需求反而会爆发——更多项目启动，更多系统需要维护，也需要更多人来决定构建什么。

*"Agents will cause more abundance than replacement."*
*"Agent 带来的是更多的丰盈，而不是替代。"*
