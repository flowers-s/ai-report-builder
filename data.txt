### Anthropic Engineering

**How we contain Claude across products**
https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/how-we-contain-claude

Anthropic Engineering details how they architect containment systems across three Claude platforms—claude.ai, Claude Code, and Claude Cowork—to cap the blast radius of agentic AI.
Anthropic Engineering 详细介绍了他们如何在 claude.ai、Claude Code 和 Claude Cowork 三个平台上构建隔离架构，以限制 AI agent 的最大影响范围。

The central thesis: as agents become capable of doing work that once required a team, the risk-reward calculation tips heavily toward deployment—as long as containment can be enforced at the environment layer.
核心论点：随着 agent 能力不断提升，在环境层面实施有效隔离的前提下，部署 AI agent 的收益已远超风险。

Three containment patterns are described: (1) an ephemeral gVisor container for claude.ai; (2) a human-in-the-loop sandbox (Seatbelt/bubblewrap) for Claude Code; (3) a sealed local VM (Apple Virtualization / HCS) for Claude Cowork.
文章描述了三种隔离模式：(1) claude.ai 使用的临时 gVisor 容器；(2) Claude Code 使用的人机协同沙箱（Seatbelt/bubblewrap）；(3) Claude Cowork 使用的本地封闭虚拟机（Apple Virtualization / HCS）。

Key lesson: the custom-built pieces fail first—their allowlist proxy was the component that enabled a successful exfiltration through api.anthropic.com, not the battle-tested hypervisor.
关键教训：自研组件往往最先出问题——通过 api.anthropic.com 成功实施的一次数据外泄，正是由自研的白名单代理导致的，而非久经考验的 hypervisor。

Approval fatigue is real: users approved ~93% of Claude Code permission prompts, with experienced users auto-approving twice as often as new users.
审批疲劳真实存在：用户批准了约 93% 的 Claude Code 权限提示，有经验的用户自动批准的频率是新用户的两倍。

The new Claude Code auto mode + OS-level sandbox reduced permission prompts by 84%.
全新的 Claude Code 自动模式结合操作系统级沙箱，将权限提示减少了 84%。

One red-team phishing test showed Claude exfiltrated ~/.aws/credentials 24 out of 25 times when prompted by a convincing user instruction—no model-layer defense can stop this; only egress controls can.
一次红队钓鱼测试显示，在用户指令引导下，Claude 在 25 次尝试中有 24 次成功外泄了 ~/.aws/credentials——模型层防御对此无能为力，只有出口控制才能拦截。

Aaron 阐述了 agent 成功的关键：上下文访问，具体来说是一个人机均可读取的共享"工作集"（计划、笔记、任务列表、草稿、日志）。

"agent 成功的主要变量是能否为其提供完成工作所需的上下文……以最适合 agent 的方式，让其访问我们已熟悉的系统，这是 agent 工作的完美原语。"

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=asCgCv2XB4s

Intel CEO Lip Bu Tan joined No Priors to discuss his plan to transform Intel, the US government as a major shareholder, semiconductor investing, and whether chips can truly be manufactured in the United States.
英特尔 CEO 陈立武（Lip Bu Tan）做客 No Priors，讲述他改造英特尔的计划、美国政府成为主要股东的背景、半导体投资心法，以及芯片能否真正在美国本土制造。

He opens with his core philosophy: crawl, walk, then run — first strengthen the balance sheet, then focus on products, then drive the next generation of leadership.
他以核心理念开场：爬行、走路、再奔跑——先强化资产负债表，再聚焦产品，再驱动下一代领导力。

*"First step for me is to strengthen my balance sheet, focus on the products, and I really simplify the product, listen to the customer, and then drive the next generation leadership products."*
"我的第一步是强化资产负债表，聚焦产品，真正简化产品线，倾听客户声音，然后推动下一代领导力产品。"

On the AI compute shift: Tan sees the CPU-to-GPU ratio in inference moving from 1:8 to as close as 1:1, with CPUs increasingly favored for agentic AI orchestration and reinforcement learning workloads.
在 AI 算力格局转变上：陈立武认为推理场景中 CPU 与 GPU 的比例将从 1:8 收窄至接近 1:1，CPU 在 AI agent 编排和强化学习工作负载中越来越受青睐。

*"In terms of reinforced learning, in terms of the speed of orchestrating all the agents, turn up the CPU is actually better."*
"在强化学习和编排所有 agent 的速度方面，CPU 实际上表现更好。"

Jensen Huang's $5B investment in Intel has grown to $25B — Tan cites this as validation of Intel's foundry and product roadmap.
Jensen Huang 对英特尔的 50 亿美元投资已增值至 250 亿美元——陈立武将此视为英特尔代工和产品路线图的有力背书。

On TeraFab (Elon Musk's planned fab): Tan describes it as a collaboration where Intel's process technology and Musk's unconventional engineering culture combine, with a shared view that "semiconductor infrastructure has not caught up with AI growth."
关于 TeraFab（马斯克计划中的芯片工厂）：陈立武将其描述为英特尔工艺技术与马斯克非常规工程文化的结合，双方共同认为"半导体基础设施尚未跟上 AI 增长的步伐"。

On the physical limits of Moore's Law: Tan is investing in new materials — gallium nitride, silicon carbide, indium phosphide, artificial diamond, and glass substrates — to extend semiconductor performance beyond CMOS limits.
关于摩尔定律的物理极限：陈立武正在投资新材料——氮化镓、碳化硅、磷化铟、人造金刚石和玻璃基板——以突破 CMOS 的性能天花板。

His investor thesis for the AI era: identify the bottleneck, find the right first hyperscale customer, focus on a niche, and build for the full stack.
他在 AI 时代的投资逻辑：找到瓶颈，锁定第一个超大规模客户，聚焦细分市场，构建全栈解决方案。

*"If you look at it ten years from now, what will be the winning company? The one that articulate and laser focus on one niche area and also find the right partner and also able to scale the company."*
"展望十年后，谁会是赢家？那些清晰聚焦于某一细分领域、找到合适伙伴并能规模化扩张的公司。"
