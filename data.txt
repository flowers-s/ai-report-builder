👤 Peter Yang (@petergyang)

Peter praised the Devin/Windsurf team for staying disciplined through challenges, noting that many AI-native builders now love Devin. He also offered a sharp take on SaaS viability: while large, multi-purpose enterprise SaaS (like Figma) may be fine, narrow single-use SaaS faces existential pressure as AI agents with personal context can solve the same problems more flexibly and cheaply. He quipped that founders building with AI are so excited they forget to find users.
Peter 盛赞 Devin/Windsurf 团队在起伏中保持战略定力，指出越来越多 AI 原生开发者爱上了 Devin。他还就 SaaS 生死问题发表了犀利见解：大型多功能企业 SaaS（如 Figma）尚可，但窄场景 SaaS 面临生存危机——拥有个人上下文的 AI 智能体可以更灵活、更低成本地解决相同问题。他还调侃道：有些 AI 创业者太兴奋于"能构建任何东西"，却忘了去找用户。

👤 Aaron Levie (@levie)

Aaron made a strategic prediction: as token budgets become a major operating expense, intelligent model routing is the inevitable and most important differentiator for the applied AI layer. Products that can route workflows to the right model tier — balancing cost and performance by domain — will have a lasting edge. Most use cases still need frontier models today, but that is changing fast.
Aaron 提出了一个战略判断：随着 Token 预算成为主要运营成本，智能模型路由将成为应用 AI 层最重要的差异化能力。能够按领域平衡成本与性能、将工作流路由到合适模型层级的产品，将获得持久竞争优势。当前大多数场景仍需前沿模型，但这一格局正在快速改变。
