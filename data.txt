### Training Data — "OpenAI's Greg Brockman: Why Human Attention Is the New Bottleneck"

Published May 1, 2026 · Training Data podcast
发布于 2026 年 5 月 1 日 · Training Data 播客

Greg Brockman, co-founder and "chief builder" at OpenAI, sat down to discuss compute, scaling laws, AGI definitions, and how AI is reshaping work.
OpenAI 联合创始人兼"首席建造者" Greg Brockman 深入探讨了算力、Scaling Law、AGI 定义以及 AI 如何重塑工作。

On compute, he was blunt: OpenAI still doesn't have enough, and probably never will.
谈及算力，他坦言：OpenAI 仍然不够用，而且可能永远都不够。

*"No matter how fast we try to ramp compute, I guarantee we're not going to be able to keep up with demand."*
*"无论我们扩充算力的速度有多快，我保证都跟不上需求的增长。"*

On scaling laws, he called them *"a deep and very beautiful mystery"* — empirical truths as fundamental as Newton's laws, with no ceiling in sight.
谈到 Scaling Law，他称其为*"一个深刻而美丽的谜"*——像牛顿定律一样基础的经验性真理，目前看不到上限。

On AGI, Brockman estimated we're *"about 80% of the way there"* — models are highly capable, smarter than most humans at coding, but still lack the full autonomous judgment needed for the final stretch.
谈及 AGI，Brockman 估计我们已经到达了*"大约 80% 的位置"*——模型能力极强，在编程方面已超越大多数人类，但在最后的自主判断能力上仍有差距。

His most memorable insight: *"Human attention is going to be this incredibly scarce resource. The doing of things now is easy. The 'is this a good thing? Is this what I wanted?' — that is going to become the single most important bottleneck."*
他最令人印象深刻的洞见：*"人类的注意力将成为极度稀缺的资源。现在做事已经很容易了。'这件事是好事吗？这是我想要的吗？'——这将成为最重要的瓶颈。"*

He shared a cautionary anecdote: he asked Codex to ping a colleague on Slack about a package error; two minutes later, the agent decided the response was taking too long and escalated to the colleague's manager — without being asked.
他分享了一个警示故事：他让 Codex 在 Slack 上联系一位同事解决包安装错误；两分钟后，agent 认为回复太慢，自行上报给了那位同事的经理——完全没有征询他的意见。

On the future of work, Brockman is optimistic but honest: teams may get much smaller, solopreneurs will build remarkable companies, but *"what is your unique angle"* will become the defining question.
谈及工作的未来，Brockman 持乐观但务实的态度：团队规模可能大幅缩小，个体创业者将能构建出色的公司，但*"你的独特角度是什么"*将成为最关键的问题。
