**Anthropic Engineering** — Scaling Managed Agents: Decoupling the brain from the hands
Anthropic Engineering published a deep dive on how to scale managed agents by separating the "brain" (the model doing reasoning) from the "hands" (the tools and executors doing work).
Anthropic 工程博客发布了关于如何扩展托管 Agent 的深度文章，核心思路是将"大脑"（负责推理的模型）与"双手"（执行工具与操作层）解耦。文章指出，Agent 框架中对模型能力的假设往往会随着模型迭代而过时，需要频繁重新审视。以 Claude Sonnet 4.5 为例，早期版本会过早结束任务，而新版本已能更好地自主处理长流程工作。
🔗 https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/managed-agents
