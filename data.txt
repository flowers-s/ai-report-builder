🎙️ PODCAST: The MAD Podcast with Matt Turck

**The Biggest AI Deployment Nobody Talks About | Samsara CEO Sanjit Biswas**
**没人谈论的最大规模 AI 部署 | Samsara CEO Sanjit Biswas**

In this episode, Matt Turck sits down with Sanjit Biswas, co-founder and CEO of Samsara — a $20B company that may be running the world's largest physical AI deployment.
在这一期节目中，Matt Turck 与 Samsara 联合创始人兼 CEO Sanjit Biswas 深入对谈。Samsara 是一家市值 200 亿美元的公司，可能正在运营全球最大规模的物理世界 AI 部署。

Samsara operates millions of vehicles, processes 25 trillion data points per year, and drives 99% of US roads every single day.
Samsara 管理着数百万辆车辆，每年处理 25 万亿个数据点，每天覆盖美国 99% 的道路。

**Core Insight:** Physical AI is fundamentally different from digital AI — the data doesn't exist online. You can't crawl Reddit to find out what happened on a construction site.
**核心洞察：** 物理 AI 与数字 AI 本质不同——相关数据根本不在互联网上。你无法通过爬取 Reddit 来了解建筑工地上发生了什么。

**Key Highlights:**

1. **AI preventing crashes at scale.** Samsara believes it helped prevent approximately 380,000 car crashes and road accidents in the last year alone — through real-time fatigue detection, mobile phone alerts, and seatbelt reminders.
   **规模化 AI 防止事故。** Samsara 相信仅过去一年就帮助防止了约 38 万起交通事故——通过实时疲劳检测、手机使用提醒和安全带提示实现。

2. **The physical world is 40-50% of world GDP** — yet it's largely undigitized. This creates enormous opportunity, but also demands "rolling up your sleeves" in a way digital software never did.
   **物理世界占全球 GDP 的 40-50%**——但大部分尚未数字化。这创造了巨大机会，但也要求"撸起袖子"亲力亲为，远比数字软件更费力。

3. **The data moat is unassailable.** These are tokens you won't find online — GPS traces, dashcam footage, weather-road correlations. The company ingests petabytes of data that cannot be scraped or replicated.
   **数据护城河坚不可摧。** 这些是互联网上找不到的数据——GPS 轨迹、行车记录仪画面、天气与路况的关联。公司摄入的 PB 级数据无法被爬取或复制。

4. **Agentic AI is closing the loop.** The last 20 years were about reporting — beautiful dashboards. Now AI can reason over the data and *take action*: schedule maintenance, coach drivers, automate routes.
   **智能 AI 正在闭合循环。** 过去 20 年是关于报告——精美的仪表盘。现在 AI 可以对数据进行推理并*采取行动*：安排维护、辅导司机、自动优化路线。

5. **Grid capacity tripling in 5 years.** An energy utility told Biswas: in the next 5 years, they'll build 3x the grid capacity added in the past 125 years — a staggering infrastructure moment that physical AI is uniquely positioned to support.
   **电网容量将在 5 年内翻三倍。** 一家能源公用事业公司告诉 Biswas：未来 5 年他们将建设相当于过去 125 年总量三倍的电网容量——这是一个惊人的基础设施时刻，物理 AI 具有独特的支撑优势。

Source: https://www.youtube.com/@DataDrivenNYC/videos
