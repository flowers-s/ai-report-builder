## Madhu Guru @realmadhuguru
Former Product Leader @ Google (Gemini, Veo, Nano)

Madhu Guru shared a practical rule of thumb from his enterprise AI experience: start with small models when replacing traditional ML systems (where success criteria are well-defined), but start with large models when building net-new generative experiences. This cost/quality tradeoff guidance is hard-won from working with enterprise clients on Gemini.
Madhu Guru 分享了一条来自 Google 企业 AI 实践的经验法则：替换传统 ML 模型时从小模型入手（因为已知好的输出长什么样）；而构建全新生成式应用时则应从大模型起步。这一成本/质量权衡建议来自他在 Gemini 企业客户侧的真实经验。

## PODCAST: AI & I by Every — "How Anthropic Uses Claude Fable 5 With Mike Krieger"

Published: 2026-06-10
URL: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XWpTgCvgYaE

In this episode, Dan Shipper (CEO of Every) sits down with Mike Krieger — co-founder of Instagram and Head of Anthropic Labs — to discuss what it's really like to use Claude Fable 5 beyond day one. Mike explains that the Anthropic team has been living with Fable-class models for months, and that extended use reveals capabilities you simply don't discover on launch day. He highlights that users who spent time with earlier Claude models in December–January consistently reported "I wasn't pushing it hard enough" — and that the same will be true for Fable 5. The conversation covers how Anthropic internally uses Claude for complex work, how the model handles multi-step tasks with greater reliability, and how Mike thinks about the frontier between what AI can do today versus what builders imagine.

在这一期节目中，Every CEO Dan Shipper 与 Instagram 联合创始人、Anthropic Labs 负责人 Mike Krieger 深度对谈，探讨超越发布首日的 Claude Fable 5 真实使用体验。Mike 指出，Anthropic 团队已与 Fable 级别的模型共处数月，长期深度使用才能发掘到发布当天感知不到的能力边界。他分享了一个规律：使用过早期 Claude 模型较长时间的用户，无一例外地反馈"我之前用得不够激进"——Fable 5 也将如此。两人探讨了 Anthropic 内部如何将 Claude 用于复杂工作、模型在多步骤任务中更强的可靠性，以及 Mike 对 AI 今日能力边界与构建者想象力之间鸿沟的思考。
