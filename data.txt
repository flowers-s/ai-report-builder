**Dan Shipper (danshipper on X) — Every CEO**

Dan Shipper offered a nuanced critique of a report on AI's impact on jobs, challenging two core assumptions: that jobs can be cleanly decomposed into tasks, and that automation always reduces human labor.
Dan Shipper 对一份关于 AI 对就业影响的报告提出了细致的批评，质疑其两个核心假设：工作可以被清晰分解为任务，以及自动化必然减少人类劳动。

*"In my experience, the answer is no. Automation often creates multiples more work for humans."*
"根据我的经验，答案是否定的。自动化往往会为人类创造更多倍的工作量。"

Drawing on Wittgenstein and Heidegger, he argued that a job is "a particular way of seeing and caring about the world" — and that as tools change, entirely new tasks emerge from the same underlying cares, a process that's structurally missing from most automation analyses.
他借鉴维特根斯坦和海德格尔的观点，认为工作是"一种特定的看待和关怀世界的方式"，随着工具变化，相同的关怀会催生全新的任务，而这一过程在大多数自动化分析中结构性地缺失。
