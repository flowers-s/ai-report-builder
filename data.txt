**The MAD Podcast with Matt Turck — "The GPU Myth: State of AI Compute 2026 | Stephen Balaban"**
本期播客深度对话 Lambda 联合创始人兼 CTO Stephen Balaban，揭穿关于 GPU 算力的最大误解。Stephen 指出，云计算从来不是商品，而是一项高度垂直整合的复杂服务，涵盖土地、建设、高性能计算、软件与虚拟化等全栈能力。
In this episode, Matt Turck speaks with Stephen Balaban (co-founder & CTO of Lambda) about why GPU compute was never a commodity. Stephen argues that cloud infrastructure is deeply vertically integrated — spanning land entitlement, construction, HPC design, software, and virtualization — which is exactly why trillion-dollar companies like Amazon, Microsoft, and Google dominate the space.
他们还探讨了 Lambda 从人脸识别初创公司到戴摄像头棒球帽、再到估值近十亿美元云业务的奇特演变历程，以及如何为数十亿美元的数据中心融资、为什么 2023 年的 H100 如今反而比当年更贵租。Stephen 强调，算力需求的增长没有尽头，当前整体行业仍处于「供给不足」状态。
*"The people who are the naysayers, you're gonna throw these GPUs out in five years, are completely wrong. They've been wrong the entire time. We continue to be generally underbuilding."* — AI 算力的悲观论者，一直都错了。
