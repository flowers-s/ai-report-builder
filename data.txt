Peter Yang — AI educator and podcast host (petergyang on X)

Peter Yang shared a key insight on AI evals from researcher Shreya Rajpal: top-down evals are tasks you can design from a description, while bottom-up evals emerge from observing real outputs.
Peter Yang 分享了来自研究员 Shreya Rajpal 的 AI 评估核心洞见：自上而下的 eval 是基于任务描述设计的，而自下而上的 eval 则来自对真实输出的观察归纳。

*"Claude does a very good job of helping you with these top-down evals. Bottom-up evals are the other half. When you look at lots and lots of sample outputs, what is your gut feedback that you want externalized into evals? Claude is very, very bad at coming up with bottom-up evals. That's all you."*
— Shreya Rajpal

这句话点出了一个常被忽视的盲区：模型善于帮你构建顶层框架，但从大量样本中提炼直觉式评估标准，仍然需要人类的判断力。
This highlights a commonly overlooked blind spot: models are good at building top-level frameworks, but extracting intuitive eval criteria from many samples still requires human judgment.

*"Not too granular. Not too coarse. Just right. Make your eval set as granular as you need to diagnose and act."*
"不要太细，也不要太粗，恰到好处。让你的 eval 细粒度足以定位问题、指导行动。"
