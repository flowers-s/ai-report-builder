**The MAD Podcast with Matt Turck**
*Jev: The AI That Makes Decisions, Not Text - and Why It Changes Everything (Diogo Almeida)*

This episode features Diogo Almeida, CEO of TypeSafe and creator of Jev — the new AI model that makes decisions rather than generating text.
本期节目专访 TypeSafe CEO、Jev 创始人 Diogo Almeida，Jev 是一种做出决策而非生成文本的新型 AI 模型。

Diogo explains that Jev is not a language model — it is a "machine-native" intelligence designed to plug directly into software systems as a programmable, composable decision layer.
Diogo 解释说，Jev 并非语言模型，而是一种"机器原生"智能，旨在作为可编程、可组合的决策层直接嵌入软件系统。

The key insight: LLMs are trained via RLHF to be pleasing to humans, which makes them sycophantic, unreliable for automation, and impossible to compose safely — what Diogo calls *"95% Einstein, 5% Mr. Bean."*
核心洞见：LLM 通过 RLHF 训练以讨好人类，导致其谄媚、难以可靠地自动化执行、也无法安全组合——Diogo 称之为"95% 爱因斯坦，5% 憨豆先生"。

Jev outputs calibrated probabilities instead of prose, allowing developers to set their own thresholds — so a Disney deployment can have very different refusal thresholds than a roleplay app.
Jev 输出经过校准的概率而非文本，让开发者可以自行设定阈值——迪士尼部署场景和角色扮演应用可以有截然不同的拒绝阈值。

Diogo argues classification is not "old ML" — it is the fundamental interface by which intelligence enters software: *"How does Facebook work? Classifiers. How does TikTok work? Classifiers. How does Google work? Classifiers."*
Diogo 指出分类并非"老旧机器学习"——它是智能进入软件的基本接口："Facebook 如何运作？分类器。TikTok 如何运作？分类器。Google 如何运作？分类器。"

On the wave of copycats since launch: Diogo is confident competitors are years behind in reliability, because getting the interface right is easy — getting reliable, composable intelligence is hard.
谈到上线以来涌现的大量跟风产品，Diogo 有信心认为竞争对手在可靠性上落后数年——因为做对接口很容易，但实现可靠、可组合的智能非常困难。

He also shared that he wrote an internal document at OpenAI arguing *"we could have had AGI last year"* — and still believes it — because the models are already incredibly capable, but the distribution/deployment problem remains unsolved.
他还透露，他曾在 OpenAI 内部撰写文件，主张"我们去年本可以实现 AGI"——并且他至今仍这么认为——因为模型已极具能力，但分发与部署问题依然悬而未决。
