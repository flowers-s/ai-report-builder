Thariq（Anthropic 工程师）以国际象棋为类比：就像象棋引擎改变了棋界但并未消除人类的兴趣，AI 正在以类似方式改变数学领域。他观察到杰文斯悖论已在数学中显现——AI 让更多数学活动成为可能，理解门槛降低，数学家有更多时间在更高抽象层次上与我们交流。他得出结论：对深度思考数学的人的需求只会增加。
Thariq, an engineer at Anthropic, drew a compelling parallel between AI and chess: just as chess engines transformed the game without eliminating human interest, AI is transforming math. He observed Jevons paradox already at work — *"there is more happening, it is easier to understand and mathematicians have more time to discuss it with us at higher abstraction levels."* His conclusion: demand for people who think deeply about math will go up, not down.

Vercel CEO Guillermo Rauch 分享了 Vercel 内部 AI 代理 "@v" 的进展——它如今驱动着公司日常运营的方方面面，包括财务、公关等，日均交互和 token 使用量呈指数级增长。他总结了一个早期教训：拥有数十个独立代理就像每个服务有自己的域名——用户体验很差，需要统一入口。他还传递了一个积极信号：AI 固然强大，但技艺与创造力加持下的 AI 才能达到另一个维度。
Vercel CEO Guillermo Rauch shared that Vercel built an internal AI agent called "@v" that now powers day-to-day operations across the entire company — finance, comms, and more — with exponentially growing daily interactions and token usage. He noted an early lesson: having dozens of separate agents is like every service having its own domain — bad UX. The solution was unifying them under one interface. His broader take: *"AI alone is cool. But mastery + creativity + AI hits on a whole different level. Don't let anyone discourage you from pursuing excellence and craft."*

Box CEO Aaron Levie 指出了一个反直觉的现象：数学、网络安全、编程等"最难"的工作，恰恰最先面临自动化，因为这些领域的成果可以被验证。正是由于可测试性，AI 更容易在这些领域学习并超越人类表现。
Box CEO Aaron Levie pointed out a counterintuitive dynamic: some of the "hardest" work — math, cybersecurity, coding — is actually most prone to automation first, precisely because it's verifiable. The ability to test whether a solution is correct makes it easier for AI to learn and exceed human performance in these domains.

Every CEO Dan Shipper 深入探讨了 AI 代理替代人类任务的深层心理冲击：当一个语言模型接管了你每一步都需要亲力亲为的任务，这是一次巨大的"能动性断裂"。他认为我们将自我认同与工作输出等同，失去输出感觉就像失去身份。他还从哲学角度指出：技术若重塑人类能力边界，就必然重塑道德"应当"——AI 将同步改变我们的道德直觉与能力边界。近期他在读拿破仑战役相关史料，推荐《战争与和平》及《随拿破仑在俄罗斯》等第一手记录。
Every CEO Dan Shipper explored the deep psychological impact of AI agents displacing human tasks: *"when you experience a language model doing a task that used to require you at every step and now doesn't, it's a gigantic agency rupture."* He argues we equate ourselves with our outputs, so losing them feels like an identity crisis. He also makes a philosophical point: if technology reshapes human abilities, it reshapes moral "oughts" — AI will fundamentally change our moral intuitions alongside our capabilities. He's been reading about Napoleon's campaigns and recommends War and Peace and historical firsthand accounts.
