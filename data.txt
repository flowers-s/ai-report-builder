### Anthropic Engineering

**How we contain Claude across products**
https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/how-we-contain-claude

Twelve months ago, Anthropic would have rejected out of hand the idea of granting Claude access sufficient to take down an internal service.
十二个月前，Anthropic 还会断然拒绝赋予 Claude 足以破坏内部服务的访问权限。

Today that level of access is routine, and Anthropic developers are more productive for it.
如今，这种访问级别已是常规操作，Anthropic 的开发者也因此效率更高。

The engineering question has become: how to cap the blast radius when AI agents go wrong.
工程层面的核心问题已变为：当 AI agent 出错时，如何限制爆炸半径。

Anthropic shares lessons from three products — claude.ai, Claude Code, and Claude Cowork — covering three isolation patterns: ephemeral containers, human-in-the-loop sandboxes, and sealed VMs.
Anthropic 分享了三款产品的经验——claude.ai、Claude Code 和 Claude Cowork——涵盖三种隔离模式：临时容器、人在回路沙箱，以及封闭虚拟机。

Key incidents revealed: a pre-trust-dialog code execution vulnerability in Claude Code, an employee phish that exfiltrated credentials 24 out of 25 times, and a Cowork egress flaw where attacker-controlled API keys bypassed domain allowlists via api.anthropic.com.
关键事件披露：Claude Code 中信任对话框前的代码执行漏洞、24/25 次成功窃取凭证的员工钓鱼攻击，以及 Cowork 的出口缺陷——攻击者通过 api.anthropic.com 利用自控 API key 绕过域名白名单。

Core principle: design for containment at the environment layer first, then steer behavior at the model layer.
核心原则：优先在环境层设计隔离，再在模型层引导行为。

The post also warns that custom-built components are consistently the weakest link — battle-tested hypervisors and syscall filters held firm while custom proxies failed.
文章还警告：自研组件始终是最薄弱的环节——经过实战检验的 hypervisor 和系统调用过滤器坚不可摧，而自定义代理屡屡失守。

**An update on recent Claude Code quality reports**
https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/april-23-postmortem

Anthropic traced recent quality degradation reports to three separate changes that affected Claude Code, the Claude Agent SDK, and Claude Cowork.
Anthropic 将近期的质量下降报告追溯到三个独立变更，影响了 Claude Code、Claude Agent SDK 和 Claude Cowork。

First: on March 4, default reasoning effort was quietly downgraded from high to medium to reduce latency — this was reverted on April 7 after user complaints.
第一：3 月 4 日，默认推理强度被悄然从 high 降至 medium 以降低延迟——在用户投诉后于 4 月 7 日回滚。

Second: a caching optimization shipped March 26 had a bug that cleared thinking history every turn (not just once after idle sessions), making Claude forgetful and causing cache misses — fixed April 10.
第二：3 月 26 日上线的缓存优化存在 bug，会每轮清除思维历史（而非仅在会话空闲后清除一次），导致 Claude 记忆力下降并频繁缓存未命中——4 月 10 日已修复。

Third: a system prompt instruction to cap response length at 25 words between tool calls hurt coding quality and was reverted April 20.
第三：一条限制工具调用间响应长度不超过 25 词的系统提示损害了编程质量，4 月 20 日已回滚。

Anthropic is resetting usage limits for all subscribers and has committed to broader evals, tighter prompt-change controls, and gradual rollouts going forward.
Anthropic 正在重置所有订阅者的使用配额，并承诺此后将实施更广泛的评估、更严格的提示词变更管控以及渐进式发布。

**Scaling Managed Agents: Decoupling the brain from the hands**
https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/managed-agents

Anthropic Engineering shares architecture lessons from scaling managed (server-side) agents at production volume.
Anthropic Engineering 分享了在生产规模下扩展托管（服务端）agent 的架构经验。

The key insight is decoupling the "brain" (the model doing reasoning) from the "hands" (the execution environment), allowing each to scale independently.
核心洞察是将"大脑"（负责推理的模型）与"双手"（执行环境）解耦，使两者能够独立扩展。

The post covers how to handle long-running tasks, retries, partial failures, and maintaining coherent agent state across distributed infrastructure.
文章涵盖如何处理长时任务、重试、部分失败，以及在分布式基础设施中维护一致的 agent 状态。

### AI & I by Every — "How a Professional Writer Writes With AI"
https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLuMcoKK9mKgHtW_o9h5sGO2vXrffKHwJL

Katie Parrott, staff writer at Every, sits down with Natalia Quintero (head of AI consulting) to discuss two years of using AI as both a productivity tool and a mental health support system.
Every 的驻站写作者 Katie Parrott 与 AI 咨询主管 Natalia Quintero 共同回顾了两年来将 AI 同时用于生产力工具和心理健康支持的历程。

Katie's journey began when she was laid off from a crypto firm and turned to ChatGPT as a career coach — not because she was an AI enthusiast, but because she couldn't afford a real coach.
Katie 的旅程始于她从一家加密公司被裁员，转而将 ChatGPT 当作职业教练——不是因为她热爱 AI，而是因为负担不起真正的教练费用。

Key insight from that experience: "Clarity doesn't arrive gift-wrapped from a digital assistant. It's something I had to dig out for myself, question by question and prompt by prompt."
那段经历的核心洞察："清晰感不会从数字助手那里包装精美地送上门来。它是我一个问题、一个提示词地自己挖掘出来的。"

She describes how she used AI to become a "content agency of one" — delivering 8 blog posts, 3 ebooks, 24 LinkedIn posts, 24 X posts, and 16 Instagram posts in a two-week window by front-loading context engineering.
她描述了如何借助 AI 成为"一人内容工厂"——通过前置上下文工程，在两周内完成 8 篇博客、3 本电子书、24 条 LinkedIn 帖子、24 条 X 帖子和 16 条 Instagram 帖子的交付。

The secret: detailed brand context documents (audience, pain points, voice, differentiators) given to the model upfront — "create guardrails and a fenced-in place for the model to play."

关于心理健康：Katie 患有双相情感障碍，她发现 AI 以变革性的方式降低了日常摩擦——从管理收件箱焦虑（用 Quora 筛选邮件），到通过 Codex 在拖延三年后终于预约了初级保健医生。
