### No Priors — "Why Traditional Benchmarks Fail Modern AI Models with OpenAI Research Scientist Noam Brown"

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AZrU6y3pUcU

The central argument of this episode is that the standard benchmark grid used to evaluate AI models is fundamentally broken in the era of large-scale test-time compute.
本期节目的核心论点是：在大规模测试时计算的时代，用于评估 AI 模型的标准 benchmark 对比表格从根本上已经失效。

Simply comparing a model's score on a benchmark without controlling for how much compute was used is misleading — it's like comparing runner speeds without noting one ran a sprint and the other ran a marathon.
在不控制计算量的情况下简单比较模型的 benchmark 分数会产生误导——就像比较跑步速度时，却不说明一个人跑的是百米冲刺，另一个跑的是马拉松。

**Key insights from Noam Brown (OpenAI Research Scientist):**

Modern models like GPT-5.5 are far more efficient than their predecessors at the same compute budget, but this doesn't show up in headline benchmark grids.
GPT-5.5 等现代模型在相同计算预算下效率远超前代，但这一点在主要 benchmark 对比表格中根本看不出来。

Models can now productively "think" for weeks before performance plateaus on certain benchmarks — meaning the plateau point is too far out to practically measure.
在某些 benchmark 上，模型现在可以高效"思考"数周才达到性能瓶颈——这意味着瓶颈点已经远到无法实际测量。

*"The capability of the model is a function of how much money you put into it, basically."*
*"模型的能力，基本上就是你投入多少钱的函数。"*

Safety evaluations (responsible scaling policies, preparedness frameworks) were designed in a pre-test-time-compute era and don't account for budget-dependent capabilities — this is an "inconvenient truth" the industry is largely ignoring.
安全评估（负责任的扩展政策、准备框架）是在测试时计算时代之前设计的，没有考虑到依赖预算的能力——这是整个行业在很大程度上选择忽视的"不便的真相"。

GPT-5.5 was used internally to disprove the Erdős unit distance conjecture — a significant mathematical result achieved at surprisingly low cost, suggesting untapped latent capability in current models.
GPT-5.5 在内部被用于推翻 Erdős 单位距离猜想——这是一项以出人意料低成本取得的重大数学成果，表明当前模型存在尚未开发的潜在能力。

Noam Brown does not believe we are headed toward an "overnight intelligence explosion" — because test-time compute scaling makes time itself the bottleneck.
Noam Brown 不认为我们正走向"一夜之间的智能爆炸"——因为测试时计算扩展使得时间本身成为了瓶颈。

**最值得关注的信号 / Most actionable signal:**

The right way to compare models is to plot performance as a function of compute budget (tokens, cost, or time) — not a single grid number.
比较模型的正确方式是将性能绘制为计算预算（token、成本或时间）的函数——而非单一的对比数字。

Until benchmark publishers adopt this standard, all model comparisons should be read with significant skepticism.
在 benchmark 发布者采用这一标准之前，所有模型比较都应保持高度怀疑。
