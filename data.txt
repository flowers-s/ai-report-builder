No Priors — Building an Autonomous Delivery Experience with DoorDash Co-Founders Andy Fang and Stanley Tang

DoorDash has quietly been a robotics company since 2018 — long before it was obvious autonomy would matter.
DoorDash 自 2018 年起便悄然成为一家机器人公司——早在自动驾驶技术的价值被广泛认可之前。

Co-founders Andy Fang and Stanley Tang explain how they concluded no one was building the right delivery robot: not the slow sidewalk robots (too slow for 3-5 mile deliveries), not the robotaxis (4,000-lb vehicles designed for people, not burritos).
联合创始人 Andy Fang 和 Stanley Tang 解释了他们为何得出结论：没有人在构建正确的配送机器人——缓慢的人行道机器人（速度太慢，无法完成 3-5 英里的配送）不行，Robotaxi（为运人而非送外卖设计的 4000 磅车辆）也不行。

So DoorDash built DoorDash DOT in-house: a 300-lb, 20 mph robot that navigates both sidewalks and bike lanes, now operating fully autonomously (L4) in Phoenix for over two years.
于是 DoorDash 自研了 DoorDash DOT：一个重 300 磅、时速 20 英里的机器人，可在人行道和自行车道上行驶，目前已在凤凰城以完全自动驾驶（L4）级别运营超过两年。

On agentic commerce, their "Ask DoorDash" AI feature is driving real behavior change: 50% of trajectories lead users to order from restaurants they've never tried before, and grocery basket sizes are 40% larger.
在 AI 代理商务方面，他们的「Ask DoorDash」AI 功能正在推动真实的用户行为变化：50% 的使用路径让用户从未尝试过的餐厅下单，杂货购物篮的金额也增加了 40%。

Stanley predicts that in ten years, DoorDash will have more Dashers doing deliveries — not fewer — because autonomous delivery will unlock so much new demand that the network will need every modality it can find.
Stanley 预测，十年后 DoorDash 将拥有更多而非更少的 Dasher——因为自动配送将释放大量新需求，整个网络将需要它能找到的每一种配送方式。

They also discuss their DashBench coding benchmark, AI spend growing 20× from January to June 2026, and the challenge of getting step-function improvements in non-coding functions like accounting and analytics.
他们还讨论了 DashBench 编程基准测试、AI 支出从 2026 年 1 月到 6 月增长了 20 倍，以及在会计和分析等非编程职能中获得阶跃式改进所面临的挑战。
