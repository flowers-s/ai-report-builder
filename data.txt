### Aaron Levie (Box CEO)

Aaron Levie articulated what he sees as the defining opportunity in enterprise AI: the massive gap between model capability and actual enterprise workflows.
Aaron Levie 阐述了他眼中企业级 AI 的核心机遇：模型能力与企业实际工作流之间巨大的鸿沟。

*"There's a massive chasm between the power of AI models and the ultimate workflows that enterprises are trying to automate. This gap is the opportunity for the applied AI layer to fill."*
*「AI 模型的能力与企业试图自动化的终极工作流之间，存在着巨大的鸿沟。这个差距，正是应用 AI 层的填补机会。」*

Bridging that gap requires connecting intelligence to workflows, re-engineering processes, aggregating context and data, enabling human-in-the-loop experiences, handling domain-specific evals, and managing security and governance.
弥合这一差距，需要将智能连接到工作流、重新设计流程、汇聚上下文与数据、支持人机协同体验、处理领域特定的评估，以及管理安全与治理。

His counterintuitive point: even as models keep improving at incredible rates, this applied AI layer doesn't become less necessary — it may actually become *more* important as greater capability enables tackling even more complex tasks.
他提出了一个反直觉的论点：即使模型以惊人的速度持续进步，这个应用 AI 层也不会变得不那么必要——随着能力增强使得更复杂的任务成为可能，它实际上可能变得更加重要。

### Training Data — Box's Aaron Levie: On Reinventing Yourself in the AI Age and Enterprise Diffusion

**核心论点**
AI diffusion in the enterprise is not primarily a technology problem — it's an organizational, process, and change management problem.
企业 AI 的扩散，本质上不是技术问题——而是组织、流程和变革管理问题。

Enterprise AI adoption requires bridging a gap that pure model improvements cannot close: you need the right data, workflows, governance, and human-in-the-loop systems to get value at scale.
企业 AI 落地需要填补一道纯模型进步无法弥合的鸿沟：要在规模上创造价值，你需要正确的数据、工作流、治理机制和人机协同系统。

**关键洞察**

- Being "wired in" to the right information feed (like Twitter/X) can put you a full year ahead of your peers — a compound advantage that's dramatically undervalued by most professionals.
  接入正确的信息流（如 Twitter/X）可以让你比同龄人整整领先一年——这种复利优势被大多数从业者严重低估。

- The "applied AI layer" is not a temporary workaround until models get smarter; as models become more capable, they unlock even more complex enterprise tasks, which *increases* the value of the surrounding orchestration layer.
  "应用 AI 层"并非等待模型更聪明的临时方案；随着模型能力增强，它们解锁了更复杂的企业任务，这反而*增加*了周边编排层的价值。

- Box has had to fundamentally rethink its product as an AI-first platform — not just adding AI features on top of existing workflows, but re-architecting around what becomes possible when intelligence is embedded everywhere.
  Box 不得不从根本上将自身重塑为 AI 优先平台——不是在现有工作流上叠加 AI 功能，而是围绕"智能无处不在时何为可能"重新设计架构。

- Founders who reinvent themselves for a new technology wave need to genuinely internalize the new paradigm, not just add it as a veneer — the competitive moat comes from deep, non-obvious application, not surface-level adoption.
  在新技术浪潮中完成自我重塑的创始人，需要真正内化新范式，而非只是表面涂一层——竞争护城河来自深层的、非显而易见的应用，而非浅层的跟风。

- Enterprise diffusion of AI is slower than the hype, but the ceiling is far higher — the organizations doing the hard integration work now will compound their advantage significantly over the next 3-5 years.
  AI 的企业扩散比炒作的速度慢，但上限远高于想象——现在做深度集成工作的组织，将在未来 3-5 年内显著积累复利优势。

**最值得关注的信号**
The companies building the "applied AI layer" — connecting raw model intelligence to actual enterprise workflows with proper governance, context, and change management — may end up capturing more long-term value than the model providers themselves.
正在构建"应用 AI 层"的公司——将原始模型智能与真实企业工作流对接，并配以完善的治理、上下文和变革管理——最终捕获的长期价值，可能超过模型提供商本身。
