The MAD Podcast with Matt Turck — Stripe's AI Chief: How AI Agents Will Buy, Sell, and Pay

Emily Beisel (Head of Data & AI at Stripe) joined Matt Turck to discuss the emerging infrastructure for agentic commerce.
Stripe 数据与 AI 负责人 Emily Beisel 与 Matt Turck 深入探讨了 AI agent 商业基础设施的最新进展。

**核心论点 / Core Thesis**

The economy is being restructured around AI agents as buyers and sellers.
经济正在围绕 AI agent 作为买家和卖家进行重构。

Stripe has spent the past year building real infrastructure — not hypotheticals — for agentic commerce, partnering with Google, OpenAI, Microsoft, and Meta.
Stripe 过去一年在真实基础设施上下注——与 Google、OpenAI、Microsoft、Meta 等合作，推动 agent 商业落地。

**关键洞察 / Key Insights**

• Agentic commerce exists on a spectrum — from fully autonomous agent-to-agent transactions (MPP, Level 3+) to AI-assisted human purchases with a "buy button" (Level 1). Today's consumer market is mostly at Level 2.
  Agent 商业是一个谱系——从全自主的 agent 对 agent 交易（Level 3+），到带"购买按钮"的 AI 辅助人类购物（Level 1）。当前消费市场大多处于 Level 2。

• The Agentic Commerce Protocol (ACP), co-created with OpenAI, is the "MCP for commerce" — it lets businesses expose their product catalog once and opt in to all agent surfaces (Google Gemini, ChatGPT, Copilot, etc.) simultaneously.
  与 OpenAI 联合创建的 Agentic Commerce Protocol（ACP），是"商业版 MCP"——企业只需发布一次产品目录，即可接入所有 agent 平台（Google Gemini、ChatGPT、Copilot 等）。

• Token theft is the most under-discussed threat in AI: *"More than one in six signups at AI companies are this kind of abuse."* Fraudsters don't need to steal money — they steal compute tokens.
  Token 盗窃是 AI 领域最被忽视的威胁：*"AI 公司超过六分之一的注册是此类滥用。"* 欺诈者不需要偷钱——他们偷的是计算 token。

• Per-seat pricing is breaking. AI usage is fundamentally consumption-based (token-based), not user-count-based, forcing the entire SaaS industry to rethink monetization.
  按席位计价的商业模式正在瓦解。AI 使用本质上是基于消耗（token 计费），而非用户数量，整个 SaaS 行业被迫重新思考货币化方式。

• The long-term vision: agents don't just execute transactions — they run entire businesses. Stripe is building toward a world where an agent is "tasked with running a business, and that includes buying some things and selling some things and making some profits."
  长期愿景：agent 不只是执行交易，而是运营整个业务。Stripe 正在构建这样一个世界：agent 被赋予经营业务的任务，"这包括购买一些东西、出售一些东西并实现盈利"。

**最值得关注的信号 / Forward-Looking Signal**

Watch token-based monetization and the ACP protocol adoption — these two will reshape SaaS pricing and B2B commerce infrastructure faster than most expect.
重点关注基于 token 的货币化模式和 ACP 协议的普及——这两者将以超出多数人预期的速度重塑 SaaS 定价和 B2B 商业基础设施。
