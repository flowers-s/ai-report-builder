### Training Data — "Andrej Karpathy: From Vibe Coding to Agentic Engineering"
https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLOhHNjZItNnMm5tdW61JpnyxeYH5NDDx8

Andrej Karpathy joined the Training Data podcast to talk about the transition from vibe coding to agentic engineering.
Andrej Karpathy 出席 Training Data 播客，讲述从"随兴编程"到 Agent 工程的演变。

He described December 2025 as the inflection point where AI coding agents stopped needing corrections and started producing coherent, large-scale work reliably.
他将 2025 年 12 月描述为关键转折点：AI 编程 agent 不再需要人工纠错，开始可靠地完成大规模任务。

*"I can't remember the last time I corrected it. And then I was bad coding."*
"我已经记不得上次纠正它是什么时候了。然后我自己反而变成了差劲的程序员。"

Karpathy introduced the "Software 3.0" paradigm: LLMs are programmable computers where prompting replaces coding, and the context window is your lever over the interpreter.
Karpathy 提出"Software 3.0"范式：LLM 是可编程的计算机，prompt 取代了代码，context window 就是你对 interpreter 的控制杆。

He contrasted "vibe coding" (raising the floor for everyone) with "agentic engineering" (preserving a professional quality bar while going faster).
他对比了"随兴编程"（拉高所有人的下限）与"agent 工程"（在保持专业质量标准的同时提速）。

*"10x is not the speed up you gain. People who are very good at this peak a lot more than 10x."*
"10 倍速不是你实际获得的提速幅度。真正擅长这件事的人，能达到远超 10 倍的峰值。"

On "jagged intelligence": AI models excel in verifiable domains (math, code) because labs use reinforcement learning with verification rewards — but remain surprisingly weak outside those circuits.
关于"锯齿形智能"：AI 在可验证领域（数学、代码）表现出色，因为实验室使用带验证奖励的强化学习训练，但在这些回路之外却意外地脆弱。

His key framing on what remains human: *"You can outsource your thinking, but you can't outsource your understanding."*
他对"人类价值何在"的核心判断："你可以外包你的思考，但你无法外包你的理解。"
