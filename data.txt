🎙️ PODCAST: Training Data — OpenAI's Noam Brown: Why Game Theory Didn't Lead to General Intelligence

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tRaFbDFBl_A
Published: 2026-11-24

## 一、深度总结

OpenAI researcher Noam Brown — the mind behind Libratus (poker) and CICERO (diplomacy) — sat down for a live conversation at the No Limit Finals to trace his intellectual journey from poker bots to general reasoning. His central thesis: game theory, despite being his life's work, turned out not to be the path to general intelligence.

OpenAI 研究员 Noam Brown——扑克 AI Libratus 和外交游戏 AI CICERO 的核心创造者——在 No Limit Finals 现场分享了他从扑克机器人到通用推理的思想历程。他的核心论点：博弈论，尽管是他毕生的研究领域，却并非通向通用智能的道路。

**From Poker to Diplomacy: The Limits of Rationality**
**从扑克到外交：理性假设的局限**

Brown started his PhD in 2012 working on poker AI — drawn to the game's perfect mathematical strategy that nobody knew but that could, in theory, yield unlimited money. He worked on it continuously through his PhD, eventually winning the MIT PokerBots competition in 2014 and 2015.

Brown 于 2012 年开始攻读博士，研究扑克 AI——这个游戏存在完美的数学策略，无人知晓，但理论上可以带来无限收益。他在整个博士阶段持续研究此课题，并于 2014 年和 2015 年连续赢得 MIT PokerBots 竞赛。

After poker, he targeted diplomacy — which in 2019 seemed like the hardest possible game for AI: natural language negotiations, cooperative alliances, deeply irrational human behavior. The game theory approach failed badly. When bots played GTO diplomacy, they would make "penny offers" in ultimatum game scenarios — offers any real human would immediately reject. The lesson: assuming rationality is a catastrophic mistake in real human interactions.

攻克扑克之后，他将目标转向外交游戏——2019 年这看起来是 AI 最难攻克的游戏：自然语言谈判、合作联盟、高度非理性的人类行为。博弈论方法彻底失败。当机器人用 GTO（博弈论最优）策略打外交时，会在最后通牒博弈中出价"一分钱"——任何真实人类都会立刻拒绝的报价。教训：在真实人际互动中，假设对方理性是灾难性的错误。

The solution was to train on massive human data — not to assume rationality, but to model how people actually behave — then optimize against that modeled behavior. This is what made CICERO superhuman at diplomacy.

解决方案是大规模训练人类数据——不假设理性，而是建模人类的真实行为方式——再针对这一行为模型进行优化。这正是 CICERO 在外交游戏中达到超人水平的关键。

**The Pivot to Reasoning**
**转向推理**

Around 2022, Brown faced a career crossroads. It was becoming clear that game theory wasn't the path — but that training on human data, modeling behavior, and scaling up reasoning via RL was. Despite game theory being his entire expertise, he pivoted.

2022 年前后，Brown 面临职业岔路口。博弈论显然不是通路，但在人类数据上训练、建模行为、通过强化学习扩展推理能力才是。尽管博弈论是他全部的专业积累，他还是选择了转型。

He joined OpenAI arguing that GPT-4 couldn't play Go, chess, or poker not because of transformer limitations, but because it needed reinforcement learning on chain-of-thought. His insight: in every game where AI beat top humans — chess, Go, poker, diplomacy — the AI needed to think before acting. Chain of thought + RL turned out to be the general solution.

他加入 OpenAI 时论证道：GPT-4 不会下围棋、下象棋或打扑克，不是因为 Transformer 的限制，而是因为它需要在思维链上进行强化学习。他的洞见：在每一个 AI 击败顶尖人类的游戏中——象棋、围棋、扑克、外交——AI 都需要在行动前先思考。思维链 + 强化学习，最终成为通用解法。

**The Centaur Era**
**半人马时代**

Brown is bullish on the "centaur" model — human + AI as a combined unit outperforming either alone. He uses poker bot building as his personal model evaluation benchmark: each new model he tests by asking it to build a poker bot. With 5.5/5.6, he felt models were 10-20x multiplying his ability — but couldn't quite do it alone. Astra is close to profitable online poker but still needs human judgment for a full-scale bot.

Brown 对"半人马"模式持乐观态度——人类 + AI 的组合单元优于任何一方单独行动。他把构建扑克机器人作为个人模型评估基准：每次测试新模型，都会让它构建一个扑克机器人。5.5/5.6 版本让他感到模型已将他的能力放大 10-20 倍——但还不能完全独立完成。Astra 已接近能在线上扑克中盈利，但构建完整机器人仍需要人类判断。

What's still missing: long-term planning and knowing when to step back. Models get overfocused on sub-problems (like perfecting CFR numerics that don't actually matter) and miss the big picture.

仍缺失的能力：长期规划，以及知道何时退后一步重新审视。模型容易过度专注于子问题（比如优化实际上无关紧要的 CFR 数值精度），而忽视大局。

**On RSI and the Speed of Progress**
**关于 RSI 与进步速度**

Brown views recursive self-improvement not as a novel concept but as the entire history of human technological progress — using technology to advance technology. He sees AI's jaggedness as actually lucky — it means humans are still necessary. But he expects very rapid progress over the next 1-2 years.

Brown 认为递归自我改进并非新概念，而是人类技术进步的全部历史——用技术推动技术。他认为 AI 的"参差不齐"实际上是幸运的——这意味着人类仍然是必要的。但他预计未来 1-2 年将出现非常快速的进展。

His most controversial take: for promising students, the best move right now might be to drop out and join an AI lab. He's seen several people skip their senior year of college and do exceptionally well.

他最具争议的观点：对于有潜力的学生，现在最好的选择可能是退学加入 AI 实验室。他见过几个跳过大四直接就业的年轻人表现出色。

## 二、金句摘录

> "The game theory approach doesn't actually work that well in diplomacy. If you assume rationality as a central pillar, you're going to make mistakes."
> "博弈论方法在外交游戏中效果很差。如果把理性假设作为核心支柱，你就会犯错。"

> "The real way to make a superhuman diplomacy AI is to train on human data — to understand people to such a degree that you don't assume rationality."
> "打造超人外交 AI 的真正方法，是训练人类数据——深入理解人类，而不是假设人类理性。"

> "The key unlock was: the longer the model takes to think before responding, the better it does."
> "关键突破是：模型在回答前思考的时间越长，表现越好。"

> "My most controversial take: if somebody is a promising student, the best thing to do is actually to drop out and join the labs now."
> "我最具争议的观点：对于有潜力的学生，现在最好的选择可能是退学、加入实验室。"
