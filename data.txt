Training Data — "From SEO to Agent-Led Growth: Profound's James Cadwallader"
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RyTwRCKeDo4

James Cadwallader, co-founder and CEO of Profound, argues we are witnessing the biggest platform shift in the history of marketing: from blue-link search to probabilistic AI responses.
Profound 联合创始人兼 CEO James Cadwallader 认为，我们正在经历营销史上最大的平台转变：从蓝色链接搜索到概率性 AI 响应。

The key insight: it's not just that the front door of the internet has changed — the *person* walking through the door has changed.
核心洞察：改变的不只是互联网的"入口"，而是走进这扇门的"人"变了。

An agent now browses on behalf of the consumer, synthesizes information from dozens or hundreds of pages (he cites 65 pages used to answer one showerhead query), and makes a large part of the buying decision.
如今是 Agent 代替消费者浏览，综合数十乃至数百个页面的信息（他举例：一个关于莲蓬头的问题被用来检索了 65 个网页），并做出购买决策的很大一部分。

The old SEO playbook no longer applies: you're now building content designed to be discovered and consumed entirely by agents, not humans.
旧的 SEO 打法不再适用：你现在构建的内容，是专为被 Agent 发现和消费而设计的，而非面向人类。

Each AI platform behaves like a different species: Gemini leans heavily on YouTube, ChatGPT pools from Reddit (consumer) or LinkedIn (B2B), and Claude has recently become more sensitive to real-time web information.
每个 AI 平台的行为方式各异，像不同物种：Gemini 大量依赖 YouTube，ChatGPT 在消费端从 Reddit 汲取，在 B2B 端依赖 LinkedIn，而 Claude 近期则对实时网络信息更加敏感。

Cadwallader raises the "dead internet" scenario: if humans stop clicking into websites, advertising revenue collapses, editorial incentives vanish, and AI has nothing fresh to train on.
Cadwallader 提出了"死亡互联网"的情景：如果人类不再点击进入网站，广告收入崩溃，内容创作动力消失，AI 也将无新鲜内容可用。

His prediction: AI labs will vertically integrate with social media networks to preserve a source of real-time human data — much like Grok and X already do.
他的预测：AI 实验室将与社交媒体平台垂直整合，以保留实时人类数据来源——正如 Grok 与 X 已经在做的那样。

On AI ads: generative advertising in conversational interfaces will be *"the most effective form of advertising the world has ever seen"* — because AI understands user intent at a granular level.
关于 AI 广告：对话界面中的生成式广告将成为"*the most effective form of advertising the world has ever seen*"——因为 AI 能细粒度理解用户意图。

The parting advice for marketers: you need visibility into how you show up across AI platforms (not just Google), and using agents to build marketing is no longer optional — it's like choosing not to use the internet.
给营销人员的最后建议：你需要了解自己在各 AI 平台上的曝光情况（不只是 Google），使用 Agent 进行营销已不再是可选项——就像拒绝使用互联网一样不可思议。
