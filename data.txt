### Anthropic Engineering: Scaling Managed Agents — Decoupling the brain from the hands
Anthropic Engineering: Scaling Managed Agents — Decoupling the brain from the hands
Anthropic 工程博客：扩展托管智能体——将"大脑"与"双手"解耦

The core insight: harnesses encode assumptions about what Claude can't do on its own — but those assumptions go stale as models improve.
核心洞察：执行框架（harness）编码了关于 Claude 无法独立完成哪些事情的假设——但随着模型不断进步，这些假设会逐渐过时。

The new architecture separates the "brain" (the reasoning model) from the "hands" (tool-use execution), enabling each layer to scale independently.
新架构将"大脑"（推理模型）与"双手"（工具执行）分离，让每一层都能独立扩展。

This decoupling dramatically improves reliability for long-running multi-step agentic workflows.
这种解耦大幅提升了长时间运行的多步骤智能体工作流的可靠性。

The post recommends designing agent harnesses that expect capability improvements, rather than freezing assumptions around current model limits.
文章建议设计智能体框架时应预期模型能力持续提升，而非将当前模型局限固化为假设。

🔗 https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/managed-agents

### Unsupervised Learning: AI Vibe Check — Lab Wars, Why APIs Might Vanish & Future Predictions
Unsupervised Learning: AI Vibe Check — Lab Wars, Why APIs Might Vanish & Future Predictions
《无监督学习》播客：AI 氛围大检测——实验室战争、API 为何可能消失，以及未来预测

**核心论点 / Core Thesis**

This episode is a wide-ranging "vibe check" on the current state of AI, featuring Jacob Efron with Ari (ex-DeepMind/Meta researcher, now AI startup founder) and Rob (Radical Ventures).
本期是对当前 AI 格局的全面"氛围检测"，主持人 Jacob Efron 与 Ari（前 DeepMind/Meta 研究员，现 AI 创业者）及 Rob（Radical Ventures）共同展开深入对话。

The central argument: we are entering a period of genuine lab-level competition, with capability jumps (like Fable) reshaping industry assumptions — and the most provocative claim is that AI labs may shut down their API businesses as compute constraints intensify.
核心论点：我们正在进入一个真正意义上的实验室级竞争时代，能力跃升（如 Fable）正在重塑行业假设——而最具挑战性的观点是：随着算力约束加剧，AI 实验室可能会关闭其 API 业务。

**关键洞察 / Key Insights**

The release of Fable generated unusually strong reactions even among AI insiders — it felt like a qualitative shift, not just an incremental improvement.
Fable 的发布在 AI 圈内部引发了异常强烈的反响——这感觉像是质变，而非简单的增量改进。

The panel discussed how close we are to RSI (Recursive Self-Improvement) — with real disagreement on timelines, but consensus that it's no longer a purely theoretical concern.
嘉宾们讨论了我们距离 RSI（递归自我改进）有多近——在时间线上存在真实分歧，但都认同这已不再是纯粹的理论问题。

The "APIs might vanish" thesis: as frontier models become increasingly expensive to run and demand skyrockets, labs may prioritize their own product surfaces over third-party API access — a major structural risk for the entire AI app ecosystem.
"API 可能消失"的论点：随着前沿模型运行成本急剧攀升、需求暴增，实验室可能会优先保障自身产品体验，而非第三方 API 访问——这对整个 AI 应用生态构成重大结构性风险。

Ari and Rob offered contrasting views on which labs will win the "lab wars" — with capability, distribution, and enterprise trust all cited as key differentiators.
Ari 和 Rob 就哪家实验室将赢得"实验室战争"提出了不同观点——能力、分发渠道和企业信任均被认为是关键差异化因素。

The most contrarian take: some participants believe the current "AGI discourse" is actively harmful, as it distracts from near-term product quality and safety work that matters more right now.
最具反传统的观点：部分嘉宾认为当前的"AGI 话语"实际上有害，因为它分散了对眼下更重要的近期产品质量和安全工作的注意力。
