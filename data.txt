**🎙️ PODCAST: No Priors — Redefining Chip Architecture with Arm CEO Rene Haas**
Source: https://www.youtube.com/@NoPriorsPodcast

In this episode of No Priors, hosts Sarah Guo and Elad Gil sit down with Rene Haas, CEO of Arm and SoftBank Group International, to explore how the chip industry is being reshaped by AI.
在这期 No Priors 播客中，主持人 Sarah Guo 和 Elad Gil 与 Arm 及软银国际 CEO Rene Haas 深度对话，探讨 AI 如何重塑芯片行业格局。

Haas explains Arm's dual role in the chip supply chain: licensing CPU IP to companies that then build chips on top (via TSMC and Samsung), while also now entering the product market with the Arm GI CPU announced at Hot Chips 2026.
Haas 解释了 Arm 在芯片供应链中的双重角色：一是向企业授权 CPU IP（由 TSMC 和三星代工），二是随着 2026 年 Hot Chips 大会上 Arm GI CPU 的发布，Arm 如今也亲自下场做产品。

Because Arm spans automotive, data centers, smartphones, and more, Haas says the company has a uniquely comprehensive view of the global supply chain — they "see everything."
由于 Arm 横跨汽车、数据中心、智能手机等多个领域，Haas 表示公司对全球供应链拥有独特的全局视角——他们"看得到一切"。

On AI in chip design: Haas is emphatic that AI has become indispensable. The biggest time sink in chip design is not the design itself, but verification, validation, and debug — and AI excels at this.
关于 AI 在芯片设计中的应用：Haas 强调 AI 已不可或缺。芯片设计中最耗时的环节不是设计本身，而是验证、确认和调试——而 AI 在这方面表现出色。

"If we were to shut it off, it's like being in the 1990s," he said. "The genie's out of the bottle and there's no stopping that."
"如果我们把它关掉，就像回到 1990 年代，"他说，"精灵已经出瓶，再也无法阻止。"

Looking ahead, Haas sees the shift from token generation to reasoning models as the key trend — these workloads are fundamentally different, requiring CPUs to orchestrate and arbitrate across heterogeneous compute.
展望未来，Haas 认为从 token 生成到推理模型的转变是关键趋势——这些工作负载有本质区别，需要 CPU 在异构计算资源之间进行调度和仲裁。

He also addressed the robotics opportunity, noting that Arm chips are already proliferating in robots, and that the humanoid robot market could ultimately dwarf even the smartphone wave.
他还谈到机器人领域的机遇，指出 Arm 芯片已大量应用于机器人，人形机器人市场的规模最终可能远超智能手机浪潮。
