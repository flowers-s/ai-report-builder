### No Priors — "Building an Autonomous Enterprise for Real-World Services with Netic Founder Melisa Tokmak"

**核心论点 (Core Thesis)**

Netic is building AI to run millions of real-world service businesses — HVAC, plumbing, pet care, hospitality, automotive — by sitting between the enterprise and its end customers.
Netic 正在构建 AI，为数百万家真实世界服务企业——暖通空调、管道、宠物护理、酒店、汽车等——提供运营支持，其定位是介于企业与终端客户之间。

The bet: that "essential services" — the businesses that keep daily life running — are massively underserved by technology and represent a huge AI opportunity.
这一赌注建立在这样的判断上：维持日常生活运转的"基本服务"行业严重缺乏技术支撑，代表着巨大的 AI 机遇。

**关键洞察 (Key Insights)**

- The real complexity isn't the AI model — it's understanding a customer's need, matching it to business rules, and then actually deploying the labor or service.
  真正的复杂性不在于 AI 模型本身，而在于理解客户需求、将其与业务规则匹配，并最终完成劳动力或服务的调度。

- Netic's agents handle inbound contact from any channel — phone, text, web — and manage the full workflow from intake to dispatch, embodying what "agentic enterprise" actually looks like in practice.
  Netic 的 Agent 处理来自任何渠道（电话、短信、网页）的入站联系，并管理从接收到派单的完整工作流程，体现了"Agentic 企业"在实践中的真实面貌。

- Founder Melisa Tokmak previously led engineering and go-to-market at Scale AI and has experience at Meta — bringing rare depth in both AI infrastructure and enterprise distribution.
  创始人 Melisa Tokmak 此前在 Scale AI 主导工程和市场推广，并有 Meta 经验——在 AI 基础设施和企业分发两方面均有罕见的深度。

- The target customer is large ($1B+ revenue) enterprises in essential services — companies interacting with millions of consumers but still largely running on manual processes or fragmented software.
  目标客户是基本服务领域的大型企业（年收入逾10亿美元），这些公司与数百万消费者互动，却大多仍依赖人工流程或碎片化软件运营。

**最值得关注的信号 (Forward-Looking Signal)**

The physical-world service economy is arguably the largest untouched frontier for AI agents — and Netic's model of "autonomous enterprise" may be the template for how AI will finally penetrate industries that software alone never could.
实体世界服务经济或许是 AI Agent 尚未触及的最大前沿领域——而 Netic 的"自主企业"模式，可能正是 AI 最终渗透那些纯软件始终无法攻克的行业的蓝图。
