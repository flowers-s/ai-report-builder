Training Data — Google's AI Infrastructure Chief, Amin Vahdat, on the Physics & Economics of Frontier AI

This episode features Amin Vahdat, Google's head of AI infrastructure, in a wide-ranging conversation about the biggest CapEx buildout in human history.
本期节目邀请到 Google AI 基础设施负责人 Amin Vahdat，深入探讨人类历史上规模最大的资本支出建设浪潮。

Google alone is expected to spend over $200 billion on CapEx this year, mostly on data centers.
仅 Google 今年的资本支出预计就将超过 2000 亿美元，大部分用于数据中心建设。

**核心论点 / Core Thesis:**

The physics and economics of frontier AI are fundamentally reshaping how data centers, chips, and networks are designed — with "goodput per watt" (not FLOPS) as the true north star metric.
前沿 AI 的物理学和经济学正在从根本上重塑数据中心、芯片和网络的设计方式——"有效吞吐量/瓦特"（而非 FLOPS）才是真正的北极星指标。

**Key Insights / 关键洞察:**

Power is the single most fundamental long-term constraint in AI infrastructure — all other bottlenecks seem solvable, but power does not yet have a clear solution at scale.
电力是 AI 基础设施中最根本的长期瓶颈——其他所有瓶颈似乎都有解决路径，但电力在规模上尚无明确方案。

Google measures infrastructure success by "goodput" — the actual useful work delivered to real workloads under real failure conditions — not theoretical peak FLOPS.
Google 用"有效吞吐量"来衡量基础设施成效——即在真实故障条件下对实际工作负载交付的真正有用工作量——而非理论峰值 FLOPS。

At 100,000 accelerator scale, hardware failures happen multiple times per hour; the ability to detect, isolate, and recover in near real-time is as important as raw performance.
在 10 万加速器规模下，硬件故障每小时发生多次；近实时检测、隔离和恢复的能力与原始性能同等重要。

The TPU program — contrarian in 2013 — succeeded because a small set of fundamental primitives (large matrix multiply, sparse core, remote load/store) has proven durable across many generations of model architectures, including the transformer revolution.
TPU 计划在 2013 年是一个逆势之举，其成功在于一小组基础原语（大型矩阵乘法、稀疏核、远程加载/存储）跨越多代模型架构（包括 Transformer 革命）被证明是持久有效的。

The rise of long-horizon agents is fundamentally changing data center design: agents remove the human "rate limiter" in the loop, driving interaction time from seconds down to milliseconds and massively increasing demand for CPU, networking, and storage alongside GPU/TPU compute.
长时 Agent 的兴起正在从根本上改变数据中心设计：Agent 消除了循环中人类"速率限制器"，将交互时间从秒级压缩到毫秒级，并大幅增加了对 CPU、网络和存储的需求——与 GPU/TPU 并驾齐驱。

Co-design across chip, network, software, and model (with DeepMind working literally shoulder-to-shoulder in the same building) enables multiplicative optimization that would be impossible across company boundaries.
芯片、网络、软件和模型的协同设计（DeepMind 团队字面意义上在同一栋楼并肩工作）实现了跨公司边界不可能达到的乘法效应优化。

**Most Forward-Looking Signal / 最值得关注的信号:**

By 2036, a single rack could consume multiple megawatts — plug in water, power, and fiber, and you're running a frontier supercomputer. Space-based deployment is also a serious consideration.
到 2036 年，单个机架可能消耗数兆瓦——接上水、电和光纤，你就拥有了一台前沿超级计算机。基于太空的部署也是一个认真考虑的方向。
