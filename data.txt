### Unsupervised Learning — Ep 90: AI Pioneer Jürgen Schmidhuber on the State of AI Today

In this episode, Jürgen Schmidhuber — widely credited as the father of modern AI and the inventor of LSTM — shares his unfiltered views on where the AI field actually stands.
在本期节目中，被誉为现代 AI 之父、LSTM 发明者的 Jürgen Schmidhuber 毫无保留地分享了他对 AI 领域现状的真实看法。

Schmidhuber argues that true AGI requires physical hardware comparable to human bodies, and we are nowhere near that despite progress in screen-based AI.
Schmidhuber 认为，真正的 AGI 需要可与人类身体媲美的物理硬件，尽管屏幕端 AI 进展迅速，但我们距此目标还差得远。

*"You can't have AGI without hardware like that. You can't have AGI just behind the screen."*
*"没有那样的硬件，你就无法实现 AGI。AGI 不可能只存在于屏幕之后。"*

He is highly optimistic about AI technology in the long run, but deeply pessimistic about AI model companies as businesses — predicting the current CapEx boom is massively overdone.
他对 AI 技术的长期前景持高度乐观态度，但对 AI 模型公司作为商业实体则深感悲观——他预测当前的资本开支热潮严重过头。

He also notably takes a far less alarmed view of AI safety than many in the field, pushing back on existential risk narratives.
他对 AI 安全问题的担忧程度也明显低于该领域许多人，对存在性风险叙事持保留态度。
