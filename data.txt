**🎙️ PODCAST: Inside Nemotron & NVIDIA's AI Lab | Bryan Catanzaro**
*The MAD Podcast with Matt Turck*

Bryan Catanzaro, who leads NVIDIA's Nemotron project, sat down with Matt Turck for a wide-ranging conversation on open-source AI, NVIDIA's frontier model efforts, and the architecture of Nemotron 3 Ultra.
NVIDIA Nemotron 项目负责人 Bryan Catanzaro 与 Matt Turck 进行了一次深度对话，内容涵盖开源 AI、NVIDIA 的前沿模型工作以及 Nemotron 3 Ultra 的架构设计。

Nemotron has two jobs: helping NVIDIA understand how to build better chips (by training frontier models), and supporting the open-source AI ecosystem so it remains viable.
Nemotron 肩负两项使命：一是通过训练前沿模型帮助 NVIDIA 理解如何构建更好的芯片；二是支持开源 AI 生态系统，使其保持活力。

Nemotron 3 Ultra was pretrained using 4-bit arithmetic (MXFP4) — a technically challenging feat that reduces memory and energy costs, and makes inference dramatically cheaper at scale.
Nemotron 3 Ultra 采用 4 位算术（MXFP4）进行预训练——这是一项技术上颇具挑战性的成就，可降低内存和能耗，并在大规模推理时显著降低成本。

The architecture is a hybrid of Transformers and state space models (SSMs) — NVIDIA research showed that mostly-SSM with some full attention actually produces a smarter model, not just a faster one.
其架构融合了 Transformer 和状态空间模型（SSM）——NVIDIA 研究表明，以 SSM 为主、辅以部分全注意力的架构实际上能产生更智能的模型，而不仅仅是更快的模型。

Mixture of Experts (MoE) is now the default for frontier AI, and NVIDIA's Blackwell hardware (NVL72) was specifically designed around MoE's dynamic token routing requirements.
混合专家（MoE）现已成为前沿 AI 的默认架构，NVIDIA 的 Blackwell 硬件（NVL72）正是专为 MoE 动态 token 路由需求而设计的。

Multi-token prediction can deliver up to 3-4x inference speedups with zero accuracy loss — and uniquely, the more accurate the model, the faster the inference.
多 token 预测可以在零精度损失的前提下实现高达 3-4 倍的推理加速——独特之处在于，模型越精准，推理速度越快。

Multi-teacher distillation (MOPD) allowed ~10-15 specialized "teacher" models to train one unified "student" model — with the bonus that it helps large teams (500+ people) collaborate without competing priorities.
多教师蒸馏（MOPD）使约 10-15 个专业化"教师"模型能够共同训练出一个统一的"学生"模型——额外好处是帮助大型团队（500人以上）在没有优先级竞争的情况下协同工作。

On safety: Bryan offered a somewhat unconventional take — arguing that open-source AI is inherently safer because more "sunlight" (diverse perspectives and scrutiny) is better than a small group controlling safety for everyone.
关于安全性：Bryan 提出了一个略显非主流的观点——他认为开源 AI 本质上更安全，因为更多的"阳光"（多元视角和审查）胜过由少数人为所有人把控安全。
