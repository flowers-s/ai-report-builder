The MAD Podcast with Matt Turck
Episode: "OpenAI's Compute Chief: We Can't Build Fast Enough | Sachin Katti"
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wEZBlmvxx4o

## 深度总结 / Deep Summary

**核心论点 / Core Argument**

OpenAI's head of industrial compute, Sachin Katti, argues that AI infrastructure is undergoing the largest buildout in human history — bigger than the railroad or highway era.
OpenAI 工业计算负责人 Sachin Katti 认为，AI 基础设施正在经历人类历史上最大规模的建设——规模超过铁路和高速公路时代。

The fundamental constraint is not talent or models, but physical compute supply, which the physical world simply cannot build fast enough to match AI's explosive demand.
根本瓶颈不是人才或模型，而是物理算力供应——现实世界的建设速度根本跟不上 AI 爆炸式增长的需求。

**关键洞察 / Key Insights**

- Data centers are "giant factories turning electrons into tokens" — liquid-cooled supercomputers the size of football fields, where cooling is required everywhere including cables and transformers.
  数据中心是"将电子转化为 token 的巨型工厂"——足球场大小的液冷超级计算机，冷却系统覆盖电缆和变压器在内的每个角落。

- OpenAI is not just consuming grid power — they actively invest in new generation infrastructure (solar, gas, hydro) and transmission so they don't take power away from existing communities; a side benefit is that America's grid infrastructure is being rapidly modernized.
  OpenAI 不只是消耗电网电力——他们主动投资新的发电基础设施（太阳能、天然气、水电）和输电线路，确保不从现有社区抢夺电力；附带效益是美国电网基础设施正在被快速现代化。

- OpenAI's custom chip "Jalapeno" is optimized for one metric: maximum tokens per watt — co-designed with the exact workload and model it will serve, offering a decisive efficiency advantage over general-purpose chips.
  OpenAI 自研芯片"Jalapeno"针对一个核心指标优化：每瓦 token 产出最大化——与其将服务的精确工作负载和模型协同设计，相比通用芯片具有决定性的效率优势。

- Inference is now arguably the majority of compute usage, even during "training" — because synthetic data generation, post-training, and test-time compute are all inference.
  推理现在可以说已占计算用量的大头，即使在"训练"阶段也是如此——因为合成数据生成、后训练和测试时计算全都是推理。

- AI doing AI research is a compounding multiplier: the number of experiments that can be run is no longer limited by the number of human researchers (a scarce resource), but by compute — which means research acceleration is itself accelerating.
  AI 做 AI 研究是一个复利乘数：可运行的实验数量不再受人类研究者数量（稀缺资源）限制，而是受算力限制——这意味着研究加速本身也在加速。

**最值得关注的信号 / Most Important Signal**

The world of "recursive AI" — where AI designs the systems it needs to train the next generation of AI — is "not that far."
"递归 AI"的世界——AI 设计它用来训练下一代 AI 所需的系统——"已经不远了"。

Every time OpenAI has thought they had enough compute and slowed down, it turned out to be a mistake.
每次 OpenAI 认为算力已经充足而放慢脚步，事后都证明是错误的。

The surprise is always on the downside: never enough compute, never enough fast enough.
意外总是来自同一个方向：算力永远不够，建设速度永远不够快。
