### Swyx (swyx on X) — AI Engineer & @latentspacepod

**On AI benchmark integrity and RLM generalization:**
A notable trajectory comparison writeup is buried in the RLM paper from researchers at late interaction.
一篇值得关注的轨迹对比分析，深藏在来自 late interaction 研究者的 RLM 论文中。

An open secret of "frontier" model training is that even without training on test sets directly, you can basically cheat by training on test lookalikes, enabling you to goalseek almost any benchmark number you want.
"前沿"模型训练有个公开的秘密：即使不直接在测试集上训练，也可以通过训练测试集的"仿制品"来作弊，几乎可以把任何 benchmark 数字调到想要的水平。

However, when models are released as open weights, 99% of the time you don't get the datasets/RL environments that would easily show if someone was training on benchmark lookalikes, so there is plausible deniability.
然而，当模型以开放权重发布时，99% 的情况下你拿不到训练数据集或 RL 环境，无法轻易判断是否有人在 benchmark 仿制品上训练，因此存在"合理推诿"空间。

The researchers apply standard NLP distance metrics on hidden trajectories — it supports the finding that RLMs can generalize to unseen tasks that share latent structure with training.
研究者将标准 NLP 距离度量应用于隐藏轨迹，支持了 RLM 能够泛化到与训练数据具有潜在结构相似性的未见任务这一发现。

**On multi-agent review patterns:**
Use one agent to do the work and another to review it against a rubric.
用一个 agent 完成任务，再用另一个 agent 按照评分标准进行审查。

For subjective tasks like "Is this a good video short?" there's no deterministic answer — a separate verification agent reading a rubric and giving feedback helps overcome "self-preferential bias," where a model is more lenient when reviewing its own output.
对于"这个短视频好不好？"这类主观任务，没有确定性答案——让一个独立的验证 agent 阅读评分标准并给出反馈，有助于克服"自我偏好偏差"，即模型在审查自己输出时会更宽松的倾向。

### Aaron Levie (levie on X) — CEO @Box

**On multi-model agentic systems as the future:**
Multi-model agentic systems are clearly the future.
多模型 agentic 系统显然是未来的方向。

Cursor's new research shows that using a frontier model as a planner/orchestrator with a cheaper workhorse model can result in a 15X cost improvement on large coding projects.
Cursor 的最新研究表明，将前沿模型用作规划者/编排者，配合更廉价的执行模型，在大型编码项目上可实现 15 倍的成本改善。

The key insight: "Few moments in a large task genuinely require frontier intelligence — such as the original decomposition, the design decisions, and certain trade-offs. Once a frontier planner has collapsed the ambiguity into a detailed, explicit instruction, less expensive models simply have to follow it."
核心洞察："在大型任务中，真正需要前沿智能的时刻很少——比如最初的任务分解、设计决策和某些权衡取舍。一旦前沿规划者将模糊性转化为详细、明确的指令，更廉价的模型只需执行即可。"

This is increasingly becoming the core design pattern of complex agents — routing to different model tiers based on task complexity.
这正在成为复杂 agent 的核心设计模式——根据任务复杂度路由到不同层级的模型。

Companies that master this in coding, finance, legal, healthcare, and life sciences will be well-positioned to take on larger workloads that would otherwise be too expensive.
在编码、金融、法律、医疗和生命科学等领域掌握这一模式的公司，将具备承接更大规模工作负载的优势，而这些工作负载若用单一前沿模型处理则成本过高。
