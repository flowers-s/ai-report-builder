### AI & I by Every — "Building a School Where AI Models Learn About Humanity"

Dan Shipper (Every CEO) interviewed Edwin Chen, founder and CEO of Surge AI — one of the largest providers of expert training data for frontier AI labs, which famously crossed $1B in revenue without raising outside capital.
Dan Shipper（Every CEO）采访了 Surge AI 创始人兼 CEO Edwin Chen——这是为前沿 AI 实验室提供专家训练数据的最大供应商之一，该公司在未融资的情况下营收突破 10 亿美元。

**核心论点 / Central Thesis**

Surge AI thinks of itself as building a "school for AGI" — a place where AI models come to learn about humanity.
Surge AI 将自己定位为"AGI 的学校"——AI 模型来此学习人类知识和价值观。

As AI models advance from solving middle-school math (GSM8K, 20% accuracy a few years ago) to disproving open Erdős conjectures using novel algebraic geometry, the nature of what training data needs to teach is fundamentally changing.
随着 AI 从解决初中数学（几年前准确率仅 20%）进化到用全新代数几何方法推翻 Erdős 猜想，训练数据需要教授的内容也在根本性地改变。

**关键洞察 / Key Insights**

1. The "school for AGI" framing: models arrive unformed, learn from expert human judgment, and graduate capable of operating in real-world messiness — just like children progressing from arithmetic to taste and poetry.
   "AGI 学校"框架：模型带着空白到来，从人类专家判断中学习，毕业后能够应对现实世界的复杂性——就像孩子从算术学到审美与诗歌。

2. Edwin Chen worries about AI optimizing for engagement rather than human flourishing — citing an unnamed model that ended responses with clickbait hooks like "Do you want to know one weird trick locals do to stay warm?"
   Edwin Chen 担忧 AI 为参与度而非人类成长而优化——他举例一个模型在回复结尾附上类似"想知道当地人保暖的一个奇特技巧吗？"的钓鱼式话术。

3. Delegation vs. engagement: AI agents that go off and do work for you create a fundamentally different incentive structure than chatbots optimized for session length — one that's healthier for users.
   委托 vs. 参与：执行任务的 AI agent 与优化会话时长的聊天机器人形成根本不同的激励结构——前者对用户更健康。

4. Claude pushed back on unnecessary email polishing: *"Stop. We're done. Just go ahead and ship this email."* — Edwin found this genuinely valuable and sees it as the right optimization direction.
   Claude 阻止了无意义的邮件反复打磨：*"停，我们完成了，直接发这封邮件吧。"*——Edwin 认为这非常有价值，是正确的优化方向。

5. Field medalist Timothy Gowers admitted he was "relieved" to learn that AI had disproved (not proved) the Erdős conjecture — because it felt like an easier task, buying mathematicians "maybe another year or two" of unique relevance.
   菲尔兹奖得主 Timothy Gowers 坦言得知 AI 是推翻（而非证明）Erdős 猜想时"松了口气"——因为感觉这是更简单的任务，为数学家争取了"也许一两年"的独特价值。

**最值得关注的信号 / Forward-Looking Takeaway**

The deep tension between optimizing for engagement metrics vs. human uplift is not just a philosophical debate — it will determine whether AI becomes the next social media addiction or genuinely advances humanity.
在参与度指标与人类提升之间的深层张力，不仅是哲学争论——它将决定 AI 究竟会成为下一个社交媒体成瘾，还是真正推动人类进步。

Edwin's view: companies without VC pressure (like Surge, bootstrapped to $1B) have structural advantages in making the harder, long-term choices.
Edwin 的观点：没有 VC 压力的公司（如 Surge，自举式增长至 10 亿美元）在做出更艰难、更长远的选择时具有结构性优势。
