**No Priors — Biohub: The Future of Biology is Open-Source**
*with Mark Zuckerberg, Priscilla Chan, and Alex Rives (Head of Science)*

**核心论点 / Core Argument**

Chan Zuckerberg Biohub is betting that the future of biological AI requires coupling a frontier AI lab with a frontier biology effort — because unlike language models, the critical training data for understanding life simply doesn't exist yet and must be invented.
Chan Zuckerberg Biohub 押注于：生物 AI 的未来需要将一个前沿 AI 实验室与一个前沿生物学研究机构深度融合——因为与语言模型不同，理解生命所需的关键训练数据尚不存在，必须从零创造。

**关键洞察 / Key Insights**

The open-source thesis is strategic, not altruistic: Biohub will have bigger impact by getting tools into more scientists' hands faster through open-source than by trying to build a proprietary moat.
开源策略是战略性的，而非纯粹利他：通过开源将工具更快地交付给更多科学家，比构建专有护城河具有更大影响力。

Science has a structural knowledge-sharing problem: research gets locked up in silos, tools live and die with individual postdocs, and there's no shared infrastructure to accelerate collective progress.
科学界存在结构性的知识共享问题：研究成果被锁在各自的"竖井"中，工具随着博士后个体的离开而消失，缺乏加速集体进步的共享基础设施。

Biohub folded over 1.1 billion proteins and predicted their structures — by building a general protein understanding model rather than a narrow antibody or target-specific model.
Biohub 折叠并预测了超过 11 亿种蛋白质的结构——通过构建通用蛋白质理解模型而非针对特定抗体或靶点的专用模型实现。

The goal is not to cure disease directly — it's to build the tools and data infrastructure that accelerates the whole scientific field so that scientists can cure disease.
目标并非直接治愈疾病——而是构建加速整个科学领域发展的工具和数据基础设施，从而让科学家能够治愈疾病。

The virtual biology initiative goes beyond proteins: the ambition is to eventually model cells and whole biological systems — a "world model" for life itself.
虚拟生物学计划的野心超越了蛋白质：最终目标是对细胞乃至完整生物系统进行建模——为生命本身构建"世界模型"。

**最值得关注的信号 / Forward-Looking Signal**

Biohub's model represents a template for what frontier science-AI collaboration looks like at scale: not a startup, not a university, not a pure philanthropy — but a novel hybrid that funds the data generation work that no one else will do because the business model doesn't exist yet.
Biohub 的模式代表了前沿科学与 AI 大规模协作的新范本：既非创业公司、也非大学、更非单纯的慈善机构——而是一种新型混合体，专门资助那些因商业模式尚未成型而无人愿意承担的数据生成工作。
