### AI & I by Every — "How Sam Altman Uses Dots to Take Back His Time"

This episode features a conversation between Dan Shipper (CEO of Every) and Sam Altman (CEO of OpenAI), recorded live at OpenAI's Dev Day.
本期节目收录了 Every CEO Dan Shipper 与 OpenAI CEO Sam Altman 在 OpenAI Dev Day 上的现场对话。

Sam Altman shared how OpenAI's new "Dots" (AI agents) feature has given him back creative mornings by filtering urgent issues overnight.
Sam Altman 分享了 OpenAI 全新的「Dots」（AI Agent）功能如何帮助他找回了富有创造力的早晨——通过在夜间自动过滤紧急问题。

*"The first thing that has given me back control of my mornings and my mental space in the mornings."*
「这是第一个让我重新掌控早晨和心理空间的东西。」

He revealed that his Dot (named "Dottie") alerted a team member about a failing live demo five minutes before it happened, having read the presentation and detected the issue proactively.
他透露，他的 Dot（名为"Dottie"）在一次现场演示出错前五分钟提醒了团队成员，Dot 已读取演示文稿并主动发现了问题。

Sam also discussed the distinction between an "Industrial Revolution" vs. a "Renaissance" model for AI's impact on society, arguing strongly for the Renaissance framing — where humans remain at the center, with AI amplifying individual creative potential.
Sam 还讨论了 AI 对社会影响的「工业革命」与「文艺复兴」两种模式的区别，强烈主张采用文艺复兴框架——人类始终处于中心，AI 放大个体的创造潜力。

*"I get sad to hear people talk about, oh, AI is going to automate everything... We're going to be like house cats and the AI will decide how to reward us."*
「听到有人说 AI 将自动化一切……我们会像家猫一样，由 AI 决定如何奖励我们，我感到很悲伤。」

他还评论了 Decisions API、「Space」（OpenAI 协作文档平台）、ChatGPT 原生应用扩展，以及降低 AI 速度成本（不仅仅是智能成本）的重要性。
