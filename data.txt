OFFICIAL BLOGS

Claude Blog — Claude Cowork and chat are now one Claude
https://claude.com/blog/cowork-is-now-claude
Published: Sep 16, 2026

Starting today, Claude Cowork and chat are merging into one Claude.
即日起，Claude Cowork 与普通对话正式合并为统一的 Claude。

Bring a quick question, or hand over a report due at noon, and Claude takes it from there, even after you've closed your laptop.
无论是一个简单问题，还是一份正午截止的报告，Claude 都会从容接手，即使你已经合上了电脑。

It's rolling out on Pro and Max plans over the next few weeks, with more plans to follow.
这项功能将在未来几周内向 Pro 和 Max 用户推出，更多方案也将陆续跟进。

Claude Docs and Claude Slides are new today, and Claude Design now works inside your conversations too.
Claude Docs 和 Claude Slides 今天正式上线，Claude Design 也已集成至对话界面中。

Ask for a document, and you and Claude write it together.
只需提出需求，你与 Claude 便可共同完成一份文档。

Ask for a presentation, and Claude drafts the slides — you can edit directly, present straight from Claude, or download as PowerPoint or PDF.
请求一份演示文稿，Claude 会自动生成幻灯片，你可以直接编辑、在 Claude 中直接演示，或导出为 PowerPoint / PDF。

All three are in beta on paid plans.
三项功能目前均处于付费方案的 beta 阶段。

People used both Cowork and Design, and told us the frustrating part was deciding where a task belonged.
用户同时使用 Cowork 和 Design，但反映最令人头疼的是不知道该把任务放在哪里。

So we stopped making you choose — Claude can now figure out what a task needs.
于是我们取消了这个选择的负担，Claude 现在会自动判断任务所需的处理方式。
