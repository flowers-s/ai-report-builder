This episode is a rare behind-the-scenes look at how Modal — now a $1B+ ARR company — actually works at the infrastructure layer, and where its CEO thinks the inference market is heading.
本期播客难得地呈现了 Modal——一家年经常性收入已超 10 亿美元的公司——在基础设施层面的真实运作逻辑，以及其 CEO 对 AI 推理市场走向的深度判断。

**核心论点 / Core Thesis**

The GPU market is still the Wild West — deals get done via late-night WhatsApp groups and chance encounters at NVIDIA conferences.
GPU 市场依然是"狂野西部"——交易通过深夜 WhatsApp 群和 NVIDIA 大会上的偶遇促成。

Modal's edge isn't just inference speed; it's the full platform — sandboxes, training, fine-tuning, batch processing — because customers don't want single-trick ponies.
Modal 的核心优势不只是推理速度，而是完整的平台能力——沙箱、训练、微调、批处理——因为客户不想要只会一招的工具。

**关键洞察 / Key Insights**

The Sandbox bet in 2023 was asymmetric: low cost to build, massive upside if coding agents took off.
2023 年的沙箱赌注是非对称的：构建成本低，如果编程 agent 爆发则回报巨大。

Inference is becoming a race to the bottom on price; the durable differentiation is being a multi-product platform.
推理服务正在经历价格战；持久的差异化在于成为多产品平台。

Deprecation is harder in the agent era — agents are trained on old code and will keep using deprecated APIs.
在 agent 时代，废弃旧功能变得更难——agent 是在旧代码上训练的，会持续调用已废弃的 API。

Senior engineers are adopting coding agents faster than juniors — because agents give them "10 interns" to finally execute on all their ideas.
资深工程师比初级工程师更快采用编程 agent——因为 agent 给了他们"10 个实习生"来落地脑海中积压多年的想法。

"Agent SEO" is the new SEO: Modal now tracks whether AI coding assistants recommend Modal unprompted, and actively optimizes for it.
「Agent SEO」是新的 SEO：Modal 现在追踪 AI 编程助手是否会主动推荐 Modal，并积极优化这一指标。

**最值得关注的信号 / Most Forward-Looking Signal**

Modal is building a "CDN for inference" — dynamically routing workloads across chip types and global regions to minimize latency for voice-to-voice and real-time video use cases.
Modal 正在构建"推理领域的 CDN"——在不同芯片类型和全球区域之间动态路由工作负载，以最小化语音对话和实时视频场景的延迟。
