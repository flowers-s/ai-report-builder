### No Priors — "Why Diffusion Will Win AI Inference with Inception Co-Founder and CEO Stefano Ermon"

**核心论点 / Core Thesis**

Stefano Ermon, a Stanford professor and one of the founding researchers of diffusion models, argues that diffusion-based architectures will ultimately dominate AI inference — not just image generation — because of their fundamental advantages in speed and efficiency.
Stefano Ermon 是斯坦福大学教授，也是扩散模型的奠基研究者之一，他主张扩散架构最终将主导 AI 推理——不仅仅是图像生成——因为其在速度和效率上具有本质性优势。

His company, Inception, is built around this thesis: challenge the large labs not by scaling bigger, but by being faster and cheaper.
他创立的公司 Inception 正是围绕这一论点构建：不靠规模更大来挑战大型实验室，而是靠更快、更便宜。

**关键洞察 / Key Insights**

- Ermon was working on generative models at Stanford as early as 2014, when the field was so unfashionable that papers were hard to publish — he had to justify training generative models as a way to learn features for supervised tasks.
  Ermon 早在 2014 年就在斯坦福从事生成模型研究，那时这个领域不受重视，论文难以发表，他不得不将生成模型的训练包装为监督任务的特征学习工具来获得认可。

- Diffusion models are not just for images — they can be applied to any generative task, and their iterative refinement process creates a natural efficiency-accuracy tradeoff that autoregressive models lack.
  扩散模型不仅适用于图像——它们可应用于任何生成任务，其迭代细化过程创造了自回归模型所缺乏的效率与精度之间的天然权衡空间。

- The "AI is too expensive/slow" zeitgeist is creating massive market opportunity for inference-optimized players; speed and cost are becoming the primary competitive vectors.
  "AI 太贵/太慢"的时代情绪正在为专注推理优化的玩家创造巨大的市场机会；速度和成本正成为主要的竞争维度。

- Inception's bet is that the hardware-software co-optimization frontier is where the real leverage lies — not in just having more GPUs.
  Inception 的赌注在于：硬件-软件协同优化的前沿才是真正的杠杆所在，而不仅仅是拥有更多 GPU。

**最值得关注的信号 / Key Forward-Looking Signal**

The inference efficiency race is intensifying: as open models close the quality gap with closed models (as Vercel's token data also shows today), the winning factor shifts to who can serve them fastest and cheapest at scale.
推理效率竞赛正在加剧：随着开源模型在质量上逐渐缩小与闭源模型的差距（Vercel 今日的 token 数据也印证了这一点），胜负的关键将转向谁能以最快速度、最低成本大规模提供服务。
