=== The MAD Podcast with Matt Turck — "OpenAI's Yann Dubois: Why AI Progress Suddenly Feels Real" ===

In this episode, Matt Turck sits down with Yann Dubois, who co-leads the post-training Frontiers team at OpenAI and helped build GPT-5.5, o3, and GPT-5 Thinking.
本期节目中，Matt Turck 与 OpenAI 后训练前沿团队联席负责人 Yann Dubois 深度对谈，后者参与构建了 GPT-5.5、o3 和 GPT-5 Thinking。

Dubois explains that AI progress feels like a "step function" not because capability gains are sudden, but because there is a reliability threshold you must cross before models become truly useful — and OpenAI crossed it around December 2025.
Dubois 解释道，AI进展之所以感觉像"阶跃函数"，并非因为能力提升是突然发生的，而是因为必须越过一个可靠性门槛模型才能真正有用——OpenAI 大约在2025年12月越过了这一门槛。

A second accelerant: once models are good enough, they start helping build the tools researchers need, compounding internal velocity.
第二个加速因素：一旦模型足够强大，它们开始协助构建研究人员所需的工具，从而形成内部速度的复利效应。

The third factor is the shift from "verifiable reward" RL (math competitions, coding contests) to real-world RL — where the lessons from structured benchmarks are now being applied to messy, open-ended use cases.
第三个因素是强化学习从"可验证奖励"（数学竞赛、编程比赛）向真实世界场景的转变——结构化基准上积累的经验现在被应用于复杂、开放式的实际用例。

*"We moved from competitions to usefulness to users — and that's what we are feeling right now."*
*"We moved from competitions to usefulness to users — and that's what we are feeling right now."*

He also highlights that continual learning remains one of the biggest unsolved problems in AI, three years after ChatGPT launched.
他还强调，在ChatGPT发布三年后，持续学习依然是AI领域最重要的未解问题之一。
