### The MAD Podcast with Matt Turck — OpenAI's Compute Chief: We Can't Build Fast Enough | Sachin Katti

Matt Turck interviews Sachin Katti, Head of Industrial Compute at OpenAI, on the largest infrastructure buildout in human history.
Matt Turck 采访了 OpenAI 工业计算负责人 Sachin Katti，深入探讨人类历史上最大规模的基础设施建设。

The central thesis: demand for compute at OpenAI has always outstripped supply, every time the team thought they had enough and slowed down, they regretted it.
核心论点：OpenAI 对算力的需求始终超过供给，每当团队认为算力已足够而放缓脚步，事后都会后悔。

*"Anytime you have thought you have enough compute, we can slow down. Always negatively surprises like, oh, we should not have slowed down."*
"每当你认为算力够用、可以放慢节奏，结果总是反向打脸：哦不，我们不该踩刹车的。"

OpenAI is spending roughly $50 billion on compute in 2026, against an estimated $700 billion industry-wide.
OpenAI 2026 年在算力上的支出约为 500 亿美元，而整个行业的估算支出约为 7000 亿美元。

The data centers being built are not traditional cloud data centers — they are giant liquid-cooled supercomputers, effectively "factories turning electrons into tokens."
正在兴建的数据中心并非传统云数据中心，而是大型液冷超级计算机，本质上是"将电子转化为 token 的工厂"。

On power: OpenAI is now investing in grid infrastructure itself — building new generation capacity, transmission lines, and transformer substations wherever it builds data centers, with the side benefit of modernizing America's grid.
在电力方面：OpenAI 现在开始直接投资电网基础设施，在每个数据中心建设地点兴建新的发电、输电和变压站设施，附带效益是推动美国电网现代化。

On Jalapeno (OpenAI's custom chip): the chip was designed in just 9 months — a record pace — enabled by OpenAI's unique advantage of knowing the exact workload (its own models) it needs to run, plus AI itself assisting in chip design.
关于 Jalapeno（OpenAI 自研芯片）：该芯片仅用 9 个月完成设计，创历史最快纪录——得益于 OpenAI 了解自身精确工作负载（即自己的模型）的独特优势，以及 AI 辅助芯片设计。

*"We do believe that the world of recursion is not that far where AI will design the systems it needs to train and run the next generation of AI."*
"我们确实相信，AI 自行设计用于训练和运行下一代 AI 所需系统的递归时代，已经不远了。"

On MRC (the new networking protocol): it's a multipath packet-spraying protocol designed to gracefully mask failures across 100,000-GPU clusters, ensuring training runs never stall due to link or hardware failures.
关于 MRC（新网络协议）：这是一种多路径分包喷洒协议，专为在十万 GPU 规模集群中优雅屏蔽故障而设计，确保训练任务不因链路或硬件故障而中断。

Biggest surprise for Sachin: the bottlenecks are not where people expect — they are in the physical world: gas turbines, transformers, qualified electricians and plumbers, permitting.
Sachin 最出乎意料的发现：瓶颈并不在人们预期的地方，而在物理世界：燃气轮机、变压器、合格的电工和管道工、审批许可。

On guaranteed capacity (tokens as a utility): OpenAI now offers enterprises guaranteed token allocations, essentially treating intelligence as a critical supply input that enterprises need to secure, just like electricity or bandwidth.
关于保障容量（token 作为公用事业）：OpenAI 现在向企业提供 token 配额保障服务，实质上将"智能"视为企业需要像电力或带宽一样提前锁定的关键供应要素。
