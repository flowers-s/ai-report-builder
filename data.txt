### Training Data — Memory and Continual Learning: Engram's Dan Biderman and Jessy Lin

This episode dives deep into one of the most fundamental challenges in AI: how do models learn, retain, and generalize knowledge over time?
本期节目深入探讨了 AI 中最根本的挑战之一：模型如何随时间学习、保留和泛化知识？

The guests, Dan Biderman and Jessy Lin from Engram, explore what makes pre-training and post-training enable models to generalize in "magical emergent ways," and how to control that process so companies can incorporate private data effectively.
嘉宾 Dan Biderman 和 Jessy Lin 来自 Engram，他们探讨了是什么使预训练和后训练能够让模型以"神奇的涌现方式"进行泛化，以及如何控制这一过程，使企业能够有效融入私有数据。

The conversation touches on continual learning — teaching models to keep learning new things without forgetting what they already know, a key challenge for enterprise AI deployments.
对话涉及持续学习——教会模型在不遗忘已知内容的情况下持续学习新事物，这是企业 AI 部署的关键挑战。
