Thibault Sottiaux (@thsottiaux) — Codex & ChatGPT @ OpenAI

To calibrate you all on which reasoning effort to use for Astra, know that GPT-6 Astra on low performs better than GPT-5.6 Sol on high.
GPT-6 Astra（低推理强度）的表现已超过 GPT-5.6 Sol（高推理强度），这意味着新模型在效率上实现了质的飞跃。

If you were using high reasoning efforts with Sol and were happy, I suggest you move down to low or medium for Astra.
Thibault 建议此前使用 Sol 高推理强度的用户，切换到 Astra 时可直接降至低或中等强度，既省成本又能获得更好结果。

Peter Yang (@petergyang) — AI tutorials & interviews

"Cheating and explaining the answer [to your child] are closer than you'd think."
Peter 分享了与 Brilliant 联创 Sue Khim 的最新播客对话，探讨 AI 时代如何让孩子真正学会思考，而不是依赖 AI 跳过思考过程。

"Using AI to skip learning is like bringing a robotic arm to the gym to lift weights for you."
Sue 用一个犀利比喻点出核心：用 AI 代替学习，就像带机械臂去健身房——结果是什么都没练到。

"Learning isn't about the answer. It is about strengthening that part of you that can concentrate and struggle, that's unafraid of trying new problems."
学习的本质不在于得到答案，而在于磨砺专注力与面对难题的勇气——这也是 Brilliant 产品设计的第一原则："永远不要直接告诉学习者答案"。
