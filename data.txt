Thibault Sottiaux (@thsottiaux)

Thibault is genuinely excited about Dots, noting that within just 2–3 days of use, after teaching it his preferences, it learns remarkably fast and can tackle surprisingly ambitious tasks autonomously.
Thibault 对 Dots 充满期待，他表示仅用 2-3 天、教它了解自己的偏好之后，它的学习速度便令人印象深刻，并且能够独立承担相当有野心的任务。

他预计数百万个 Dots 将在数日内上线，团队正在观察用户如何使用各自的主 Dot，然后再开放创建 Dot 团队的能力。

Nikunj Kothari (@nikunj)

Nikunj shared two sharp, honest observations about the current market: first, it's harder than ever to find missionaries — founders are jumping ship to "obvious winners," and mercenary behavior is rampant even among people who don't think of themselves that way.
Nikunj 分享了两个关于当前市场的犀利坦诚观察：其一，如今比以往任何时候都更难找到"传教士型"创始人——创始人们纷纷跳槽去"显而易见的赢家"公司，雇佣军心态泛滥，即使很多人自己都没意识到。
Second, defensibility is no longer one big moat — it's a thousand small things done well and made invisible, and in this hyper-competitive landscape, execution speed and adaptability have become the real moat for any founder.
其二，护城河不再是某一件大事，而是千件小事做到极致、融于无形；在这个极度竞争的环境下，执行速度和适应能力才是每位创始人真正的护城河。
