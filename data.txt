### No Priors: "Why Diffusion Will Win AI Inference" with Stefano Ermon (Inception)

Stefano Ermon — Stanford professor turned CEO of Inception — joined No Priors to make the case that diffusion models are the future of AI inference. No Priors 播客邀请了 Stefano Ermon——斯坦福教授、Inception 联合创始人兼 CEO——来阐述为什么扩散模型将主导 AI 推理的未来。

He traces his journey back to 2014 when generative models were a niche research area, and explains why he always believed that building a generative model is the right way to understand structure in data. 他回顾了自己从 2014 年起研究生成模型的历程——那时这还是冷门方向，连发论文都要靠"无监督特征学习"来包装；但他始终相信，生成模型才是真正理解数据结构的正确路径。

The core thesis of the episode: speed and efficiency will define the next wave of AI, and diffusion architectures have fundamental advantages over autoregressive transformers for inference throughput. 本期核心论点：速度与效率将定义 AI 的下一轮竞争，而扩散架构在推理吞吐量上相比自回归 Transformer 具有根本性优势。He also draws an analogy between generative modeling and compression — *"whenever you train these models, you're essentially building a compression scheme"* — positioning Inception as a challenger to the large labs on the inference frontier. 他还将生成建模类比为数据压缩，将 Inception 定位为在推理赛道上挑战大型实验室的有力竞争者。

Source: https://www.youtube.com/@NoPriorsPodcast
