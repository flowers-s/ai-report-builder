### Aaron Levie — Box CEO (levie on X)

Aaron Levie made a sharp observation that applies to all agents, not just coding agents: the biggest challenge in enterprise AI agent strategies is giving agents the right constrained context for each task.
Aaron Levie 提出了一个适用于所有代理（而不仅仅是编程代理）的深刻洞察：企业 AI 代理策略中最大的挑战，是为每项任务提供恰当且受约束的上下文。

Too much information or conflicting sources causes agents to draw incorrect conclusions; too little information limits their upside entirely.
信息过多或来源冲突会导致代理得出错误结论；信息过少则从根本上限制了代理的价值上限。

*"A lot of challenges with AI strategies are actually data strategy challenges in disguise"* — the real premium is on getting structured and unstructured data environments set up properly so agents can work effectively.
*"许多 AI 战略挑战实际上是伪装成数据战略挑战"*——真正的溢价在于将结构化与非结构化数据环境正确搭建，使代理能够有效工作。

This creates a clear advantage for startups that can be architected for agent-readiness from day one.
这为那些从第一天就能以代理就绪架构设计的初创公司创造了明显优势。
