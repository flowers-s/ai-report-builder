## Aaron Levie (@levie)

Aaron Levie made a bold declaration: he's fully "forward deployed engineering pilled," arguing AI fundamentally differs from traditional software.
Aaron Levie 发表了一个大胆宣言：他完全支持"前沿部署工程"模式，认为 AI 与传统软件有着根本性的不同。

With software, you ship once and customers adopt it. With AI, the underlying models keep evolving — workflows change with every model upgrade, best practices shift constantly.
软件可以一次交付，用户就能采用。但 AI 不同——底层模型持续演进，每次模型升级都可能改变工作流，最佳实践也在不断变化。

His conclusion: one vendor sharing cross-company best practices at scale is more efficient than every company figuring it out alone, and as we move from chat to agentic systems, the FDE (forward deployed engineering) model becomes a *core competency* for AI deployment at scale.
他的结论是：一家供应商跨公司大规模分享最佳实践，比每家公司单独摸索效率更高；随着我们从聊天系统迈向智能体系统，FDE（前沿部署工程）模式将成为大规模 AI 部署的*核心能力*。

He also flagged a two-word thesis: *"Headless software is the future."*
他还提出了一个简洁的论断："无界面软件是未来。"

## Dan Shipper (@danshipper)

Dan Shipper published a deep-dive on Every's attempt to launch an agent-as-a-service platform built on OpenClaw, sharing two hard-won lessons.
Dan Shipper 发布了 Every 尝试在 OpenClaw 上构建智能体即服务平台的深度复盘，分享了两条来之不易的经验教训。

First: OpenClaw is powerful but *extremely* hard to build on as a platform — it moves fast, regressions are frequent, and being the middleware layer between OpenClaw and end users is painful.
第一：OpenClaw 功能强大，但作为平台*极难*在其上构建——它迭代迅速，回归问题频繁，作为 OpenClaw 与最终用户之间的中间层非常痛苦。

Second: one excellent company-wide super-agent beats 1-on-1 agents for every employee — because maintaining agents requires real effort, and people with day jobs don't want to spend time debugging AI internals.
第二：一个出色的公司级超级智能体，胜过为每位员工单独配置的智能体——因为维护智能体需要真正的投入，而有本职工作的人不想花时间调试 AI 内部机制。

He also nodded to the future: *"Codex-native apps are the future."*
他还展望了未来："以 Codex 为原生的应用是未来。"

**第86集：Yann LeCun 谈离开 Meta、打破 LLM 范式，以及为何 Hinton 是错的**

This episode features Yann LeCun — one of the founding fathers of deep learning — in a wide-ranging conversation about the fundamental limits of large language models and the future of AI architecture.

LeCun 认为，LLM 并非通向人类水平智能的正确路径，这一领域需要从下一个 token 预测的范式，根本性地转向基于世界模型的架构。

He also directly challenges Geoffrey Hinton's views on AI risk and consciousness, offering a contrarian take that is sure to spark debate.
他还直接挑战了 Geoffrey Hinton 关于 AI 风险和意识的观点，提出了一个必将引发争议的反主流立场。
