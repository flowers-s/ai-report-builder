### No Priors — The Rise of the Full-Stack Builder and Hyper-Leveraged Generalist with Microsoft CEO Satya Nadella

The world is demanding that tech companies deliver tangible benefits, not just promises.
这个世界要求科技公司兑现实实在在的价值，而不是空头支票。

Microsoft CEO Satya Nadella sat down with the No Priors and Lenny's Podcast hosts at Microsoft Build 2026 for a wide-ranging conversation on AI strategy, the future of engineering roles, enterprise software, and the societal responsibility of the tech industry.
微软 CEO Satya Nadella 在 Microsoft Build 2026 上与 No Priors 和 Lenny's Podcast 的主持人进行了深度对话，涵盖 AI 战略、工程师角色的未来、企业软件及科技行业的社会责任。

**Core thesis: The era of the hyper-leveraged generalist has arrived.**
**核心论点：超级杠杆型通才时代已经来临。**

Nadella argues that AI's biggest unlock is not replacing specialists, but giving generalists unprecedented leverage.
Nadella 认为，AI 最大的解锁不是取代专才，而是给通才提供前所未有的杠杆。

A CEO who once only wrote Word documents can now build apps — in the same sentence.
一个过去只写 Word 文档的 CEO，如今可以直接构建应用——这件事发生在同一句话里。

**Key insights:**
**关键洞察：**

The real competitive moat is your private eval, not your model.
真正的竞争护城河是你的私有评估集，而不是你用的模型。

Every company that can train a specialist model on proprietary traces — and hill-climb it using private evals that can't be leaked — will compound in value in ways that were impossible before.
每家公司只要能用私有数据训练专有模型，并通过无法泄露的私有评估集持续迭代，就能以之前无法想象的方式实现复利增长。

Microsoft is reconceptualizing itself as a "harness and evals company."
微软正在将自己重新定位为"评估与 harness 公司"。

The platform play is: give every company the tooling to achieve frontier intelligence with their own data, their own tools, and their own private evals — and make switching between models painless.
其平台战略是：让每家公司都能用自己的数据、工具和私有评估集实现前沿智能，并让模型切换毫无障碍。

The Azure networking team's transformation is the template for enterprise AI adoption.
Azure 网络团队的转型，是企业 AI 落地的最佳模板。

Instead of doing Azure networking, they built the agentic system that does Azure networking.
他们不再亲自做 Azure 网络运维，而是构建了负责 Azure 网络运维的 Agent 系统。

Their ask changed from "we need headcount" to "we need tokens."
他们的诉求从"我们需要招人"变成了"我们需要 token 额度"。

SaaS is not dead — it's being unbundled and rebundled.
SaaS 没有死，它正在被解绑和重新打包。

The data model and business logic underneath legacy SaaS are actually valuable; what's changing is how they're packaged and delivered.
遗留 SaaS 底层的数据模型和业务逻辑实际上很有价值；正在变化的是它们的打包和交付方式。

Outcome-based pricing sounds great until you actually have an outcome.
结果定价听起来很美，直到你真的产生了结果。

*"Most people love outcomes until they have an outcome. Because once you have an outcome, it's like giving away royalty."*
"大多数人在获得结果之前都喜欢结果定价——但一旦真的产生了结果，那感觉就像在分红。"

Education is the next frontier — and maybe the next big startup opportunity.
教育是下一个前沿领域，也许也是下一个重大创业机会。

The way people learn, acquire credentials, and find economic opportunity has fundamentally changed; someone who builds a new university or pedagogy for this era could build a generational company.
人们学习、获取资质、寻找经济机会的方式已经根本性地改变；在这个时代构建新型大学或教学法的人，有机会打造一家具有时代意义的公司。

**Most forward-looking signal:**
**最值得关注的信号：**

Nadella predicts that in six months, knowledge workers will wake up to discover that AI autopilots — running overnight with delegated authority — completed significant work on their behalf.
Nadella 预测，六个月后，知识工作者醒来将发现，在被授权的 AI 自动驾驶系统在夜间已代为完成了大量工作。

The new bottleneck won't be doing the work — it will be reviewing and directing it.
新的瓶颈不再是做工作，而是审查和指导工作。
