## Madhu Guru (@realmadhuguru)
*Sr Director, AI @ Meta (prev Google — Gemini, Veo, Nano Banana)*

Madhu shared a sharp strategic insight: AI product playbooks have a ~3-month half-life. Traditional product teams are built to discover a playbook and milk it for 5 years; AI labs consciously throw away playbooks every few months. His advice — focus on meta-principles for continuous market learning and extreme urgency in execution. Build teams optimized for *inventing*, not playbook-milking.

Madhu 分享了一个犀利的战略洞察：AI产品的打法"半衰期"约为3个月。传统产品团队习惯发现一套打法后躺赚5年；而AI实验室则每隔几个月就主动淘汰旧打法。他的建议是：聚焦于持续学习市场动向和极速执行的元原则，打造一支擅长"发明"而非"收割"的团队。

*"AI product building has a ~3-month playbook half-life... Legacy teams are optimized for playbook milking. AI teams need to be optimized for inventing."*

*CEO @ Vercel*

Guillermo laid out a compelling vision for the web's future: it's bifurcating into ultra-fancy human experiences (powered by Three.js, TypeGPU, etc.) and agent-centric pure-signal APIs (markdown + MCP). Guillermo 勾勒了一幅关于 Web 未来的清晰图景：Web 正在向两个极端分化——一端是为人类打造的极致体验（Three.js、TypeGPU 驱动），另一端是面向智能体的纯信号API（Markdown + MCP）。他认为，中间的"功能型"Web将被智能体实时生成的UI所取代，"智能体就是新浏览器"。他还分享了 `mcp-handler` npm 下载量的爆炸性增长数据，并推介 Vercel 的 "eve" 框架——让你在一个 Git 仓库中完整拥有自己的智能体堆栈。

## 🎙️ Podcast: No Priors — "Rethinking Legacy Data Infrastructure"
*Eon Co-Founders Ofir Ehrlich and Gonen Stein*

This episode of No Priors tackles a critical but underappreciated topic: legacy data infrastructure in the AI era. Eon's co-founders discuss how nonhuman actors (AI agents) are now creating a new class of security and compliance threats — agents with legitimate access permissions inside organizations, operating at extreme velocity. They explore the challenges non-technical employees face as "builders" creating agents that handle sensitive data, often without understanding the security and compliance implications.

Key takeaways: (1) The threat model has shifted from human to non-human actors; (2) The detection methodology is similar, but velocity has exploded; (3) Organizations urgently need data infrastructure that was designed for the AI era, not retrofitted from legacy cloud/backup systems. Google's acquisition of Spirit Airlines' data out of bankruptcy was cited as a signal of how valuable legacy data assets have become.

本期 No Priors 聚焦一个重要但被低估的议题：AI时代的遗留数据基础设施。Eon 联合创始人探讨了非人类行为者（AI智能体）如何带来新型安全与合规威胁——这些智能体拥有组织内部的合法访问权限，但运行速度极快、边界模糊。他们深入分析了非技术员工成为"构建者"时所面临的挑战：这些人创建的智能体在处理敏感数据时，往往对安全合规意味着什么毫无概念。

核心洞见：(1) 威胁模型已从人类行为者转向非人类行为者；(2) 检测方法类似，但攻击速度大幅提升；(3) 组织迫切需要为AI时代原生设计的数据基础设施，而非从遗留的云备份系统勉强改造而来。Google 在破产程序中收购 Spirit Airlines 数据资产的案例被引用，揭示了遗留数据资产的巨大价值。

🔗 https://www.youtube.com/@NoPriorsPodcast
