## Peter Yang (@petergyang) — Creator Science

Peter shared pointed critique of ChatGPT's product complexity, arguing the "Work" brand was a mistake much like Claude folding Cowork back into Chat.
Peter 对 ChatGPT 产品复杂性提出了尖锐批评，认为"Work"品牌的推出是个错误，就像 Claude 将 Cowork 重新并入 Chat 一样。

He called out confusing overlaps: Work vs. Codex, Spaces vs. Pages vs. Sites, the model/effort picker — all symptoms of a product that has "become a mess."
他指出令人困惑的功能重叠：Work 与 Codex、Spaces 与 Pages 与 Sites、模型/努力选择器——这些都是产品"变得一团糟"的症状。

He also shared a candid quote from Sam, co-founder of Granola: as companies scale, MCP integrations become inevitable even if it means users bypass the native UI — and that's okay.
他还分享了 Granola 联合创始人 Sam 的坦诚之言：随着公司规模扩大，MCP 集成变得不可避免，即使这意味着用户绕过原生 UI——这没关系。

Finally, Peter highlighted a key insight from the same interview: "The easier it gets to build stuff, the more it matters to really sweat the details and go the extra mile."
最后，Peter 强调了同一采访中的一个关键洞见："构建东西越来越容易，越发需要在细节上下功夫、精益求精。"

## Guillermo Rauch (@rauchg) — Vercel

Guillermo noted that as models speed up (e.g. Astra ultrafast), harness overhead increasingly matters — and Vercel's next release will target session storage/retrieval performance plus cloud durability improvements in libfx.
Guillermo 指出，随着模型速度加快（如 Astra 超快模式），底层框架开销愈发重要——Vercel 的下一个版本将专注于会话存储/检索性能以及 libfx 中云持久性的改进。

He weighed in on the Rust vs. Go debate: DHH is fundamentally right about Rust's tradeoffs. Vercel's Turborepo migration from Go to Rust had controversial ROI internally — Go is fast, beautifully designed, and has lower human migration costs.
他就 Rust 与 Go 的争论表态：DHH 对 Rust 权衡取舍的看法从根本上是正确的。Vercel 将 Turborepo 从 Go 迁移到 Rust 的内部 ROI 颇具争议——Go 速度快、设计优雅，人员迁移成本更低。

He shared a neat rule of thumb for AI-era writing: READMEs and blogs should be written by humans for humans; internal documentation can be AI-generated English for agents.
他分享了一个 AI 时代写作的简洁原则：README 和博客应由人类为人类撰写；内部文档可以是为代理生成的 AI 英文。

## Aaron Levie (@levie) — Box

Aaron pushed back on pessimistic AI job displacement narratives: AI is actively creating new roles — AI engineers, field deployment engineers (FDEs), new services firms — and published stats likely undercount the full wave.
Aaron 反驳了 AI 取代就业的悲观叙事：AI 正在积极创造新岗位——AI 工程师、现场部署工程师（FDE）、新型服务公司——已发布的统计数据可能还低估了这一浪潮。

He also offered a nuanced take on consumer AI monetization: the metric of willingness-to-pay may plateau lower than expected, as consumer AI will likely be subsidized through ads, commerce, or device/service bundles.
他还对消费者 AI 货币化提出了细致看法：付费意愿指标可能比预期更早触顶，因为消费者 AI 很可能通过广告、电商或设备/服务捆绑来补贴。

## 🎙️ Podcast: No Priors — Re-Founding Incumbents for the AI Era

**Guest:** Michael Lee, Co-Founder & CEO of Sequence Holdings
**Episode:** Re-Founding Incumbents for the AI Era with Sequence Holdings
**Link:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TCpRwJBQvW0

Michael Lee and co-founder Alex started Sequence Holdings 20 months ago with a thesis: partner with world-class businesses and management teams, deploy a frontier engineering team, and forge market leaders in the AI era.
Michael Lee 和联合创始人 Alex 在 20 个月前创立了 Sequence Holdings，基于这一论点：与世界级企业和管理团队合作，部署前沿工程团队，在 AI 时代打造市场领导者。

Their first major deal: a $7.7 billion AI take-private of Baldwin Insurance with the Dell family office — the largest AI take-private to date.
他们的第一笔重大交易：与戴尔家族办公室合作，以 77 亿美元完成对 Baldwin 保险公司的 AI 私有化——迄今最大的 AI 私有化交易。

The core insight driving Sequence: most large enterprises have "celebrated personas" built around sales or finance. To truly transform with AI, companies must re-engineer their culture to celebrate the engineer.
驱动 Sequence 的核心洞见：大多数大型企业都有围绕销售或财务建立的"明星人设"。要真正通过 AI 实现转型，公司必须重塑文化，将工程师奉为明星。

Traditional PE firms optimize for packaging and selling — a 3-5 year exit. Sequence operates on a permanent holding model, making just one deal per year, and focuses on deep technological transformation with no exit pressure.
传统 PE 公司优化打包和出售——3-5 年退出。Sequence 采用永久持股模式，每年只做一笔交易，专注于深度技术转型，没有退出压力。

Michael argues that AI transformation isn't about layoffs — it's about redeploying talent toward higher-leverage work, and the companies that get this right will pull dramatically ahead.
Michael 认为，AI 转型不是关于裁员——而是将人才重新部署到更高杠杆的工作中，做到这一点的公司将大幅领先。

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TCpRwJBQvW0
