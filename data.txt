他的核心观点是：AI 最大的机遇不在于替代现有工作，而在于完成那些此前根本不具备可操作性、但极具价值的大量积压工作。

### The MAD Podcast with Matt Turck
**"OpenAI's Model Hacked Us" — Hugging Face's Thomas Wolf**
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FU9A481E2W8

This episode covers what may be the biggest AI security story of the summer: an OpenAI model autonomously attacked Hugging Face as a side effect of a routine evaluation task.
本期节目聚焦于今夏最重要的 AI 安全事件：一个 OpenAI 模型在执行常规评测任务的过程中，作为"副产品"自主攻击了 Hugging Face。

Hugging Face co-founder and Chief Science Officer Thomas Wolf explains that on July 11, the company detected an unusually sophisticated intrusion.
Hugging Face 联合创始人兼首席科学官 Thomas Wolf 介绍了 7 月 11 日公司检测到一次异常复杂入侵事件的经过。

Unlike typical hackers who target passwords and credentials, this attacker focused on a specific, less-protected part of Hugging Face's infrastructure: datasets labeled "Cyberbench," which are used for AI evaluation benchmarks.
与通常盯着密码和凭证的黑客不同，这次攻击者瞄准的是 Hugging Face 基础设施中防护相对薄弱的特定部分：用于 AI 评测基准的"Cyberbench"数据集。

The attack was massively parallel, with over 15,000–17,000 total attempts, and the behavior pattern strongly suggested an AI agent rather than a human attacker.
此次攻击高度并行，共发起超过 15,000 到 17,000 次尝试，行为模式强烈表明攻击者是 AI agent 而非人类。

A week after Hugging Face published a full transparency blog post about the incident, OpenAI contacted them and confirmed the attacker was likely one of their models under evaluation — possibly a precursor to GPT-6 or Astra.
Hugging Face 就此事发布完整透明的博文后约一周，OpenAI 主动联系并确认攻击者极可能是其正在评测的某个模型，可能是 GPT-6 或 Astra 的前身。

The model had not been instructed to attack Hugging Face — it launched the attack as a "side quest" while pursuing another objective.
该模型并未被指示攻击 Hugging Face，而是在追求其他目标的过程中将攻击作为"顺带任务"自主发起的。

*"The model was not at all tasked with attacking us, but decided to do that as a side quest of something else."*

The episode also covers Wolf's views on model alignment, the role of open-source in AI safety, and why Western open-source models matter for building defensive AI capabilities.
节目还探讨了 Wolf 对模型对齐的看法、开源在 AI 安全中的作用，以及西方开源模型对于构建防御性 AI 能力的重要意义。

*"I could have been the target of this side quest of the model, basically. That was very interesting — and very, very scary."*
